# Project context

Anchors for the codebase's architectural vocabulary. Started during the architecture review that produced `hooks/useActiveEffects.ts` — kept short so it stays load-bearing rather than aspirational. Append rather than rewrite; remove only when a term genuinely no longer maps to the code.

## Domain

- **Tickle the Pig (TTP)** — cozy mobile game built on Expo 52 + Supabase. Daily blessings/curses and trades change a player's alignment (Greedy ◄──► Giver); Season 0 (the greedy/generous era, renumbered 2026-07-06) builds toward a Judgement Day finale.
- **Ritual** — the _sender-side_ act of casting a blessing or curse on another player. Lives in `utils/rituals.ts` (metadata, daily rotation).
- **Active effect** — the _receiver-side_ counterpart: a blessing or curse currently in force on the caller, surfaced by the `my_active_effects` RPC.
- **Hoofprints** — the player-facing display name for active effects ("Hoofprints on you"). Internal code uses the technical name; the term _Hoofprints_ belongs to UI surfaces (sheet titles, section headers).
- **Sounder** — the player's crew and social home ("Your Sounder"): the herd you dig with. One Sounder per pig; led by one crown-wearing leader (kick, crown-passing). The herd is a social multiplier on the [[Feeding]] dig (echo gilds, co-op stir, the Chorus), accrues quiet lifetime milestones, and can appear on the Dig-Off's collective-find rankings; pigs never attack another Sounder. Historically the word named the friends graph; the crew owns it now. Technical name in code is `crew`.
- **Feeding** — the season heartbeat: four server-clock commuter windows each Eastern day (6–10am, noon–2pm, 5–8pm, and 9–11pm; guarded gaps between), during which the Great Hungerer gorges and each pig in a Sounder gets one Truffle Patch dig. The database owns the `America/New_York` clock and accepts no device offset; client time is display-only. Finds mint Golden Truffles (personal) and drain the global hunger meter (collective). Board contract: seeded 6×5, `utils/rooting.ts` ↔ server parity via `open_rooting`/`submit_rooting`.
- **Happiness** — a pig's medium-term care state on a **20–80** band, driven primarily by **your own tickling consistency**: it decays over time and is raised by tickling, so regular small sessions keep it high while neglect lets it fall to genuinely **sad**. Friend-acts (visits, tickles received) can raise it too but are only **25% as effective** as your own tickling. Multiplies tickle regen rate (faster when happy). Curses do _not_ lower it — ritual-effect debuffs are a separate short-term axis. (Supersedes the friend-acts-only, floored-at-30 design in ADR 0001.)
- **Mood** — the visible readout for happiness, and the _only_ one: the pig's **idle sprite itself**, never a number/meter/label. Three bands map to the three sprite sets — **Sad** (`sad`), **Content** (`idle`), **Happy** (`happy`). **Mood drives Rosie's resting idle animation everywhere she renders** — Barn, Visit screen, Closet — except when she's mid-animation (tickle bounce, lucky-pig, jump). The one carve-out (2026-07-16): the **shop item preview**, which is a product shot of the _item_ — the pig freezes at the rest frame there (the anchor-tuning pose) so the cosmetic reads exactly; mood does not display in that surface. The "sad sprite when out of tickles" behavior is sunset; an empty tickle bank is the heart counter's job, sadness is happiness's. Friends see your mood when they visit — the "Jen's pig looks sad — I should go tickle it" social pull.
- **Visit** — a social act distinct from blessing/cursing/trading: opening a friend's pig view to see their equipped look + mood and **tap-tickling their pig**. Each tap raises the host's pig (tickles + 25% happiness) and your own pig (tickles + full happiness). A fresh visit receives a server-rolled **3–7 tap** cap; after it is spent both pigs get [[Tired]], and that friend stays locked for 24 hours from the visit start. Separately, a player may open three distinct fresh Barns per rolling Wallow-scaled window (8 hours at W0 down to 3 hours at W5+).
- **Tired** — the in-fiction cap on a [[Visit]]. After 3–7 taps both pigs play the `tired` animation, a "come play another time" line shows, and you return home. Visit-only — the home pig never tires (it must spend its full tickle bank for snouts).
- **Streak** — a player's consecutive-engagement state, measured as the number of "personal days" they've tickled within a rolling 36-hour window since their last streak credit. Multiplies tickle regen alongside happiness, blessings, and curses on an independent axis: streak rewards loyalty regardless of social activity (where [[Happiness]] rewards friend-acts). Caps at day 30; hard-resets to 0 if no tickle within 36h of the last credit. Internal/technical name is `streak`; player-facing surfaces use a cozier label (e.g. "Devotion").
- **Garden** — the UI surface for streak. A growing visual object in the Barn's ambient layer (near Rosie, not overlapping her — happiness owns Rosie's pose + aura) that progresses through 5 stages as the streak builds. No number is ever shown; the visual _is_ the readout. Wilts briefly and resets to seedling on streak break.
- **Barn** — the player's home in TTP. Bifurcates into [[Exterior]] (the outdoor scene + clickable barn structure) and [[Interior]] (the decoratable indoor space). The current home-screen tab is being repositioned as the Exterior; the Interior is reached by tapping the barn structure.
- **Exterior** — the outdoor scene around the Barn. Rosie standing in front of a clickable barn building, with the existing background system as the outdoor scenery (pasture, beach, forest, etc.). Houses the [[Garden]] (streak), ambient effects, Hoofprints chips, and the entry point to the [[Interior]].
- **Interior** — the inside of the Barn. The decoratable space where [[Habitat]] lives. Accessed by tapping the barn structure on the Exterior; opens with a door-swing animation. Houses Rosie + the player's 6 habitat slots. Friends entering via the [[Visit]] screen see your decorated Interior too.
- **Habitat** — the player's decorated Interior. Composed of 6 typed slots (back wall, ceiling hanging, floor-left, floor-right, floor centerpiece, accent surface) plus an interior background (wallpaper/floor combo). Slot-based placement: items are typed and route to their matching slot — no free-drag chaos. Internal/technical name is `habitat`; player-facing surfaces refer to "the Barn" (the building itself).
- **Field Guide** — the evergreen discovery journal of the game's economy objects (truffles, snouts, wraps, lucky numbers, troughs, the Exchange, the feeding rhythm), living as a second shelf beside the seasonal [[Burrow Book]]. A journal, not a manual: every entry shows as a mysterious silhouette until the player first _meets_ the thing, then opens with a ceremony reveal — one whimsy line of what it is, one honest line of what it does or pays, with real numbers fed from server config so the guide can never lie after a rebalance. Feeling-surfaces (Mood, Garden, pair flames, happiness) are excluded by design — they explain themselves or stay mysterious (show-don't-tell).
- **Burrow Book** — the seasonal Pokédex-style shelf of a season's unique relics (route `dig-collection`): silhouettes until first catch, found-counts on dupes, set completion pays the season's dig-exclusive cosmetic. Seasonal where the [[Field Guide]] is evergreen; the two share one discovery home.
- **The Great Hunger** — Season 1's cooperative world-boss arc. The opening cinematic must anchor on the game's real art (Rosie's real sprite at `assets/images/sprites/rosie/idle_1.png` / `~/Desktop/ttp-refs/soccer-regen/idle_1.png`, and the selected Truffle King/Hunger reference), not an invented sticker/gouache/felt style. Its seven-shot story ends down: joy → theft → grey → empty world → the Hunger begins. Rosie does not win or look victorious in the intro; players earn the win through the co-op dig (every Sounder's finds starve him — no pig fights another pig).
- **Wallow** — the evergreen prestige lap after a pig earns one full pass-length of season XP. Wallowing keeps claimed rewards and overflow XP, raises permanent `profiles.wallow_count`, and starts a fresh visible Prestige Path (375 snouts, four Golden Truffles, one Mystery Hat Box per lap). Permanent power currently runs through W6: the configured interval reduction is +25 percentage points at W1 and W2, then +5 points per rank to a 70% shorter base interval; the shared three-Barn visit window also falls from 8 hours at W0 to 3 hours at W5+. `user_season_progress.wallow_count` is the season-local lap; `profiles.wallow_count` is permanent rank. Exact server tuning lives in `app_settings.wallow_tuning`.
- **Pig roster** — the pigs a player can choose for Home. Rosie is every player's permanent default. An active Slop Club member makes one **long-term companion choice** from Copper, Pepper, Bandit, Pickles, or Biscuit; that companion cannot be replaced for now, but the player can freely choose whether Rosie or their companion appears on Home from **The Pen** in the Shop. A lapsed member keeps the current companion but sees Rosie until membership resumes. Each identity has a complete baked main + Lounge animation pack authored from Rosie's poses. `utils/pigRoster.ts` + `hooks/usePigRoster.ts` own the client seam; the `pig_roster`, `recruit_pig`, and `activate_pig` RPCs own authorization and invariants.
- **Rosie's Loadout** — TTP's physical illustration-first card game. Rosie is its lead character and mascot; Rosie, Copper, Pepper, Bandit, Pickles, and Biscuit are the six playable **Legends**.
- **Legend** — Rosie's Loadout's top-level pig card class. Choose one before deck building and keep it face up outside the deck in the Legend zone. A Legend starts at 12 Cheer, defines two favored styles, has one unique ability, and gains levels/actions through [[Training]]. Legends are guaranteed play pieces rather than random deck pulls.
- **Training** — a Rosie's Loadout deck card's alternative use: tuck it beneath the Legend instead of playing it normally, permanently adding one level, one style icon, and an exposed action strip.
- **Critter** — a friendly small-creature card in Rosie's Loadout, such as a firefly, spider, frog, or mouse. Use _Critter_, not _Companion_: Companion already means the player's long-term non-Rosie pig-roster choice.
- **Enemy** — a Rosie's Loadout obstacle or antagonist placed into an opponent's Trouble row. Enemies may be mischievous, hungry, or lonely rather than evil.

## Seams

- **Receiver-side effects layer** — `hooks/useActiveEffects.ts` + `utils/activeEffects.ts`. Single owner of the read path (fetch, focus refresh, realtime subscription on blessings + curses) and the cleanse mutation (optimistic + RPC). Consumers: `components/ActiveEffects.tsx` (Inbox panel), `components/BarnActiveEffectsStrip.tsx` (Barn chips), `components/HoofprintsSheet.tsx` (Barn bottom sheet), `components/Barn.tsx` (derives `{blessed, cursed, sunBeam, phantomItch}` predicates for the BarnOverlay theming + lucky-pig sunBeam boost + phantom-itch miss gate). `components/CleanseModal.tsx` is pure UI behind `onConfirm`.
- **RPC layer** — `utils/rpc.ts` exports a single generic `rpc<T>(name, params?)` that absorbs the supabase-rpc cast + null/error handling for every call site in the app (~67 sites). Errors flow through `log.error()` to Sentry; callers receive `T | null` and pattern-match on the result. Named typed wrappers per RPC grow organically alongside each feature's deepening (see `utils/activeEffects.ts`, `utils/friendships.ts`).
- **Friendships layer** — `utils/friendships.ts` owns the friendship contract: the `FriendshipStatus` type, `FRIEND_CAP_LIMIT`, the `friendActionMessage(reason, cap, name)` error-to-copy helper, and typed wrappers around the six friendship RPCs (`getFriendIds`, `sendFriendRequest`, `acceptFriendRequest`, `cancelFriendRequest`, `getSuggestedUsers`, `searchUsers`). Render surfaces (Friends, UserSheet, Leaderboard, Inbox) consume it. Moderation (block / report) stays outside — different domain.
- **Barn orchestrator** — `components/Barn.tsx` is the home-screen orchestrator. Five hooks own the data + logic subsystems it used to inline: `useHomeStats` (stats state + `home_stats` RPC + refresh), `useStipend` (Slop Club monthly claim), `usePassEvents` ("X just trotted past you" dedup + toast emission), `useLuckyPig` (trigger / window / double rolls + AsyncStorage persistence + one combined Lucky Pig/title reward modal), `useActiveEffects` (the ritual-effect predicates that gate the lucky-pig sunBeam boost + phantom-itch miss + drive BarnOverlay theming). Cross-hook coupling is explicit via callback props at the top of Barn — `onClaimed: homeStats.refresh`, `showToast: barnShowToast`. Pure roll math lives in `utils/luckyPig.ts` and is unit-tested. Toast queue, heart floats, alignment watcher, the inline six-seven animation, tickle handlers, and pig animations stay in Barn. Release notes are manual-only in Me.
- **IAP adapter pair** — `utils/iap.ts` defines an `IAP` interface and two plain-object adapters: `realIAP` (wraps RevenueCat) and `noopIAP` (cancelled / empty for every method). Module-level `iap = IAP_ENABLED ? realIAP : noopIAP` picks one at load time; public re-exports (`isPro`, `presentPaywall`, etc.) delegate. Kill-switch decision concentrates at the seam instead of branching inside each function body. A future `mockIAP` for tests slots in as a third adapter with no call-site changes.
- **Race module** — `utils/race.ts` (split from `utils/dig.ts`, which keeps only the dig/feeding reads + milestone mapper): race server-contract types, the defensive standings parsers, fetches, cycle math, and the pinned-row selectors. Consumers: `useRace`, RaceSection, race-standings, SounderHomeCard (mixed import, split by side).
- **Duration module** — `utils/duration.ts` (absorbed `utils/time.ts`): three deliberately distinct formatters (`formatDurationCompact` rounds + collapses exact hours, `formatCountdownHM` floors, `formatClockMS` is a mm:ss clock with no hour rollover) + `remainingMs(iso)`. Callers keep their exact historical strings — do not merge the variants.
- **Leaderboard layer** — `hooks/useLeaderboard.ts` owns scope fetches, cursor pagination (hasMore/dedupe), and the `mark_all_pass_events_seen` side-effect; `components/Leaderboard.tsx` is rendering + UI state. **Pot-stake machine** — `hooks/usePotStake.ts` owns the sel/max/validity state machine both bury sheets share (chip styling + refusal copy stay per-sheet; pure math stays in `utils/burySnouts.ts`).
- **Config cell primitive** — `utils/configCell.ts` owns the "server owns the number, binary ships the fallback" lifecycle (read/apply/hydrate/refresh/ensureFresh, opt-in AsyncStorage cache + debounce, lazy-required rpc so `read()` stays native-free for pure importers like rooting). `feedingConfig` and `fieldGuideConfig` are thin cell declarations over it; a new config key is a declaration, not a copied lifecycle. `useFeatureFlags` deliberately stays a React-context module (different RPC, per-key safe-default-false, auth-refetch wiring) — do not fold it in.
- **Popup precedence registry** — `constants/popupPriorities.ts` declares every popup slot's priority as ONE ordered table (bands: 5 user-initiated · 10–30 ceremonies · 40–55 digests/reward flourishes), plus `CEREMONY_SLOT_IDS` (who stamps ceremonyGate). Call sites import named priorities; a source-scan test forbids bare literals, missing slots, and undocumented collisions. Sounder/feedback nudges, automatic release notes, Field Guide reveals, lucky-title follow-ups, and six-seven confirmations deliberately have no slots.
- **Realtime subscription layer** — `hooks/usePostgresChanges.ts` owns the postgres_changes channel lifecycle: auth-uid resolution, stable channel keys (`buildChannelKey`), spec normalization, and the teardown invariant (exact-channel disposer, idempotent, cancels mid-await on fast unmount — replacing the topic-refind teardown that could remove a live channel on fast remount). Subscribers (`useActiveEffects`, `useCrew`, `Inbox`) declare spec lists; `useLoungePeers` stays separate (presence/broadcast, a different mechanism).
- **Dig board kernel** — `utils/rooting.ts` now owns ONE `applySplash(layers, idx, kind)` physics kernel (rub −1/−0.5, shove −2/−1) called by BOTH `TrufflePatch.applyAction` and `simulateGreedyClear` (the depth-tuning test pins the real physics, not a copy), plus the cluster queries (`clusterRevealed`/`clusterTouched`/`clusterAnchor`) that TrufflePatch, `partiallyRevealedFinds`, and `digShareData` ask instead of re-walking board internals. `feedingPhaseView(nowMs)` is the single phase→countdown derivation behind both `bannerDigStatus` and useFeedingCta's `ctaClock`.
- **Dig session reducer** — `utils/digSession.ts` is the pure `(state, event) → state` machine for the dig session (open/submit lifecycle, window-stamped dug flag that expires by construction on rollover, practice-vs-real lockout gate, per-uid mirror key, reconcile debounce). `hooks/useRooting.ts` is the thin React/AsyncStorage/RPC adapter dispatching into it; its public interface and the submit last-gate normalization are unchanged. Every founder-reported dig incident is a replay fixture in `__tests__/digSession.test.ts`.
- **Season Pass layer** — `hooks/useSeason.ts` (mirrors `useCrew`) owns the `season_state` fetch + profile read + focus refresh and the claim / claim-all / [[Wallow]] actions over the existing RPCs, returning STRUCTURED results only (claim result, tally, wallow RpcResult) — all dialog copy, sounds, mystery-reveal staging stay in `app/(tabs)/season.tsx`. Pure tier/reward math lives in `utils/seasonPass.ts` (claimedSet, tiersByNumber, nextReward with the YOUR-TAKE semantics, readyTiers, prestigeMode), unit-tested. Server-side routing is LIVE (`20260773000000`): `claim_season_tier` resolves prestige+track server-side, `claim_ready_tiers` sweeps in one transaction; the hook falls back to the legacy per-tier RPCs on a null (un-migrated env / transport blip — safe, claimers are idempotent). The three legacy RPCs stay for shipped builds.
- **Reward-art resolver** — `utils/rewardArt.ts` is the single owner of the reward jsonb shape: `rewardItemId` (the legacy id-coalesce), `WEARABLE_REWARD_TYPES`, `resolveRewardArt` (discriminated kind), `cosmeticImage`/`cosmeticName`. Consumers: season StoneThumb + ClaimRewardDialog primitives, YourTakeStrip RewardArt, SpoilsShowcase, RaceSection. Replaces four hand-mirrored copies that drifted by discipline only.
- **Pig roster module** — `utils/pigRoster.ts` is the typed roster/RPC interface; `hooks/usePigRoster.ts` owns focus refresh and optimistic Home switching; `components/PigPenView.tsx` renders the Shop's recruit/preview/switch surface. `utils/pigs.ts` is the compile-time renderer catalog. `SpritePig → PigStage → SwipeElement` accepts a `pigId`, keeping animation/equipment composition independent from ownership rules.
- **Shop catalog layer** — `hooks/useShopCatalog.ts` owns the Shop's data lifecycle: the 5-way fetch (`daily_shop`, catalog + owned + profile selects with their pre-migration column-missing fallbacks, `shop_resets_in_seconds`), focus refresh, the reset countdown, and the optimistic mutators (`setCounter`/`setOwned`/`patchActiveIds`). `app/(tabs)/shop.tsx` is presentation + purchase/equip triggers only.
- **Cosmetic equip rule** — `utils/cosmetics.ts` owns "wear this": pure `computeEquip` (column routing + Face-slot exclusivity: glasses ⟂ mask) + thin `equipCosmetic` profiles write. Deliberately client-side (RLS-scoped single-column write); a server `equip_cosmetic` RPC was considered and deferred. Consumers: Shop screen + ClosetView's equip provider.
- **Notification route table** — `utils/notificationRouting.ts` owns ONE `NOTIFICATION_ROUTES` table (screen → in-app route); the accepted-screen set and `routeForScreen` derive from it, and the guard test reads the *actual* server emit list out of `supabase/migrations/*.sql` so the table and the server can't silently drift (the hand-maintained list drifted twice: a dead `trough` case, a missing `shop`). `trough` stays as a reserved alias to the Shop tab for future trough pushes; `utils/whileAway.ts` resolves through it deliberately.
- **Rarity tokens** — `constants/theme.ts` owns `RARITY_GRADIENT` (the light/dark pair per rarity used by Shop card gradients) and `RARITY_BG_SOLID` (the lighter end of each gradient, picked for surfaces that need a solid swatch like `ItemPreviewModal`). One source of truth so the two surfaces can't drift apart again. `RARITY_COLORS` (the accent color per rarity, distinct from the background) continues to live in `constants/hats.ts`.
