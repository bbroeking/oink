# Homecoming hierarchy prototype

Question: how should a complete Homecoming separate the named Discovery from
repeatable Farm materials so the payoff reads in one glance?

Three variants render on the real Position 10 route and use the existing
bottom switcher / `?variant=` URL:

- A — Reward Ledger: shipped baseline; quantity appears in both the Discovery
  plaque and stock ledger.
- B — Discovery First: one large, quantity-free Discovery leads into a separate
  compact Farm-supplies receipt.
- C — Unpacking Story: physical return props receive individual causal labels
  for Carrier, Discovery, Tool, and trail supplies.

All variants use the same reducer state, return animation, physical props,
reward amounts, action, persistence, and accessibility facts. The variants are
throwaway and must not ship on main.

## Verdict

Choose **B — Discovery First**.

The baseline labels `Glowroot Seed +2` as the Discovery and then repeats the
same quantity in Farm stock, collapsing world expansion and repeatable
materials. C makes every cause explicit, but four paper labels compete with
Rosie and the physical table. B creates the cleanest hierarchy: **Glowroot** is
the one new living Discovery, followed by a separate supply receipt containing
the exact Seeds, Compost, and Willow Fiber. It preserves the physical return
scene and gives the next action—taking a Seed to Bed 3—an obvious reason.
