# v0.108 annual Clover ending study

## Question

After the player harvests annual Clover and completes the first Adventure, what should Bed 1 show when Glowroot changes Home?

The three variants live on the existing Position 11 route and switch with `?variant=A`, `B`, or `C`.

## Variants

- **A — Resting soil:** Bed 1 remains visibly empty through the changed-Home scene.
- **B — Cut stubble:** a low, desaturated remnant remains after harvest.
- **C — Replant choice:** Bed 1 is empty but gains an immediate `Replant Clover` action.

## Verdict

**A — Resting soil wins.** It is the only ending that preserves the already-established harvest fact without implying automatic regrowth or adding another objective. The new Glowroot, open hedge, pond visitor, and next crop remain the scene's clear payoff.

Variant B reads as unexplained debris and can be mistaken for a regrowing perennial. Variant C makes the changed-Home payoff compete with a second planting decision that the next-morning crop choice already handles.

## Production decision

Keep Bed 1 `empty` from Clover harvest through Homecoming, changed Home, and the next morning. Do not add copy, a modal, a replant button, a new economy rule, or a Rive contract value.
