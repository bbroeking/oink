# v0.114 idle-watch structure prototype

Question: can Position 9 preserve its story, expected return, exact Bag, and
three-step route while reading as one calm glance instead of four paper cards?

Run `npm run prototype:homegrown`, then open Position 9 with `?watch=A`,
`?watch=B`, or `?watch=C`. The floating switcher and Left/Right arrow keys
cycle the same variants.

- **A — One field folio:** story, Expected Home, and Packed choices share one
  physical folio; the route remains an environmental progress line.
- **B — Trail leads:** the route moves above the story and becomes the first
  read.
- **C — Pocket journal:** story, route, and trip facts all live inside one
  larger journal surface.

Verdict: **A wins.** It removes both detached tickets, preserves the strong
story-first hierarchy, and reveals more of the Farm than C. B separates the
route from its explanation and leaves the top of the scene feeling scattered.
Main should retain only A's consolidated folio, without this switcher or the
losing structures.
