# v0.109 Provision beat prototype verdict

## Question

Can the first Adventure cause read as an event in Rosie's expedition instead of an inventory report?

## Compared

- **A — Field note:** the current large Provision receipt over the clearing.
- **B — Rosie picnic:** a small item-anchored caption, the food moving to Rosie, and a Rosie reaction.
- **C — Dusk answers:** no local card; the existing HUD names the cause while the food and clearing visibly change.

## Verdict

**C — Dusk answers wins structurally, with B's single Rosie response.**

A asks the player to read before watching Rosie. B proves that Rosie's reaction makes the packed Provision feel physical, but its caption still becomes a second card and overlaps the action. C preserves the illustrated clearing, keeps cause and effect legible in the existing HUD, and lets dusk or nightfall become the reward.

The production checkpoint should therefore:

1. remove the visible Field note from the Provision beat only;
2. move and consume the packed food at Rosie;
3. trigger one delighted Rosie motion without firing the Tickle game action;
4. let route-colored lights rise as the scene changes;
5. keep the cause text in the existing quiet HUD; and
6. retain the current short beat duration, reduced-motion behavior, and all deterministic Adventure outcomes.

This is a presentation correction, not a new system. Tool and Pack beats remain outside this checkpoint so the next director cycle can judge them from the newly improved baseline.
