# Crop-choice logic prototype

Question: which bounded model makes the remembered **Choose a Seed** moment
genuinely authored without losing Rosie's current **Lights Past the Open Gate**
purpose?

Run:

```sh
npm run prototype:homegrown:crop-choice
```

The TUI compares two Adventure-ready crops, a purposeful crop versus a
stockpile detour, and two beds planned in parallel. It keeps growth durations,
Compost, guaranteed yields, no spoilage, Farm stock, and whether Rosie's Bag
may open visible after every action.

## Verdict

The two-Adventure-crop model wins. A stockpile-only detour makes Rosie's clear
purpose wait through two complete farming cycles; a shared two-bed plan makes
one timer and one Compost decision govern unlike crops. Clover and Moonberries
instead remain independent stockpiles and viable Provisions with learned,
predictable differences.

On the real Position 2 route, the equal-crop layout wins over a weighted
recommendation and a separate time scale. The cards themselves already expose
duration, guaranteed yield, and Adventure effect, so both options read as
authored without another comparison control.
