# Pig head contact landmark audit

Generated by `python3 scripts/accessory-audit/measure-heads.py` from the 288
shipping PNGs (six pigs × 48 source frames). The JSON records a SHA-256 for
every source image. Product data receives the crown contact `(x, y)`,
skull/forehead tilt, raw measured width, and suggested head-width scale.

The cyan dot is the hat contact at the crown; the cyan bar is the local head
tilt. Measurements use the opaque skull contour in a pose-local window. The
window excludes ear tips, tears, blink shapes, eye spacing, and raised hooves.
Coordinates are on the 300 × 300 contain stage. Rosie `idle_1` remains the
authored baseline `(154, 16), 0°, 1.0`. Front-facing scales are relative to
Rosie `idle_1`. Turned `face` and `face_sit` scales are normalized within their
own families so the existing authored side-art perspective and per-animation
hat tuning remain intact; `rawWidth` is included for alternate calibration.
Raw width is the narrowest complete contiguous skull run sampled 65, 70, and
75 stage pixels below the measured crown. Tiny internal alpha gaps are bridged;
separate hooves and expression marks remain separate runs. This replaces the
old fixed ±105-pixel window that artificially capped many widths at 210.
Sad frames are the explicit exception: their ears fold into that band, so width
uses 85, 90, and 95 pixels below crown, after the ear silhouette separates from
the skull.

`current-vs-measured.png` demonstrates the principal failure in the existing
single-Rosie anchor table. In particular, current `sad_1` uses `(118, 16)` where
the crown contact is `(158.3, 14)`; `tired_1` uses `(192, 40)` where contact is
`(161, 32.9)`; and `jump_1` uses `(128, 116)` where contact is
`(158.7, 111.7)`. The same
Rosie points are currently inherited by Copper, Pepper, Bandit, Pickles, and
Biscuit even where their silhouette and framing differ.

## Measurement exceptions / uncertainty

- `face_1`–`face_4`, all pigs: side profile exposes only one strong forehead
  contour. Contact and local contour tangent are measured, but the missing far
  side makes the head-axis interpretation less certain. Verify broad brims.
- `face_sit_1`–`face_sit_4`, all pigs: the far ear overlaps the true crown, so
  the visible contour is ear/forehead art rather than the skull seat. All 24
  entries use the reviewed authored calibration `(178, 40), +6°`, marked
  `authored-occluded-crown`; their per-pig family widths/scales remain measured.
- `tired_4`, all pigs: the near ear folds across the crown. Contact follows the
  skull under the fold and should be checked for hats with unusually deep
  brims.
- `jump_1` and `jump_4`, all pigs: airborne compression makes the crown band
  narrower than rest. Position is high-confidence; scale should be treated as
  a real pose change but visually checked for rigid helmets.
- Rosie `idle_1`: y is pinned from the established product baseline rather than
  shifted by the contour fit's one-pixel anti-alias variation.

No frame fell back to a guide coordinate in this run. Of 288 entries, 264 are
contour-category entries (including the pinned Rosie `idle_1` product baseline)
and 24 `face_sit` entries are the explicit `authored-occluded-crown`
calibration. Boundary validation permits exemptions only for those 24 entries
and the named baseline; the baseline also remains within tolerance, so all 264
non-`face_sit` points pass against first-opaque source pixels. The per-pig
sheets are the complete frame-by-frame audit and
`measurements.json` records `method` alongside the reproducible source data.
