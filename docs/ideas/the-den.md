## 1. The Covenant (the law everything below obeys)

**"A timer punishes you for leaving; this game only ever pays you for
showing up — nothing you earn is ever taken."**

Operating rules:
- Streak-shaped things never hard-reset. Better: they're **archives** —
  lifetime accumulations that cannot decrease at all.
- Lapses are never attributed to a person. The fire rests; no pig is named.
- Pushes are **celebrations of completed outcomes only**. Never
  about-to-expire, never "your friend is waiting," never unfinished work.
- **The display line** (Codex's ruling, adopted): *presence may add a
  celebration; time may settle it back to a complete resting state; absence
  may never make anything look diminished, neglected, lonely, or less
  alive.* No multi-day cooling, no ash, no cold embers, no "last warmed"
  copy, no sorting by recency.
- **The export invariant** (the violation we'll be tempted into): shared
  artifacts may show *what happened and who appeared* — never non-actions,
  near-misses, contribution imbalance, or who failed to appear. The
  dangerous violations aren't timers; they're funny, data-driven social
  copy that quietly identifies the absent pig.

## 2. The Hearth Archive (Pair Flames, final form) — roadmap, post-share-cards

The grilled-and-rebuilt version. A pair of friends keeps a **hearth**: an
archive of **lifetime reciprocal days**. A kindness (trade fulfilled,
blessing, visit) leaves a non-expiring ember; any later kindness back
completes a **link** — max one per pair per calendar day. Links only ever
accumulate. No consecutiveness, no expiry, no relay deadline, no half-height
anything: **shared history, not relationship health.**

- Milestones at **3 / 10 / 30 / 75 / 150** lifetime days → the hearth art
  grows grander (5 stages) + matching pair cosmetics at the big marks.
- Cosmetics are **modular, not bespoke**: 5 base items (ribbon → hearth-pin
  collar → embroidered cape → lantern harness → ceremonial blanket) ×
  8 palettes × 12 crests × 3 trims = 288 stable pair identities from only
  **28 authored elements**. A pair keeps its crest/palette as items grow.
- Celebration on link-completion (flare + sparks, replayed for the absent
  pig on next open). Resting state always looks warm and finished.
- Honest role: **attachment, not DAU**. The daily engine is artifacts
  (below), not fragility. Kill-test before Phase-2 expansion: flame-lit
  pairs ≥1.5× both-active D14 vs matched pairs, no cannibalization.
- Devotion (ADR-0002's personal streak/Garden) amends to the same law:
  lifetime growth, no hard reset, warning push deleted. One covenant for
  every streak-shaped thing.

## 3. The Five Ships (next two seasons, in order)

| # | Ship | Status | Riskiest assumption |
|---|------|--------|---------------------|
| 1 | **Shared Dig text-grid share** — the dig receipt becomes a paste-able Wordle-style block; seeded crew boards make results comparable; "found the golden in N digs" is the 3/6 | roadmap (wedge Phase 1) | a non-player understands the artifact cold |
| 2 | **Herd-landing invite links** — one link drops the group chat into your Sounder as pre-approved knocks | roadmap | a game link in the gift doesn't read as acquisition spam |
| 3 | **Monday recap card** — spoils day produces the herd's week-story artifact | roadmap | a normal week reads as story, not analytics (see export invariant!) |
| 4 | **Oinkograms** — a daily 4-panel comic auto-composed from the day's real events (outfit, dig, visitor, blessing), exportable to the chat | candidate → promote | the writing budget survives novelty decay |
| 5 | **Sounder Supper** — herd drops found ingredients into a shared pot; completed recipes become illustrated pages + barn-table scenes | candidate | async contributions feel collaborative when one pig does most of it |

Oinkograms feasibility (Codex's budget for 90 days of daily novelty):
18 silhouette-distinct poses · 6 expressions · 8 narrative grammars
(escalation, eyewitness report, visitor interview, false tutorial,
courtroom accusation, before/after, internal monologue, callback) ·
6 layouts · **600 context-tagged caption beats** (+50–75/month), with
repetition guards (no caption within 45 days, no grammar twice in 7 days).
Honest target: pleasant daily, 2–3 irresistible per week — not 90 bangers.

## 4. The Library (candidates)

| System | Pitch | Pillar | Guilt-risk to dodge |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rosie's Ritual Shelf** | chosen small rituals (morning apple, bedtime bonnet) permanently add props/animations to a shelf | Collect | no consecutive-day requirements; Rosie never reacts sadly to a skipped ritual |
| **Garden Guests** | cumulative solo care attracts permanent barn visitors (moths, frogs, shy mice) with tiny story beats | Collect | guests may sleep/hide, never leave because you stopped |
| **The Truffle Cabinet** | odd dig specimens (singing tubers, moon truffles) fill permanent dioramas; dupes → pigments | Collect | seasonal specimens debut in a season, never vanish forever |
| **Rosie Remembers** | lifetime care choices teach Rosie harmless preferences (nap spot, greeting dance, tickle reaction) | Collect | additive/editable only; no early choice may weaken her |
| **Patchwork Pennant** | kindnesses add motifs to a Sounder banner; async choices pick borders/crests | Connect·Collect | hide contribution counts; no unanimous votes |
| **The Mud-Pie Ceremony** | at herd milestones anyone may ring the bell → replayable parade of the herd's collections | Connect | attendance can't affect rewards; ringing can't deny others their moment |
| **The Unfinished Pig Tale** | friends async-add prop/location/expression; the game binds it into a shareable mini picture book | Connect·Collect | no turn order, no expiry, any pig can advance it |
| **Porchlight Photo Booth** | open a brief photo session; friends who wander in appear in the strip; solo produces an equally collectible portrait | Connect | no invitation push, no "nobody came" state |
| **Hungerer Tapestry** | every community threshold adds a permanent illustrated panel of the valley reclaimed | all three | every panel is a victory; no failed-season ending exists |
| **Truffle Trails** | evergreen dig courses with personal-best + friend ghosts; mastery cosmetics | Contend | **parked** — easiest place to reintroduce obligation; hold until contention demand is proven |

Codex's structural notes, adopted: the Hungerer can never produce a failed
season (tiered transformation — every threshold a complete chapter); don't
ship two cadence systems at once (the barn must never feel like a
maintenance dashboard); celebration pushes must announce *completed*
outcomes, not incoming batons.

## 5. Where each idea goes when it grows up

Promotion path: candidate → grilled (the /grill-me treatment) → UI brief
(shape) → wedge-plan phase → charter log entry → build. Nothing skips the
grill; the flames redesign proved why (three "obvious" mechanics died under
questioning: mutual-day windows, half-height relight, warmth decay).
