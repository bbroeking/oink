# Build 62 — 2026-05-20

## Headline

The big one: friends, Tickle Trades, push notifications, the unified
achievements system, Lucky Pig, and the full Season 1 "Goblins vs
Angels" alignment system — schism reveal, blessings/curses, weekly
bounties, alignment leaderboard, and the Judgement Day finale.

## User-facing

- **Tickle Trades** — ask a friend for tickles (1-5), they fulfill,
  you repay the doubled thanks. 24h per-pair cooldown.
- **Friends** — add by username#discriminator, Discord-style.
- **Sounder referral** — invite links + a referral leaderboard.
- **Lucky Pig** — rare bonus windows where tickles double, with
  occasional one-off title drops.
- **Achievements** — a unified achievements screen with tiered
  rewards (titles, items, snouts) and a reveal modal.
- **Push notifications** — get pinged when a trade needs you.
- **Tap any user** on the leaderboard → a sheet with their stats,
  given/received totals, and friend / tickle / bless / curse actions.
- **Season 1: Goblins vs Angels**
  - Every player has an **alignment** that shifts with how they
    trade — give + repay + bless tips you Generous, receive + hoard
    + curse tips you Greedy. Shown everywhere identity shows.
  - A one-time **schism reveal** when you first cross ±25.
  - **Daily blessings** (warm gifts to friends) and **daily curses**
    (capped, cleansable mischief). Both rotate kinds daily.
  - **Weekly bounty board** on the season tab.
  - **Alignment leaderboard** — a third leaderboard scope ranking
    the most generous and most greedy.
  - **Judgement Day** finale — top 3 each side get exclusive titles.
- App renamed to "Tickle the Pig" (carried from build 61).
- Release-notes "What's new" modal on first launch after an update.

  the Barn tickle loop does not yet honor regen multipliers /
  half-taps / miasma overlay. The data layer is complete; a
  follow-up wires the Barn to read it.

  the Barn (needs a `my_active_effects` poll on Barn focus).
