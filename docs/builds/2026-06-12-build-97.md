# Build 97 — 2026-06-12

## Headline

The Shop got its design-handoff redesign (color is the rarity, the image is
the hero, the price chip is the action), visiting a friend stopped spending
your own tickles and now pays THEIR leaderboard, country flags became a
switch-anytime allegiance you tap on your Barn, referral codes finally have a
home on the Me page — and the referrer actually gets paid and told about it.

## Shop redesign (Claude Design handoff)

- **Today + Browse**: uniform 2-col grid (bento mosaic + featured hero
  retired), rarity legend up top, color dot per card instead of word pills,
  image full-bleed on a rarity-tinted panel, alternating sticker tilt.
- **The price chip is the action**: gold = buy, muted + 🔒 = can't afford,
  ✓ OWNED — no more per-card buttons (the "Not enc" truncation class is
  dead; Button text also auto-fits everywhere now). Tap a card → preview
  sheet → buy.
- Today always shows the full **8-item** daily drop.

## Visit & Tickle rework (server `20260646` ⚠️ push with this build)

- Visiting **no longer spends your tickle bank** — the YOUR TICKLES bar and
  its refill countdown are gone from the visit page.
- **PIG ENERGY** bar at the top instead: the shared visit budget (random 3–7
  taps), drains as you play, colored + labeled (wide awake → getting sleepy).
- Every tap now pays the host's **leaderboard** (+1 snout & score), not
  their bank.
- Tire-out no longer slams a full-screen scrim: a small "All tickled out!"
  bubble pops, and **Leave** opens the nap summary (hearts shared +
  next-visit timer).
- Visit page background fixed (was a 2×-zoomed corner of the art — Yoga
  intrinsic-size quirk, fifth sighting) and the two pigs now share one
  ground row, closer together.

## Flags = allegiance, not merchandise (server live: `20260640`)

- Country flags left the shop entirely (Browse too).
- **Tap the flag on your Barn** (or the "pick a country" pennant) to open
  the country picker — and you can **switch whenever you want** ("Switch to
  France" when changing). First pick still grants the Sunny Pitch background.

## Referrals (server live: `20260644`)

- **"Got a code from a friend?"** entry on the Me page refer card —
  pre-fills codes from invite links, +50 snouts on success.
- **Fixed: referrer rewards were unreachable** (the engagement gate was
  silently dropped in build 93's tickle hardening). Restored — and the
  referrer now gets announcements at redeem ("X used your code!") and at
  payout ("+100 snouts — 2 more for the Messenger Hat"), plus a "🐽 1
  friend on the way" line on the card while it's pending.

## Username moderation (server `20260645` ⚠️ push with this build)

- Banned/reserved usernames blocked at signup with "pick a different name"
  copy — tiered list with leet/unicode normalization, Scunthorpe-safe
  (Cassandra is fine), enforced client-side AND by a DB trigger.

## Quality of life

- **Snout balance updates instantly** on every spend: buys, titles, Trough
  seeds, chip-ins (no more delayed counter).
- Trough CTAs only appear for items actually in today's shop (server live:
  `20260639`) and the preview's ✕ never overlaps the art (paper chip).
- "Next tickle" toast counts down **live** on every tap and shows your TRUE
  regen rate — VIP, blessings, curses, alignment, happiness all folded in
  (server live: `20260643`).
- Truffle bury XP scales with the stake: 1/2/5 XP for 10/20/50 (live:
  `20260637`).
- 26 emoji-art PNGs re-cut from tall canvases — the achievement trophy
  (and friends) render full-size instead of as a speck.
