## User-facing — Visit & Tickle

- **Tap either pig to tickle** — no button. Each tap spends one of *your* tickles,
  gives it to the host, and makes **both** pigs happier.
- **Two heart tallies (YOU | host) that tick up together** on every tap, with a
  shared heartbeat pulse between them and synchronized "+1 ♥" floats — so it's
  unmistakable you each get a heart.
- **"YOUR TICKLES" bar** — your spendable resource, shown separately: count `/ cap`,
  "−1 each tap", and a live "+1 in m:ss" refill timer.
- **Energy on the host's pig** — a green→amber→red bar ("wide awake → sleepy") that
  drains as you play, so it's clear the *pig* is tiring, not your tickle supply.
- **"How visiting works" sheet** + a first-tap "tap a pig to start" nudge.
- **Nap screen** when the pigs tire out (or you arrive still on cooldown): hearts
  shared this visit + when you can visit again, and a "Head home" button.
- Still wired to the real rules: cost-a-tickle, one friend every 3 hours, the 7/hr
  tire ceiling, and the buried-truffle dig.
