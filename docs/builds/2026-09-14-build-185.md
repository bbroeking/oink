## Headline

The Friends list is one count line and its rows, in an order that never shifts, and a friend's profile scrolls all the way down.

## User-facing

- No empty band under the Friends title: the first friend sits one gap below the Friends/Add switch.
- The "your Sounder" strip is gone from the list. Sounder is the fourth button in the top nav, as before.
- Today's rituals are one quiet line, "Three glimmer left · three truffle left", counting down as you cast. Tap either half to read what today's blessing or curse does to a friend.
- The ritual name chips and their captions are off the list. Names and art stay on the row's action panel, the explain sheet, and the "blessed · Cloud Nine" notice a row shows after a cast.
- Friends stay in order: pinned first, then by name, with ties broken so two rows never swap places between refreshes. Nameless friends sort last.
- Opening a friend's profile scrolls to the bottom on every phone size and text size, so Report and Block are always reachable. The sheet bounces at its end again.
- Cloud Nine and Topsy-Turvy have new icons: a pig asleep on a cloud, and a pig on its head.
