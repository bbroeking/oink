## Headline

Every day has one blessing and one curse, and you wear them: seven of each, locked to the weekday, cosmetic only. It's Bacon Friday.

## User-facing

- Blessings and curses are a week now. Monday is Cloud Nine and Pickle Brine, Tuesday Bubble Bath and Topsy-Turvy, Wednesday Butterfly Crown and Pipsqueak, Thursday Confetti Snout and Little Raincloud, Friday Golden Hour and Bacon Bits, Saturday Firefly Night and Hiccups, Sunday Sunday Best and Old-Timey Pig. The day flips at midnight UTC, the same clock as the daily cap.
- Rituals no longer touch play. No regen change, no lucky boost, no free tickles or snouts, no snout pinch, no tap slips. Each is something you see on your pig or your Barn for six hours.
- The Barn shows the wash (amber, briny green, dusk with fireflies, sepia with grain); the pig shows the rest (floating on a cloud, bubbles, upside down, half size, a raincloud, bacon stripes, hiccups, a bow tie, a butterfly, a monocle); tickles pop confetti under Confetti Snout.
- Friends see it. Visiting a Barn shows the host's wash and pig exactly as the host sees them, tickling a Confetti Snout host pops confetti in their Barn, and a friend's profile portrait wears the still parts (skin, size, flip, forced cosmetics).
- The Field Guide's Mud Wrap page becomes Rituals.
- Effects already on you from the old set keep their old names and icons until they expire (twelve hours at most).
- Friends list: one "…" per row opens an anchored actions panel (Visit · Bless · Curse · Pin · Profile); rows sit at 70pt with even gaps.
- Every pig breathes: twelve-frame idles and a stage breath, and the barn-visit tallies show both pigs as dressed.
