The barnyard social pass: a clearer weekly Dig-Off, lasting traces of friend
visits, Rosie’s companion roster, and a broad native layout/accessibility polish.

  and compresses Monday’s prizes into a legible payout strip.

  Lounge, profiles, cards, cosmetics, and animation states.

  receive the current clarity, layout, and interaction pass.
