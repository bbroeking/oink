## The co-op dig

  guarded within each feeding (20260721). Three rotating dig games:
  Truffle Patch, Deep Root (new ambience/SFX set), Snout Hook.

  herd milestones pay purses + titles (Root Rustler 150 → Truffle Baron
  600 → Hunger's Bane 1800).

  regression fixed and tested; server cast hardened in 20260716/17).

  pig, and every board row expands into that crew's member ledger
  ("N this week · M season") — any crew, not just your own.
