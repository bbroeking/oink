## Headline

One app: a daily riddle, a trader who deals in sets, a seasonal Pop-up Stall, and the next season waiting in the wings.

## User-facing (on at review)

- **The Sheep's Riddle.** One shared picture riddle per UTC day: the Ghost Sheep shows a clue, two more on request, nine pictures, two guesses, a share line, "come back tomorrow". A `Riddle` row on the Barn button's fan. Local only; mints nothing. (`87c5a320`)
- **The Ghost Sheep trades in sets** (`7405aa5a`, `c279b643`; canvas A, https://claude.ai/artifact/Xkcm8AUVS4dtacVLHZoDFd). A pair handed over in one visit pays half again; three alike trade up one rarity (his seeded pick; rare has no trade-up); nine finds per visit (the Satchel stays unbounded). Applied tickles only, never snouts. Without the server half the sheet falls back to one-at-a-time rather than promising a bonus. Arrival and leave are toasts (the yard hedge scene was cut).
- **In-app What's new** entry `1.17.0` (the riddle, the trader's sets). (`cf943db7`)
- Everything in the 1.3 line since 1.2: the Barn and visits, Sounders of eight, the Satchel and swaps, Snout Deep, Feed · Draw · Pass and the Monday drum, the Wallow ladder, the copy and em-dash sweep (`5452304c`), measured per-pig head attachment (`ab06478d`).
