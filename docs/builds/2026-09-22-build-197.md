## Headline

A Wallow is a rank other pigs can see, and the ladder never ends: six ranks of regalia, then a prize every rank, forever.

## User-facing

- **The rank sheet is the ladder.** From the Wallow row on Me: where you are (portrait with your aura ring, this rank's regalia piece with *wearing / wear it*), the next rung with its prize and the two clock numbers (old value struck, new beside it), every later rung with its prize and a lock, then a *how to wallow* band with the pass XP bar and a door to the Season tab. Past W6 the rungs are headed *then, forever*.
- **The regalia.** Ranks 1–6 each grant one piece, one slot each, so a W6 pig wears the set: Rookie Wallow Cap · Bronze Wallow Specs · **Marsh Wreath** (the Marsh Crown, now worn at the neck) · Gilded Wallow Bow · Golden Wallow Trowel · Wallow Sovereign Crown (replaces the cap; both stay yours).
- **Past W6, every rank pays.** A keepsake frame at W10 and every fifth rank after, a new aura stage at W8 and every third rank after, otherwise two Golden Truffles and one Mote. Pigs already at W7–W11 are back-paid on the migration. No rank grants tickles.
- **Rank on the homepage.** A small `W{n}` mark tucked behind the tickle count. Nothing on Rosie herself; nothing at W0.
- **Gone.** The Closet's prestige-only filter, the Me *Prestige gear* row, and the Barn decor items as rank gifts (they stay for sale; anyone already granted one keeps it). The Barn Collection's *Wallow gifts* section now lists the inscribed keepsakes only.
