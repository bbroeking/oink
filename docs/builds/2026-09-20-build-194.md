## Headline

The Me tab is a passport: one identity card, then every big surface is a door — a page you read in one pass instead of four screens of stickers.

## User-facing

- **Me, simplified.** The scrapbook page had grown to seven stacked stickers and eight settings rows: the friend-code card, a Wallow rank card, the Achievements row, a tall Slop Club pitch, a taller referral card, the Golden Ticket, then Settings. Now it is the card and three short groups of rows (≈1.6 screens on a 17 Pro).
- **Your story** — *Wallow rank W{n}* (the rank on a tag; the sub says the rank's name and that W{n+1} refills faster) opens a **rank sheet** with what used to be the card: current, next, the two deltas, the `+ exclusive W{n+1} wearable` line, and the prestige-gear door. *Achievements* keeps its ready-to-claim badge. *Prestige gear* (`{n} earned · in your closet`) opens the closet on the prestige filter. A Slop Club member sees a fourth row, *Slop Club · ACTIVE* → the Pen (a referral-only member reads *Companion access through {date}*).
- **Invite & redeem** — *Invite new pigs* shows `CODE · n/goal · reward` with a **Share** button on the row; the row itself opens Referral Rewards (the pending list and the reward ladder already live there, and that page now carries a *the board ›* door to Your Recruits). *Got a friend's referral code?* is a row only for accounts the server would let redeem (never redeemed, < 24 h old, < 5 tickles) and opens a keyboard-aware sheet with the code field and Apply; a success still reads *You're in — thanks to {name}! +50* under it. *Redeem a Golden Ticket* is unchanged.
- **Slop Club** — for pigs who haven't joined, one gold band: the Rosie + Bandit pair, *Give Rosie a friend.*, one hand line (*A companion in the Pen, the members band, monthly drops.*), **Join the Slop Club**, the auto-renew line and Terms · Privacy. The "Also included · Premium season collectibles" row and the second headline are gone; plans and prices stay the paywall's.
- **The back door** — *Settings* opens a sheet: Change your name (with its cost line), Blocked users, Barn introduction · Manage subscription (members with a real subscription) and Restore purchases · Sign out and Delete account. *What's new* and *Found a bug or have an idea?* stay as rows on the page. The version line closes the page as before.
- Gone from the page, with a home elsewhere: the Wallow deltas (the rank sheet), the referral intro / milestone bar / recruiter standing / recent referrals / two lines of fine print (Referral Rewards), the membership "also included" row (the paywall), five settings rows (the Settings sheet). Copy-referral-code is gone as its own control — the share sheet's own Copy covers it.
- **Shop.** The strip that replaced the folded fitting room ("wearing N of M") is retired (`523eaba7`, 2026-09-19).

## Decisions taken while building

1. **The Slop Club band keeps Terms · Privacy and the auto-renew line.** Apple expects both where a subscription is sold, and the band still sells one; only the second headline and the "also included" row left.
2. **Manage subscription moved into Settings**, next to Restore purchases — a member's row on the page is a door to the Pen, not a billing control.
3. **The redeem box is a sheet, not a row-with-a-field**, so the page never grows a text input; it is shown under exactly the gate it was shown under before.
4. **The invite row's line is `code · n/goal · reward`** with the reward's leading "the" dropped — one line at 390pt with the Share button beside it.
5. **The recruiter standing strip is not carried** — its count is the same `referrals_completed`, and the board is one tap further, on Referral Rewards.
