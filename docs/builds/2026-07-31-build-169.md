## Headline

Sounder leaders can recruit beyond their friends, backed by an all-time
truffles-dug board and consent-based switching.

## User-facing

- Includes build 168's Barn update dismissal, Truffle Patch opening reminder,
  commuter-safe clock support, and Archery Bow held-slot fix.
- Sounder leaders can open **Recruit any snout** to browse players ranked by
  actual all-time truffles dug or search by username.
- A player already riding with another Sounder can accept and switch or decline
  and stay; no switch occurs without consent.
- Recruiting rows show each player's all-time truffle count and whether they are
  free to ride, already invited, or currently in another Sounder.

## Internal

- Truffle rank counts claimed `truffle_l` / `truffle_d` finds from submitted
  digs, excluding tickles and Golden Truffle currency bonuses/prizes/gifts/spends.
- Block checks, a 24-hour decline cooldown, reserved-seat caps, and explicit
  security-definer grants protect the recruiting path.
- The database harness proves truffle rank wins even when tickles and currency
  events point in the opposite direction.
