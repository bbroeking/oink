## Headline

The Dig has a nose that gets noticed, the Shop is a storefront with the herd's Trough by the counter and in the yard, and Monday's Barn Draw is a moment the whole crew hears.

## User-facing

- **Five free sniffs, then he notices.** Each dig gives five sniffs at the table's odds; every sniff past that draws the Hungerer's attention a step. The verb cards wear the live odds (*free · 3 left* → *1 in 120*…), his face lifts and his tag reads *noticing you*. *Tie it off · leave* is the gold button at every layer; *Dig deeper · reset* is a fresh board.
- **The Storefront.** The Shop is a chalkboard, hanging signs and shelves, a counter with today's buyers, and the herd's Trough by the counter.
- **The Trough in the yard and on the fan.** A friend's leading Trough stands beside the mound on Home, and a *Trough* row rides the Barn button; both open the Trough sheet. Friends' Troughs only — never your own ask.
- **The Barn Draw is revealed.** Every Monday one furnishing goes to one digger per herd. The winner sees a reveal sheet (once), everyone in the crew hears who drew (*"Jen drew the hay bale"*, tap → Season), and a *Barn Draw* row on the Race panel reopens the last result any time. The Trough's quarter prize opens the same sheet. (First draw on prod: Monday 2026-09-21.)
- **Your Monday purse reads as pocketed.** After you draw, the Race panel row says *Your Monday purse, drawn · 60 tickles pocketed · 1 in 8 for rare or better next Monday*, and reopening the sheet shows *60 tickles, pocketed.* with *Back to the race* — not a second *Pocket* button. (Bug report: "the award doesn't clear after claiming" — the tickles were always granted once; the copy said otherwise.)
- **No finds flash over the Barn after a dig.** The dig CTA slides out with the session it was showing.
- **The three habitat rooms** re-rendered empty (v2 art).
