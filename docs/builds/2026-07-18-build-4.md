# Android build 4 — 2026-07-18 (v1.3, Android versionCode 4)

## Headline

Tickle the Pig's first Google Play build, with the full cozy social game and
Great Hunger season prepared for Android.

## User-facing

- Tickle Rosie, visit friends, collect cosmetics, and join a Sounder on Android.
- Dig for Golden Truffles and help the valley through the Great Hunger.
- Sign in with email, manage your account, and request full account deletion.

## Internal

- Added the generated Android native project for reproducible local AAB builds.
- Targets Android API 35 for current Google Play submission eligibility.
- Removed unused microphone, legacy storage, and system-overlay permissions.
- Disabled Android application backup for account/session safety.
- Android purchase UI fails closed until the Google Play RevenueCat app and
  public Android SDK key are configured; iOS billing is unchanged.
- Updated the public privacy policy and Terms for Google Play distribution.
- Added Play listing copy, a Data safety worksheet, release checklist, app icon,
  and feature graphic under `docs/google-play/`.

## Known issues

- Android push notifications require the Firebase/FCM credentials described in
  `docs/google-play/release-checklist.md`.
- Slop Club and Season Pass purchases remain hidden on Android until Google Play
  products and the RevenueCat Android app are configured and tested.
