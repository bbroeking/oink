## Headline

Feedings now follow each player's local midnight, 8 a.m., and 4 p.m. schedule, and completed digs stay out of the way until the next Feeding.

## User-facing

- Feeding windows display at midnight–4 a.m., 8 a.m.–noon, and 4–8 p.m. in the player's registered local timezone.
- Countdown, rooting, Barn, and Sounder actions use the same server-owned Feeding windows.
- A completed dig action is hidden until the next local Feeding opens.

## Internal

- Registers the device IANA timezone after authentication and observes the server's deferred timezone-change rules.
- Uses namespaced local-schedule window IDs while preserving completed legacy Feeding IDs and transition-week behavior.
- Preserves the exact build 180 source graph outside the bounded feeding-client delta. `utils/database.types.ts` remains at build 180 to avoid unrelated generated habitat-schema churn; the widened RPC helper compiles without that generated change.
