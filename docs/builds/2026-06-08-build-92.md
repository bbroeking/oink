# Build 92 — 2026-06-08

## Headline

The Trough gets legible and easier to fund, blessings/curses say what they do,
and the truffle-dig + Trough-funding fixes (server-side, already live) get their
client polish. Two server regressions were fixed and pushed ahead of this build.

## Truffles — digging actually works again

- **Root cause (server, already pushed `20260618`):** the live `dig_truffle`
  called the admin-gated `send_system_announcement`, which raised `admin_only`
  for every non-test user and rolled back the whole dig. It only "worked" on
  admin/test accounts — i.e. broken for real players. Fixed by inlining the
  announcement INSERT (the pattern `tickle_at_barn` already used).
- **Client (this build):** `dig()` no longer hides the shovel on a transient
  failure, and shows "Already dug up!" on a lost race instead of vanishing with
  no reward.

## Troughs — clear, fundable, shareable

- **Reward rate (server, pushed `20260622`):** 10 snouts → 1 tickle (was 2:1),
  paid out when the drive funds. **+5 season XP** on your first chip-in each day.
- **`donate_to_drive` regression (server, pushed `20260619`):** same admin-gate
  rollback as the truffle — funding donations silently failed for normal donors.
  Fixed (inlined the announcements).
- **Card redesign (client):** names the prize ("Unlocking: Cowboy Hat"), a
  "how the Trough works" explainer, social proof ("6 friends in · you've chipped
  40"), "640 to go", and a reward preview ("+5 tickles when it fills · +5 XP now").
- **Easier to contribute:** one-tap preset amounts **10 / 25 / 50 / Max** (Max =
  fill the gap, capped to your balance) replacing the ±2 stepper, with the balance
  shown and real error messages (the old code silently swallowed insufficient /
  cooldown).
- **Ask your Sounder (server `20260623` + client):** new `nudge_trough` RPC —
  opener-only, 6h cooldown — drops a "needs your help" card into each friend's
  WhileAway feed, surfaced as an "Ask your Sounder to chip in" button on your own
  Trough.

## Blessings / curses — what they do is obvious now

- The Barn active-effect chip now shows the **effect blurb** ("1-in-3 taps slip —
  for 24h") under the name, instead of making you tap through to learn it.
- The ritual "✦ sent ✦" confirmation now shows the effect, so the caster sees
  what they gave/inflicted.

## Other (server, pushed)

- **Friend-request block bypass (`20260620`):** the block check lived on an
  unused 1-arg overload; the client always hit the 2-arg one. Consolidated so
  blocked users can no longer send friend requests.
- **Happiness floor (`20260621`):** one-time lift of every decayed pig to 50
  (true middle of Content) — 17 sad pigs → 0, avg 35.8 → 51.7. Floor only; the
  engaged weren't demoted.
