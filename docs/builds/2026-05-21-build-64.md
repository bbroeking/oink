# Build 64 — 2026-05-21

## Headline

The trade economy flipped, the Tickle Trade modal reborn as a 1920s
livestock yard, and Season 1 polished — alignment as a Greedy↔Giver
bar, hand-drawn alignment emblems, and the whole Season 1 migration
batch finally pushed to the database.

## User-facing

- **Trade economy flipped** — when a friend fulfills your request,
  the **asker now pockets DOUBLE**; the giver spends N from their own
  bank for nothing but goodwill. No repay step — a fulfilled trade is
  terminal. Asking is the profitable move; alignment is the only
  counterweight. (This is the Season 1 design, corrected.)
- **The Tickle Stockyard** — the trade modal is now a 1920s
  livestock-auction reskin: a hanging sign, pen cards with fence
  rails, brass GIVE buttons, a chalkboard tally. Same mechanics.
- **Alignment is now a bar** — UserSheet shows a Greedy ◄──► Giver
  continuum with your marker, replacing the old chip.
- **Alignment emblems are real art** — hand-drawn halo / balance
  scale / goblin horns, replacing emoji, everywhere alignment shows.
- **Invite simplified** — "Copy my code" copies your handle to the
  clipboard. The fragile GitHub-Pages invite link is gone.
- Release notes now drip — Season 1 features announce themselves on
  their dates rather than all at once.
