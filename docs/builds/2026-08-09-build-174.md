## Headline

A hardening build: the full-codebase deslop sweep and typed Supabase client
ship alongside dark AdMob rewarded-refill scaffolding, a new app icon, and a
new splash screen.

## User-facing

- New app icon and splash screen.
- Equipping a title from the Barn now reports a failure ("Couldn't equip
  title") instead of always claiming success.
- A failed profile read can no longer snap the alignment placard to neutral
  with a phantom "toward Greedy" shift toast.

  back to an unmapped key).

  its ✕; reduced-motion snaps instead of sliding). Commit `6a30b3d`, added to
  this candidate after 172's rejection.

  the feature is dark behind a server flag that does not exist in production.
