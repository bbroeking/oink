## Headline

On a visit the two pigs actually turn to look at each other — bigger, in a room that's finally theirs to furnish.

## User-facing

- **Pigs face each other.** Visiting a friend, your pig turns toward theirs and theirs toward yours — a real three-quarter turn, standing outside and sitting inside — instead of two pigs staring out at you. Hats, glasses and the rest ride the turn. When their pig naps or waves it still turns its head your way.
- **Bigger pigs inside the Barn.** The Inside view was drawing both pigs at roughly a third of the size it should; they are now about twice as large, and the "you" tag and the tickle count under them are readable.
- **The Barn rooms are empty by default.** Warm Plank Barn, Spring Whitewash and Midnight Rafters no longer come with a painted shelf and wardrobe; what's in a room is what its owner put there. The Shelf decorating spot draws its own plank under whatever you set on it.
- Your pig keeps facing your friend's pig for the whole visit (it used to swing back to the camera with a grin after the first shared heart); the hearts still fly.
- Outside, the two pigs stand a breath further apart so the turn doesn't bump noses.
