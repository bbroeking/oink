## Headline

real snouts for finishing them), a 25-piece batch of **Mud War cosmetics** is

behind a flag for season 2. Under the hood, a `/deslop` cleanup pass.

## First-Week Checklist — "Rosie's chores" (server `20260649` ⚠️ push with this build)

- A guided checklist now lives on the **Barn under Rosie**, walking new
  players through the five D1–D7 retention actions:
  - **First tickle** — give Rosie her first tickle (+25)
  - **Dress Rosie up** — equip a hat or anything (+25)
  - **Make a friend** — add your first friend (+50)
  - **Visit a Barn** — visit a friend's barn and tickle their pig (+50)
  - **Cast a ritual** — bless or curse a friend (+50)
- **200 snouts** total. Rewards are **server-authoritative + idempotent**: the
  client never grants snouts — it calls `claim_onboarding()`, which re-checks
  each milestone and dedupes via the `onboarding_claims` PK + `ON CONFLICT`.
- Re-checks on focus and after each tickle, so progress updates live.
- Spec: `docs/onboarding-first-week-checklist-spec.md`.

## Mud War cosmetics (server `20260650` ⚠️ push with this build)

- **25 new cosmetics** seeded into the catalog — hats (Muddy Cap, Slop-Bucket
  Hat, Reed Hat, Bog Helmet, Swamp Crown, Rosette Cap), held items (Slop
  Bucket, Mud Shovel, Mud Pie, Golden Truffle, Crew Pennant, Festival
  Pennant), auras (Mud Splatter, Swamp Bubbles, Fireflies, Golden Bog,
  Heirloom Mire, Confetti), backgrounds (Mud Pit, Reed Marsh, Mud Derby
  Grounds, Bog at Dusk, Golden Mire, Festival Night) and a Prize Sash (neck).
- **War-exclusive — `cost 0` = NOT for sale** (`buy_hat` rejects cost ≤ 0).
  These are granted by the future War Spoils system, never sold in the daily
  shop. The `war_exclusive` / `war_season` / `set_id` columns land with that
  later build; for now cost 0 is the lock.
- **Verified shop-safe** (the leak check): cost-0 items are excluded from the
  daily drop (`daily_shop()` filters `cost > 0`, retained through `20260632`)
  and from Browse (client filters `cost > 0 || owned`). They surface only once
  a player has been *granted* one, then show as OWNED/equippable. No
  accidental shop appearance.
- Art bundled in this build; each row carries an **emoji fallback** for
  not-yet-updated clients.
