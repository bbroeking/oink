## Headline

Bullion's Market: one price, two ways to trade.

## User-facing (new in 201)

- **Bullion's Market** (spec `docs/design/market-2026-10-31/bullions-market.md`; SKILL.md 2026-10-31): a **Market**
  row on the Barn button's fan opens it.
  - **The Counter.** Bullion the bull always buys a set of any find a little under its price and sells one a little
    over. Every set at his counter moves the price; quiet prices wiggle and drift home every half hour. Each find
    has a sparkline and a two-day chart with his buy/sell band and every trade.
  - **The Noticeboard.** Pin a set For sale, a Wanted ad, or a straight-up Swap, to everyone or to one friend
    ("For you"). A price between Bullion's two prices beats his counter for both sides. Up to 4 listings, 24 hours each.
  - Confirm sheets show where the price goes before you tap; Bullion's receipt cards (gavel, cheer, shrug); your
    ledger and bag with what you paid; three onboarding cards on the first visit.
  - Paid in tickles ready (the bank); sales may bank past the cap.
