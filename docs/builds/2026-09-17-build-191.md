## Headline

Hand a friend's pig the find it is hoping for and take something back: a swap in the barn, one a day with each friend.

## User-facing

- **Swapping finds at a barn.** Tap the lifted find in your Satchel strip on a visit and a tray rises: up to three finds that friend's pig can spare, and *…or just give it*. Take one and the two finds change hands — theirs into your bag, yours into theirs. Both pigs still get their 3 tickles.
- **Giving still counts as giving.** *Just give it* takes nothing back and is the only one that pays a generous tick, the way a delivery always did.
- **What you receive is yours.** A find that comes back from a swap sits in your bag like one you dug — tossable, giveable on, and it lifts its silhouette in the catalog. The shelf is gone; nothing that was on it was lost.
- **One swap per friend per day.** After a swap the pig shows its next wish under *next time*, and that friend's row stops marking until tomorrow. The Friends row mark still says *you have it* for everyone you haven't swapped with today.
- **The host hears about it.** *"Jen swapped your pig the blue feather it was hoping for and took the old key."* — on the while-away card, tappable straight to the Barn, and as an Inbox row.
- **The Board counts swaps.** The delivery line reads *N swapped*.
- **Your tickle breakdown names them.** Swap tickles have their own slice instead of landing in the home-taps residual.
- Everything in builds 183–190 — see *Week in review* below.
