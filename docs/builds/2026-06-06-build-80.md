# Build 80 — 2026-06-06

## Headline

The big social + care build: **pig happiness** (Rosie visibly gets sad/happy by
how consistently you tickle her, and it speeds/slows regen), **barn visiting**
(go to a friend's pig and tap-tickle it until you both tire out), and a redesigned
**Closet** with **multi-slot cosmetics** (wear a hat *and* glasses at once). Plus
the World Cup suite and a pile of fixes. Also: Sentry now tags the signed-in user
so crashes are attributable.

## User-facing

- **Pig happiness.** Tickle your pig consistently → she's **Happy** (faster
  regen); neglect her → she gets **Sad** (slower regen). Her **idle sprite is the
  readout** — sad / content / happy faces, no number or meter. Window-capped so
  spreading tickles out beats binging.
- **Barn visiting.** From a friend's profile, **Visit their Barn** → see *both*
  pigs (yours + theirs) and **tap-tickle their pig**. Each tap makes you both
  happier + sends tickles. After 3–7 taps **both pigs tire out** ("come play
  another time!") and you head home. New **`tired` pig animation**.
- **Buried truffle.** Bury a truffle on your Barn (20 snouts); the first friend
  to visit digs it up.
- **The Closet.** The Shop's wardrobe is now a dress-up screen: live pig preview,
  tappable slot chips, by-category owned-item grids, tap to wear it live.
- **Multi-slot cosmetics.** Hat / Eyes / Face / Neck / Aura / Held / Background /
  Flag are now **separate slots by anchor** — wear a hat *and* glasses together.
  Existing equips were migrated to the right slot.
- **World Cup.** Allegiance pick (47 flags), soccer backgrounds + always-buyable
  soccer ball, flags on the pig + leaderboard + Barn corner.
- **Leaderboard cleanup.** Demo + junk accounts no longer appear on the board.
