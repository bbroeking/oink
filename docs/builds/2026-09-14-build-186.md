## Headline

Your Dig fills a Satchel; a friend's pig is hoping for one of the things in it; hand it over and you both get tickled. And on a visit, you tickle *their* pig — the two of them face each other now.

## User-facing

- **The Satchel.** Every Dig can turn up a small find — a river pebble, a blue feather, a four-leaf clover, a snail shell, a brass button, a tuft of wool, red berries, a pinecone, an old key, a chip of honeycomb, a glass marble, a tin whistle. The receipt says what went in ("your Satchel got heavier") or what stayed in the mud when the bag was full (six finds). The Satchel is a row on the Barn button's fan.
- **Every pig is hoping for something.** Yours shows it in the Satchel sheet, with one free "Not this one". Friends' pigs show it as a thought bubble when you visit, and a friend's row marks "you have it" when the thing their pig wants is in your bag.
- **Hand it over.** On a visit the matching find is lifted in the strip above the action bar; tap it and their pig gets it. Both pigs get three tickles, the receipt names their pig first ("Maple's pig got the blue feather it was hoping for — you both got 3 tickles"), and their wish moves on to something else. A wrong find just gets sniffed at and stays in your bag. You can still hand a find over after the tickles are spent, and from a napping barn ("Leave a find").
- **Deliveries count.** "N delivered" sits beside tickles on the Board and in the Satchel sheet; at 10, 50 and 100 deliveries the sheet grows a keepsake. Never Snouts, never Golden Truffles — finds are given, not sold.
- **The Satchel sheet** shows the bag (hold a find to toss it), every find as a silhouette until you have carried one, what friends have brought your pig, and your keepsakes.
- **Visiting: their pig is the one you tickle.** Your own pig waves when tapped and, once, says so. The two pigs stand on one ground line facing each other.
- **Tickled out is something you can see.** The chip under their pig counts the visit's tickles and, when the visit is spent, becomes a "tickled out" tag while their pig naps; a tap on the napping pig wobbles the tag. Your pig beams once a heart has been shared. Hearts move the instant you tap and settle to the server.
- The host's return brings a system line: "Jen brought your pig the blue feather it was hoping for."
