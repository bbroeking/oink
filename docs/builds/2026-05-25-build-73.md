### Bug fixes
- **`fulfill_tickle_trade` regen**. Trade fulfillment was checking against raw `user_items.item_count`, ignoring regen catch-up. Players saw "you have 9" everywhere except the trade insist they had 3. Fix: materialize regen catch-up into `item_count` before the balance check; deduct + reset `last_increment` atomically.
- **Achievements page tall-chip layout**. Filter chips were rendering as 600pt-tall vertical pills because the horizontal ScrollView's contentContainer defaulted to `alignItems: stretch`. Two fixes: `flexGrow: 0` on the ScrollView + `alignItems: center` on the contentContainer.
