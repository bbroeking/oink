## Headline

The Season page is three verbs — Feed, Draw, Pass — and Monday's purse is a drum you fill by digging.

## User-facing

- **The Season page is simpler.** Four cells became three: **Feed · Draw · Pass**. The Herd and Race panels are gone, and so is everything on the page that asked you to belong to a Sounder first. The Sounder still lives on the Friends tab.
- **Monday's drum.** Every dig you submit during the week (Monday to Monday, UTC) drops one ball in your own drum, up to one per feeding (21 on the usual schedule). On Monday the drum turns once for you: each ball is one spin of the same purse wheel and you keep the best. More digging, better odds, and the odds are printed before Monday from your own count — `rare or better: 1 in 2`. No streaks, no herd standing, no rank, and nobody else's week changes yours.
- **The Draw panel** prints three lines and one button: your balls this week, the odds at that count, when the drum turns; then *Draw your Monday purse* when one waits, the receipt once it's drawn, or a quiet invitation if the week held no balls. A full drum reads *21 of 21 · every last feeding*.
- **The Feed panel** shows your ball counter under the dig button (`3 balls this week · +1 if you dig now`). Its kicker now says *next feeding in 3h 5m* once the patch has closed, instead of *feeding open* for the whole window.
- **The weekly Dig-Off, its standings page, the Monday spoils ladder and the per-herd furnishing prize are retired.** Bunting and keepsakes already earned stay owned. The Trough giver's weekly reward is unchanged.
- **One Monday push**, only to snouts with a ball in: *The drum turned — 7 balls in, see what you drew.*
- The season guide is three steps: dig every feeding, draw on Monday, starve the Hungerer.
