# Build 95 — 2026-06-11

## Headline

Titles are switchable from the Closet (with a tappable nameplate on Rosie),
the Closet's overflowing art and tiny tap targets are fixed, and buried
truffles can be re-dug by the same friend after a 3-hour wait — no more
stranded pots for small Sounders.

## Closet — titles + cleanup (tracker #2)

- **Titles in the Closet:** a Titles section now lives inside the Closet
  itself (same equip state as the Shop → Titles tab, which stays for the
  battle-pass deep link). The in-app copy pointing at the "Wardrobe" is
  finally true.
- **Tappable nameplate:** Rosie's fitting-room preview shows her current
  title under her ("No title — tap to pick" when bare); tapping scrolls to
  the picker. No new native modals (popup-queue discipline).
- **Overflow fix:** item art no longer spills out of tiles. Root cause: Yoga
  doesn't resolve %-sized children against aspectRatio-derived heights, so
  big art (1024² legendaries, 752×1584 backgrounds) rendered at intrinsic
  size. Absolute insets + contain + overflow hidden.
- **Tap targets:** slot-chip ✕ now a true 44pt zone; unequip row padded.

## Truffles — re-digs after 3h (tracker #1, server `20260629` pushed with this build)

- One-dig-EVER per visitor per truffle stranded shared pots (live case: pot
  20 → Jen 8, Freddy 5, ~7 stuck forever; host blocked from reburying).
- **Server:** `truffle_digs` gets a surrogate PK; `dig_truffle` now enforces a
  per-(truffle, digger) **3h cooldown** instead — same bite math (40% of
  remaining), returns `dig_cooldown` + `next_at`.
- **Client:** the dig shovel reappears once your last dig on that truffle is
  3h old (the old gate hid it forever after one dig — caught in verification);
  cooldown copy: "You've dug here recently — come back in Xh Ym."
