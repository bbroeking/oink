## Headline

Perfect weekly Feeding attendance now earns a one-time achievement, 500 snouts,
and the Unmissable title.

## User-facing

- Submit a dig in every server-scheduled Feeding of a full Monday-to-Monday
  Dig-Off week to earn Every Last Feeding, 500 snouts, and the Unmissable title.
- Count attendance rather than luck: a zero-find submission counts, while one
  missed Feeding does not.
- Backfill the reward for historical full-week qualifiers and keep all future
  awards one-time and settlement-driven.
- Show every current real player one persistent feature announcement on their
  next login; in build 179 the announcement opens Achievements when tapped.
- Show Rosie's personal manual-tickle Streak and one shared Visit Streak per
  friendship; same-cadence repeats do not double-credit either streak.

## Internal

- Compute the canonical weekly Feeding slate from the server clock, including
  the dormant 28-window commuter schedule and boundary-straddling openings.
- Keep Dig-Off payout settlement fail-soft: an achievement failure cannot block
  weekly Sounder spoils.
- Route allow-listed system announcements through the existing notification
  screen map while preserving malformed Trough nudges as non-pressable.
- Add the Contraption inventory/service ledger, guaranteed Clockwork Acorn Mote
  reward family, Auto-Tickler service sweep, and personal/Visit Streak ledgers;
  their Mote UI remains compile-time hidden.
- Normalize the durable Mote receipt across both known schema histories. The
  compatibility repair is SQL/types-only and does not change the signed app
  bundle.

  does not authorize committing or pushing all current source changes.

  happened after build 179 was signed. Those files are not packaged in the IPA,

  retires its earlier experimental three-input spin RPC; a tester who remains on

- Current public clients can display and dismiss the feature announcement, but

- Real-device login-announcement, streak, achievement-digest, accessibility,

  production observation after activation.
