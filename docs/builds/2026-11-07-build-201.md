## Headline

Bullion's Market: buy and sell finds at the counter, or sell high to other pigs.

## User-facing (new in 201)

- **Bullion's Market** (spec `docs/design/market-2026-10-31/bullions-market.md`; SKILL.md 2026-10-31, 2026-11-01,
  2026-11-07): a **Market** row on the Barn button's fan opens it.
  - **Sets** are three of a common, two of an uncommon, or the tin whistle on its own.
  - **The Counter.** Bullion the bull always buys a set of any find a little under its price and sells one a little
    over. Every set at his counter moves the price 5% of usual; quiet prices wiggle and drift home every half hour.
    Each find has a sparkline and a two-day chart with his buy/sell band and every trade.
  - **The Noticeboard.** Pin a set For sale, a Wanted ad, or a straight-up Swap, to everyone or to one friend
    ("For you"). A price between Bullion's two prices beats his counter for both sides. Up to 4 listings, 48 hours
    each. **Bullion's daily shelf:** three sets a day priced inside his gap, one per pig a day.
  - Confirm sheets show where the price goes before you tap; Bullion's receipt cards (gavel, cheer, shrug); your
    ledger and bag with what you paid. If Bullion doesn't answer, the sheet says so and keeps the same trade, so a
    retry can't trade twice.
  - **Onboarding** (the round-4 design): the fan row reads "new here? · buy, sell, swap" until the three cards are
    done; three cards in Bullion's voice end on "Step up to the counter" and a guided first trade on a set you hold;
    then three one-time moments: "You moved the price!", "Nobody's traded pinecones for a while" (a quiet price
    wandering home), and "Nice selling." (a pig paid you more than Bullion would have).
  - Pushes when a friend pins an offer for you, or your listing sells, crosses or swaps.
  - Paid in tickles ready (the bank); sales may bank past the cap.
- **The Ghost Sheep** pays his double for the find he fancies only when you didn't buy it (dug, given, swapped or
  traded up); a bought one pays his usual price, and his sheet says so. From Season 2 day one his fancy is sometimes
  an uncommon or the whistle (70 / 25 / 5 by rarity), not nearly always a common.
- **What's new** (`constants/release_notes.ts` 1.18.0): Bullion's Market, selling high to other pigs, the Sheep's
  double for what you find.
