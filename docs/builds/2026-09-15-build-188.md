## Headline

Rosie is one pig again: same pink in every pose, nothing under her hooves, and every reaction lands instead of flashing past.

## User-facing

- **Reactions you can see.** A tickle's jump, surprise, happy wiggle and wave all play a fifth slower — a jump is now 0.8 s instead of two-thirds of one — and the standing idle breathes on a 2 s loop instead of 1.6 s. Sitting and turned-toward-a-friend rests are unchanged, so a pair in a Barn still rests in step.
- **Rosie's colour holds.** Her pink used to shift when she moved from idle into a reaction (the tickle answers ran pale and washed) and when a friend arrived in the Barn. Every family now shares the idle's pink; the sad mood keeps half its greyer cast on purpose.
- **No disc under her hooves.** The pale ellipse that showed under Rosie's feet when she was happy, surprised, or sitting in a room (and the stray specks around it) is gone. Copper, Pepper, Bandit, Pickles and Biscuit never had it.
- **A blessing or curse you send rises as a bubble.** From a friend row's Bless/Curse door or the Inbox's bless-back, the ritual's own art rises in a soap bubble from the bottom of the screen to just under the header and pops, with a tag naming the ritual and the friend. A blessing is buoyant with sparkles; a curse is heavier with drips. Reduce Motion: a cross-fade mid-screen. The profile sheet's Bless panel keeps its own "sent" beat. Refusals still show the toast.
- **Friend rows sit straight and mark rituals in art.** "blessed · Cloud Nine" as words is gone; a friend you've blessed or cursed today wears the ritual's art on a small sun (blessing) or sage (curse) disc at the end of their line — VoiceOver still hears the full sentence. Rows no longer lean, and the card's edge and shadow are whole on both sides.
- **Toasts show on the visit screen and over the profile sheet.** "Not the old key", "Their pig changed its mind" and "All tickled out" used to fire invisibly under the scene. They show now, and read as a short title plus a line.
