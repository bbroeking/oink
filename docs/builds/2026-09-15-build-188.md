## Headline

Rosie is one pig again: same pink in every pose, nothing under her hooves, and every reaction lands instead of flashing past.

## User-facing

- **Reactions you can see.** A tickle's jump, surprise, happy wiggle and wave all play a fifth slower — a jump is now 0.8 s instead of two-thirds of one — and the standing idle breathes on a 2 s loop instead of 1.6 s. Sitting and turned-toward-a-friend rests are unchanged, so a pair in a Barn still rests in step.
- **Rosie's colour holds.** Her pink used to shift when she moved from idle into a reaction (the tickle answers ran pale and washed) and when a friend arrived in the Barn. Every family now shares the idle's pink; the sad mood keeps half its greyer cast on purpose.
- **No disc under her hooves.** The pale ellipse that showed under Rosie's feet when she was happy, surprised, or sitting in a room (and the stray specks around it) is gone. Copper, Pepper, Bandit, Pickles and Biscuit never had it.

## Internal

- Timing: `PIG_IDLE_FPS` 7.5 → 6; `PIG_ANIMATION_SPECS` jump/surprise 6 → 5, happy/wave/walk 4 → 3.2, sad/bounce 3 → 2.4, tired 2 → 1.6 (`components/ui/pigRendererContract.ts`). Per-family edits, not a multiplier, so `PIG_BARN_REST_TEMPO` (0.8) doesn't compound; `PIG_REST_FPS` (2.5) and the sit/face/face_sit loops untouched. Reaction hold in `PigStage` derives from `pigAnimationDurationMs`, so nothing else needed re-tuning.
- Art: 35 Rosie PNGs in `assets/images/sprites/rosie/` edited in place (no regeneration) by three new PIL passes in `scripts/pig-tweens/`:
  - `strip_ground_disc.py` — border flood-fill confined to the foot band; low-alpha pixels carry connectivity through the disc's ink fringe, only disc-coloured pixels are cleared. `happy_1–4`, `surprise_1–4`.
  - `strip_foot_specks.py` — detached islands under 30 px and pale pixels outside the solid silhouette, foot band only (tired's sweat drops and surprise's motion strokes are above it). Visible hits in happy, sad, surprise_2/4, walk_4, jump_4; invisible alpha dust swept from idle, wave, face, face_sit_1.
  - `normalize_body_pink.py` — one uniform CIELAB shift per frame on the pink band so its mean lands on the idle family's (L* 86.2 · a* 15.2 · b* 3.8); sad at half strength. `--check` reports per-family ΔE and exits 1 past ΔE 2 — run it after any new Rosie sheet. Rosie only; recruit coats need their own band/canonical.
- Audit of the pixel edits: every cleared pixel ≥ 2 px from the solid body (no outline anti-aliasing touched); alpha→0 only for the strips; the normalise pass changes colour only inside the pink band, outline/eyes/hooves/highlights untouched; canvas sizes unchanged, so `PIG_FRAME_ANCHORS` and every RelSpec still hold.
- Review surface: the Rosie Motion Sheet artifact (every family live at current vs proposed fps, colour normalisation preview) — the proposal is now what ships.
