## Headline

The Barn is Rosie's stage: one button that remembers what you want, an empty room that's yours to fill, and doors that open crisp.

## User-facing

- The Barn button remembers your quick action. Pick Dig, Barn, or Bury a truffle from its fan once; tapping the button does that thing until you pick another.
- The home is Rosie and two corners: tickles earned top-left, your bank and streak top-right, the Barn button bottom-right.
- New barns start empty. Nothing is placed for you — the room is yours to furnish.
- Rosie sits in her barn now, settling and blinking instead of standing stock-still.
- The barn doors swing crisp, and going in cuts straight through them instead of sliding.
- A buried truffle shows as a mound in the yard; tap it to see how many snouts are down there, tap the tag to check on it.
- Your Barn collection is a grid you can scan: sections by collection, "N of M owned", and one clear mark for what's in the room.
- Sheets end where their content ends — no more empty paper under the buttons.
- Digging runs on the player-local Feeding schedule with the server's clock; the Truffle Patch is a continuous mud surface; the Barn guestbook is retired.
