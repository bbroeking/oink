## QR redemption + limited cosmetics

- `/redeem/<code>` universal links route installed players into the claim
  screen; pre-auth codes survive sign-in and are submitted automatically.
- The production landing fallback is live at `ticklethepig.com`: uninstalled
  players see the App Store link, their code, and rescan/manual-entry guidance.
  The live AASA file now includes `/redeem/*`.
- Crown and Cap stay out of random hats and store acquisition. Supply is
  server-enforced at 10 owners, not merely hidden in the client.
- Admin minting can target any existing catalog item with
  `scripts/mint-redemption-codes.mjs --item <hat_id>`, list catalog IDs, and
  preview batches with `--dry-run`; existing items do not require a migration.
- Scanner success reveals the awarded item and refreshes the Closet.
