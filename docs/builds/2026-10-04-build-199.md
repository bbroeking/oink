## Headline

Every tab polished on device, from scroll edges to large text, plus five small games a pig can play with friends or a stranger, built and switched off.

## User-facing

- **Large text holds.** Fixed chrome states its ceiling instead of breaking mid-word: tab signs, the Friends nav, passport stats and the pig's name, profile stats, slot labels, and tile names. A row's Share pill moves under its label at accessibility sizes rather than squeezing it.
- **VoiceOver reaches what it couldn't**: the Barn's "More actions" fan, the invite row's Share, the Shop's remove buttons. Every sheet's close is a button named "Close". Tabs announce as tabs.
- **Headers own their edge.** A rule appears under a pinned header once content scrolls beneath it: Friends, Season, Shop, the Pen, sheets, and the Barn collection.
- **Barn**: the mound tag sits off Rosie, the item card stays out of her face, the earned stamp loads quietly, the collection gains clearer set progress, and Shop rarity colours match.
- **Friends**: the Inbox names rituals instead of printing ids, each list keeps its own scroll, Global shows your rank, and pairs mark "(you)".
- **Season**: the Slop Club ask is honest, guide and story cards keep their close pinned, the drum shows a countdown, and the dig's Tie / Dig deeper buttons never fall off an SE.
- **Shop**: anything the pig wears can be named and taken off, and the preview fits the item instead of cutting it.
- **Me**: the passport draws the whole pig, the invite row works with VoiceOver, the rank sheet uses one clock, and Settings sub-flows return to Settings.
- **Sign-in and the handle screen**: Rosie sizes to the phone, so the button and the legal line stay above the fold on an SE.
- **Signing out on one phone no longer stops another phone's notifications.**
- **Catalog copy** (needs its migration): title and achievement descriptions lose their em dashes, read "1,000", and stop naming the retired Dig-Off.
- **Dark (test accounts only after the push and deploy): matchmade play.** Five games are reached from a Play together door in Friends:
  - Rootle (push your luck)
  - Fence the Pen (close a pen, draw again)
  - Mud Volley (follow through or flick it)
  - Snout Says (one picture as a clue, nine to guess from)
  - Out Looking (alone you take the pile)

  Live or by post; random matchmaking or invites by friend, Sounder or link, with pushes; practice against labelled bot pigs. The only public number is wins this month.
