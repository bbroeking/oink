## Headline

Turn Rosie and what she wears turns with her: glasses, masks and hats have a real side now, and a button on Home lets you look.

## User-facing

- **A turn button on Home.** A round paper button under the tickle-bank pill cycles Rosie front → left → right, so you can see her outfit from the side. Rest pose only — a tickle still plays from the front, mirrored, like a visit. A well-tickled (happy) pig turns too; a tired or sad one keeps facing you. (`7d681e60`)
- **What she wears has a side.** On the turn, 50 items now swap to a three-quarter sprite instead of a front sticker pasted on a turned head: every pair of glasses and goggles (17), every mask (11), the monocle / crest / lorgnette, and 13 hats with a front (tricorn, messenger cap, plume hat, plume helm, aurora hood, rookie cap, feather cap, viking helmet, signet visor, muddy cap, ticket-taker's cap, paper boat, bog helmet), plus the original six (acorn bow, astronaut, aviators, cowboy, top hat, wizard). Glasses keep their bridge over the nose and their near lens on the near eye; masks wrap the snout; the venice beak points where the pig looks. Items without side art keep their front sprite, as before. (`fa960dd3`, `30a47b9d`, `73875e22`)
- **The digging story is gone.** The "Your digging story" row on your profile and the "Digging story" door on a friend's profile sheet are removed, with the stats page behind them. Digging itself is unchanged. (`7b09faf1`)
- **The Mud Shovel is clean.** A sliver of a second shovel that sat to the left of the held Mud Shovel is erased. (`b4c2f762`)
- Everything in builds 183–189 — see *Week in review* below.

## Internal

- **Side-sprite pipeline.** `tools/gen_side_items.py` repaints an item's front sprite at Rosie's `face_1` camera through the Codex ImageGen lane; output `assets/images/hats/side/<id>.png` (256 px, keyed, recentred), registry `constants/hat_side.generated.ts` (rebuild-all from the directory). The camera is stated from the pig's geometry — the near side of the face is the viewer's LEFT (the first six were prompted inverted; a brim survived it, lenses did not). Eye-anchored items get a lens / mask / one-eye addendum picked off the RelSpec anchor, reading both `hat_rel.generated.ts` and `membersRel.generated.ts`. (`fa960dd3`, `6f6fda2f`, `73875e22`)
- **Renderer.** `PigStage.resolveSlot` takes `HAT_SIDE_IMAGES[id]` on the `face` / `face_sit` families only; forced ritual images always win. An item on the `eyes` anchor of the turned families sits back `TURNED_EYE_SHIFT` (16 canvas px, `constants/hats.ts`) so its bridge lands over the nose — the midpoint of a near eye and a foreshortened far eye is not where a bridge goes; single-eye anchors take no shift. `resolveFacingAnimation` turns the happy rest as well as idle/sit. `slop_club_monocle_crest` now anchors `eye_l` with its pivot on the lens (`membersRel.generated.ts`).
