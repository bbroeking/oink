## Headline

Sounders can Oink preset notes, Feeding alerts persist across devices, and
Rosie can finally wear a Hat and Bow together.

## User-facing

- Send exact preset coordination notes to the Sounder; recipients see them in
  a next-active popup and retain them on the notification board.
- Leaders can attach the recruiting Oink to a real Sounder invite.
- Opt into every future Feeding-open push once, sync the setting across
  devices, and disable it from the same control.
- Equip Hat and Bow independently and render both across Barn, Closet, preview,
  visits, and roster surfaces.
- Barn totals stay exact and localized through six digits, then abbreviate from
  one million; the Me view remains exact.
- Sluggish Snout no longer refunds suppressed Tickles when cleansed, cancelled,
  or expired and preserves fractional regeneration progress.
- Watering Can Cap and Tiny Umbrella use their corrected anchors; Sparkle copy
  now says five-point star without changing the artwork.
