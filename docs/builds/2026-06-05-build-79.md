# Build 79 — 2026-06-05

## Headline

The **World Cup** build. Players can pick a country to back for the
tournament (locked once chosen), earning that country's flag + a soccer
background; the flag flies on the pig, the leaderboard, and the bottom-left
of the Barn. Plus this build finally ships the app-side UI for a season's
worth of backend work already live in prod: **alignment teeth**, **the
Trough** (friend-funded item drives), over-cap tickle grants, the curse
countdown, the Judgement Day countdown, and the battle-pass progress fix.

## User-facing

- **World Cup allegiance pick.** A one-time, skippable modal on launch invites
  you to back one of the 47 qualified countries. Choosing is locked for the
  tournament — pick the eventual champion and there may be a reward at the
  final whistle. On pick you get the country **flag** (auto-equipped) + the
  **Sunny Pitch** soccer background. "Maybe later" only hides it for the
  session; it re-offers each launch until you choose. Skippers also get a
  "Back your country" card in the Shop.
- **Equipped flag shows everywhere.** Your chosen flag renders on the pig (as
  a tilted corner sticker), as a chip on the leaderboard, and pinned to the
  bottom-left of the Barn page.
- **4 soccer backgrounds.** Sunny Pitch, Joga Bonito (street pelada), Champions'
  Podium, and Night Match. One is granted via the allegiance pick; the others
  are buyable (600 snouts).
- **Alignment has teeth now.** Generous vs. greedy isn't just a label: it scales
  your tickle regen and the strength/duration of blessings + curses, growing
  with your score. An explainer modal + a "Your effects" readout on the Account
  screen show exactly what your alignment is doing, in plain language.
- **The Trough — friend-funded item drives.** Open a Trough for any Shop item
  (you stake ≥10%), and your Sounder chips in the rest within 48h. If it fills,
  the item is yours; donors earn claimable over-cap tickles (2 snouts → 1
  tickle). Under-funded drives refund everyone. Lives in a Shop section.
- **Over-cap tickles.** Granted/rewarded tickles can now push you past the 25
  cap (normal regen still stops at the cap). Wasted-over-cap is tracked.
- **Curse countdown.** Cursing an already-cursed target now shows how long the
  existing curse has left instead of silently stacking.
- **Judgement Day countdown.** The Season 1 finale (Jul 15) now shows an in-app
  countdown banner, and finalize runs automatically via a scheduled job.
- **Battle-pass tier progress restored.** The XP-to-next-tier bar is back on the
  Season screen.
- **Shop fix — defaults no longer "for sale."** The default barn backdrop +
  other earned/free (cost-0) items no longer rotate into the daily store as
  buyable tiles. (This was the "Rosie is in the shop" bug.)

## Pod-install + build notes

`NODE_OPTIONS="--max-old-space-size=16384"` for the build;
`COCOAPODS_DISABLE_STATS=true` + clear Pods if CocoaPods null-byte-flakes.
Built via `eas build --local`; uploaded via Transporter, not `eas submit`.
