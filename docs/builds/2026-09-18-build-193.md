## Headline

The Pen is an errand board: send a pig — Rosie, or a Slop Club companion — to look for the Find a friend's pig is wishing for; four hours later it comes home with it or without it, and you give it to the friend or keep it.

## User-facing

- **The Pen, rebuilt.** A paddock with the pigs that are home, a fence row of six medallions (Rosie first; out pigs dashed and faded, a returned pig wears a sun dot, a resting or not-yet-recruited pig is dashed cream), and one card about the selected pig: its job (*At home · In the Pen · Out looking* — the first two are the old home toggle) and one action.
- **Send a pig to look for…** the picker, in order: friends' wishes (the friend's pig, what it hopes for, how long the wish has left, *you have it* when your bag already holds one), your own pig's wish, or *Anything*. Then the ticket: the find, who it is for, *who goes?* (every pig that may go; one already out is dashed *out*), *back by 7:40pm · one errand a day*, **Send {pig}**. The pig walks out on the tap; a refusal walks it back with a toast and the day is not spent.
- **Rosie goes too.** While the greeter is out, Home shows the mound and a sticker — *Rosie's out looking · back by 7:40pm · tap to visit the Pen* — and nothing to tickle (a tap wobbles the sticker). The Barn button's fan gains a *Rosie · out* row beside the Trader's; once she is back, a *Rosie · back · one on the board* row. A visitor to your barn sees the same sticker, can bless and wish, cannot tickle; the visit still counts.
- **The homecoming.** A *"{Pig}'s back"* push (never revealing empty hands — *Back from the hedge — come and see.*) opens the Pen; the return plays once on the reward-return stage: the pig walks in with the find on its snout, the card says *he found it · a blue feather · the one Maya's Pickles is hoping for* with **Give it to Maya** (gold) / **Keep it** (paper), the coins hop to the bag. Empty hands are *muddy trotters · Ok, Rosie*. A pig that got distracted comes back with something else you keep. Unclaimed returns wait on the **corkboard** under the fence row (three at most — past that the pig *rests until you look*); a tap on a pin replays the homecoming.
- **Give is the swap's gift.** Giving pays exactly what a hand-off pays (flat 3 to both, the daily paid caps, the ledger, the friend's while-away line). If the friend's wish moved while the pig was out, the find lands in your Satchel instead and the card says who beat him to it — never lost.
- **Call {him} home** — a decision dialog: *He'll come back now, empty-handed. Today's errand is spent either way.*
- **Friends row.** A wish you cannot fill from your bag gets a *send a pig* tag that opens the Pen's ticket with that wish preset; one a pig is already out for says *back ~7:40pm*.
- **Field Guide.** An eleventh page, *the Pen* — a silhouette until the first send; its hours come from the live tuning.
- **Satchel page.** Says *Holds every find you carry home* now that the bag is unbounded (was *Holds 9999 finds*).

## Decisions taken while building (the brief's open questions)

1. **Walk strips: shipped without.** The homecoming and the Paddock use the existing `walk` / `idle` families; the six walk-out/walk-in strips are a follow-up (§5.7 of the brief).
2. **Give-from-the-board opens the full homecoming** (the default), not a bare Give/Keep.
3. **The empty-hands push body** is *"Back from the hedge — come and see."*; a friend-targeted hit says *"She found the blue feather Maya's pig was hoping for."* A return claimed before the sweep is never announced (the player has already seen it).
4. `away` lives on the errand state (`pig_errands().away`, read through `usePigErrands`) rather than being folded into `usePigRoster` — one RPC, one hook, and the roster stays the roster.
5. The Friends row's door and the visit's sticker are gated on `errand_tuning.enabled` (or `is_test`) like the Pen's action; the visit's `host_pig_away` read is a tiny read RPC rather than a carried `barn_visit_status`.
6. A pig cannot change job while it is out (the segment's two live options disable); an out companion that is the active pig empties the yard, which is the rule.
7. The homecoming auto-opens once per `back` row per app session (a module-level set); after that the pin waits on the corkboard.
