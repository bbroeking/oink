# App Store screenshot plan — v1.3 (build 196)

Status: **seven owner-approved screenshots uploaded and saved to App Store Connect on 2026-10-03**. English (U.S.) is the sole saved localization; every smaller iPhone size inherits the new 6.9-inch set. The [final set and capture/upload record](../screenshots/v1.3/README.md) supersede the staging directions below where the actual captured state differs. Build 197 and the current post-196 working tree are out of scope.

## Source lock and delivery rules

- Capture from the exact TestFlight build **1.3 (196)** on a physical device, or from a frozen checkout of candidate commit `ecda223413e5edc9f203731dda1597ccee68e168` running in production mode solely for screenshot staging. A screen rendered from the current checkout is not evidence of build 196.
- Do not show developer controls, test labels, fixture data that reads as a developer tool, debug toasts, permission prompts, or personal information. Stage believable account data without changing the shipped UI.
- Deliver portrait PNG or JPEG with **no alpha**. Accepted 6.9-inch sizes: **1260 × 2736**, **1290 × 2796**, or **1320 × 2868**. Accepted 6.5-inch sizes: **1242 × 2688** or **1284 × 2778**. Prefer one consistent 6.9-inch set; do not upscale the existing 1206 × 2622 references.
- If captions are composited, preserve the full authentic game screen, use one short line per image, and flatten the final export onto an opaque background. Verify the exported pixel size and alpha channel before upload.
- Replace the stale screenshots in every supported App Store localization/size slot with this coherent set. App Store Connect permits 1–10 images; seven are planned here. Build 196 is iPhone-only, so no iPad set is needed.
- Do not show or mention hidden Errands, the Mote Machine, the Lounge, endless Wallow, the retired Race/Dig-Off, or the retired herd furnishing draw.

## Recommended seven-image sequence

The sequence starts with the character and home, expands into social play and the tactile Dig, then explains the weekly loop and collection payoff.

| # | Screen to stage in build 196 | Capture direction | Short caption |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Barn exterior with Rosie** | Rosie large and unobstructed, wearing one expressive cosmetic; catch hearts or a happy tickle reaction. Keep the current two-corner ledger and Barn button visible. | **Meet Rosie. Make her day.** |
| 2 | **Decorated Barn interior** | A warm, fully furnished room with Rosie centered and six slots reading clearly. Avoid edit/list overlays; this is the emotional home shot. | **Make your Barn feel like home.** |
| 3 | **Friend visit with Satchel** | Show both pigs facing each other, the host's wish bubble, and the Satchel strip with a matching find lifted or the swap tray open. Keep names non-personal. | **Visit friends. Swap little finds.** |
| 4 | **Living Mud Patch mid-Dig** | Capture the shipped tactile board during an active brush/tap/shove moment, with uncovered rewards and enough board state to make the hunt legible. | **Dig through mud. Find little surprises.** |
| 5 | **Monday Drum** | Use Season → Draw with a meaningful earned-ball count and printed odds. Prefer the reward reveal if it remains readable at thumbnail size; otherwise use the ready-to-draw panel. No `dev` chips. | **Every Dig counts. Monday brings rewards.** |
| 6 | **Season Pass** | Use Season → Pass with a claimable tier and a mix of Barn and wearable rewards visible. No developer state controls. | **Fill your Pass with cozy rewards.** |
| 7 | **Closet / cosmetics** | Show the owned cosmetic grid with a selected outfit item. The final capture shows hats and an Acorn Bow, with Paper Boat selected; Rosie's worn glasses and bandana are visible in the opening shots. | **Dress Rosie your way.** |

If App Store Connect needs only six images, combine the final two ideas by using the Pass screen with a strong wearable reward and omit the Closet shot. Keep the social/Satchel and Living Mud shots: they distinguish v1.3 from the older listing.
