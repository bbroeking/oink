## Saved store copy

### What's New

Build a Barn that feels like yours, then invite friends over to see it. Sounders now support up to eight pigs, and the Living Mud Patch makes every Dig a tactile hunt for hidden rewards.

Finds travel with you in the Satchel: discover them while digging, follow your friends' wishes, and swap or give finds during visits.

The Season page is now three simple verbs: Feed, Draw, Pass. Every submitted Dig adds a ball to your own Monday Drum, and when it turns you keep the best prize from your rolls.

This update also adds player-local Feeding schedules and fixes across Barns, visits, notifications, and shops.

### Description

Tickle the Pig is a cosy social pet game about Rosie, your friends, and the little rituals that make a shared world feel alive.

Tickle Rosie, care for her mood, earn Snouts, and dress her in hats, glasses, bows, auras, and other keepsakes. Decorate your own Barn, save room arrangements, and visit friends to see the spaces they have made.

Join a Sounder of up to eight pigs. Dig during player-local Feedings, explore the Living Mud Patch, collect finds in your Satchel, and swap or give the find another pig is wishing for.

The Season page keeps the week clear: Feed, Draw, Pass. Each submitted Dig earns one ball for your own Monday Drum, up to one per Feeding. When the Drum turns, every ball gives you a roll and you keep the best in-game purse.

Progress through the season pass, complete achievements, build your collection, and make a Barn that feels like home.

Tickle the Pig supports Sign in with Apple, optional notifications, Reduce Motion, and optional in-app purchases.

## Saved App Review notes

Use email/password sign-in with the credentials provided in the Sign-In Information fields. On the welcome screen, tap 'or use email' beneath Sign in with Apple. The populated demo account shows the existing social and gameplay state; Apple Sign In creates a new account.

With the populated review account:

1. On **Barn**, tap Rosie, then tap **Inside** to view and edit the furnished room.
2. On **Friends**, visit a friend's Barn. Sounders support up to eight members. Inspect the pig's wish and use the Satchel strip to swap or give a matching find when available.
3. On **Season**, open **Feed** for the player-local clock, **Draw** for Monday's Drum and earned balls, and **Pass** for progression.
4. On **Shop**, review cosmetic and Barn furnishing purchases using Snouts.
5. On **Me**, review achievements, Wallow rank, settings, and account controls. Test Slop Club membership and Restore purchases using Apple's sandbox.

The Monday Drum uses only balls earned through free gameplay. It has no paid entry, stake, player-to-player wager, or real-money redemption. The server rolls once per earned ball and grants the best in-game purse.

Rewarded ads are disabled for this release. The optional AdMob feature is server-gated and does not request or display ads in the verified production configuration. The Mote Machine and Lounge are compile-time hidden. Pig Errands are separately server-gated and may be unavailable.

Sign in with Apple and email/password are supported. Notification permission is optional. Camera permission scans Golden Ticket QR codes; microphone audio is not recorded. Privacy and support information are available through the app's settings.
