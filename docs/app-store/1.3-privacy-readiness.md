## Proposed public privacy-policy correction

Replace the current categorical “no advertising SDKs” claim and accurately describe the dormant dependency without claiming it currently requests advertising data. Exact proposed copy for the present globally-disabled v1.3 state:

> **Advertising.** Tickle the Pig includes a dormant Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) integration for a possible future optional rewarded-ad feature. That feature is disabled globally in the current service: the app does not offer or request ads through it while the server gate remains off. If we enable it in a future release or service update, we will update this policy and the App Store disclosures before activation to describe the information Google and we process for ad delivery, consent, measurement, security, and reward verification.
>
> We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, or microphone audio. We store account and gameplay data needed to operate the game, and use first-party, account-linked gameplay analytics to understand feature use and game health. We use Sentry for crash and performance diagnostics and RevenueCat for purchase validation and subscription analytics. If you allow push notifications, we store a device-linked push token and use Expo and Apple to deliver notification content.
>
> Purchase and entitlement information is processed when you buy or restore an in-app purchase. Push-token and notification delivery information is processed when you opt into notifications. We do not sell personal data.
>
> **Google AdMob** — an embedded but globally disabled integration reserved for a possible future optional rewarded-ad feature. No ad request is made through this feature while the server gate remains off. [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Before publishing, verify that the gate is still false and reconcile the rest of the policy with the confirmed account, gameplay, analytics, Sentry, RevenueCat, purchase, and push flows.
