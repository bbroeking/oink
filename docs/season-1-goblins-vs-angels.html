Season 1: Goblins vs Angels

One alignment continuum, derived from action. No teams. Your behavior names you.

Length: 8 weeks Hook: Tickle Trade behavior New systems: 3 Cosmetic rewards: 8 (4 per side) Finale exclusive: Top 3 each side

The big idea in one paragraph

Every player has an alignment score (-100 to +100), derived purely from how they trade. Here's the twist: asking is the profitable move — when a friend fulfills your request you pocket double, and they give it from their own bank for nothing but a promise. Fulfilling requests + blessing pulls you toward Angel; asking-and-pocketing-double + cursing pulls you toward Goblin. Greed is mechanically rewarded; alignment and reputation are the only counterweight. You don't pick a side — your side picks you, live, every week. Cosmetics unlock at alignment milestones and you can only equip the ones matching your current state. After 8 weeks, the top 3 angels and top 3 goblins get exclusive finale titles and the slate wipes for Season 2.

The one continuum

Goblin King Hoarder Pilgrim Saint Halo Bearer

Three derived states, descriptive (not teams):

  • Generous (>+34) — you give more than you take, you close your loops → angel-coded cosmetics unlock
  • Neutral / Pilgrim (-33 to +33) — balanced or new player
  • Greedy (<-34) — you take more than you give, you hoard debts → goblin-coded cosmetics unlock
Live with your reputation. Alignment drifts in real time. You keep every cosmetic you've unlocked forever, but you can only equip ones matching your current state. A former-saint-now-goblin has a closet of halos they can't wear until they redeem themselves — you can't fake it.

Daily rituals: Blessings & Curses

☀️ Blessings (Angel-coded)

One per day, cast on up to 3 friends. Rotates daily.

  • Warm Tea (Mon): 2× tickle regen for 1h
  • Sun Beam (Tue): next Lucky Pig doubles
  • Halo Kiss (Wed): faint halo glow visible for 6h
  • Bountiful Snouts (Thu): +5 snouts instantly
  • Fri–Sun: rotation continues

"alice blessed you with Warm Tea ☕" — creates lore.

🟢 Curses (Goblin-coded)

One per day, cast on up to 3 friends. Rotates daily.

  • Sluggish Snout (Mon): 0.5× regen for 1h
  • Phantom Itch (Tue): next 3 taps register as half-tickles
  • Goblin Whisper (Wed): green miasma cloud over pig for 4h
  • Coin Pinch (Thu): −3 snouts

Anti-grief: max −10 snouts & 2h regen debuff per day across all incoming curses. Cleanse for 5 snouts. Blessing while cursed cancels both.

Engagement loops

  • Daily (~3 min): check in → cast 1 blessing + 1 curse → do 1-2 trades → glance leaderboard.
  • Weekly (~15 min): complete bounty board → check your alignment trend (sparkline of last 7 days).
  • Seasonal (8 weeks): drift toward a side → unlock cosmetics → battle for top 3 spot → finale verdict.

Open questions

  1. Blessing/curse rate limit: once per friend per day, capped at 3 total casts per day? (Recommend yes.)
  2. New-user starting alignment: 0 or +5 nudge so first received-trade doesn't immediately register as goblin? (Recommend +5.)
  3. Inactivity decay: after 7 days inactive, alignment drifts −1/day toward 0 to stop "lock and leave"? (Recommend yes.)
