# Tickle the Pig — World Cup 2026 Country Cosmetics

This is the country-representation content list for the standalone **World Cup 2026 add-in**, which lets players dress their pig to represent any country on Earth. Every nation here gets a **flag cosmetic by default** — so the flag is always implied and is never listed below. Instead, each country gets ONE **signature item** (a cozy, warmly-recognizable accessory beyond the flag) and an earnable **title**.

The list covers all FIFA member nations, organized by the six confederations in order: UEFA, CONMEBOL, CONCACAF, CAF, AFC, OFC. Signature items lean on beloved positive icons — cuisine, friendly fauna, landmark motifs, traditional dress, and football heritage — kept gentle, whimsical, and tasteful in TTP's storybook voice.

---

## ⛔ Excluded — sanctions denylist (do NOT ship)

Removed from the shippable catalog by product decision: we do **not** offer
cosmetics — **flag, signature item, or title** — for states on the US
sanctions / banned list. The rows below were pulled from the confederation
tables.

- Iran
- Russia *(also FIFA-suspended)*
- North Korea
- Syria
- Cuba
- Belarus
- Venezuela

Notes:
- "US sanctioned/banned list" is **not a single fixed list and it changes** —
  this is a conservative set covering comprehensively-sanctioned states plus
  the two explicitly called out (Iran, Russia). **Legal should confirm the
  exact set before launch**; it's a one-line edit to adjust.
- **Enforce in code, not just by omission:** ship a `WORLD_CUP_DENYLIST`
  constant so even if an asset ever exists, the cosmetic **cannot be
  equipped** — a backstop against accidental seeding.

**Other exclusion (product decision, not sanctions):** **Palestine** is held
out; **Israel** is retained. Qualification aside (Palestine was eliminated in
AFC qualifying; Israel was a long-shot in UEFA and hasn't reached a World Cup
since 1970), the catalog covers all countries regardless of who qualified —
so this is a deliberate product call.
