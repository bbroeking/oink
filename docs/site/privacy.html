Tickle the Pig — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-05-04

This is the privacy policy for Tickle the Pig (the "App"), operated by Brian Broeking ("we", "us"). We tried to keep this short and plain — no dark patterns.

What we collect

  • Account info: the username you choose, and the Apple Sign-In identifier we get when you log in. We do not see your real name or email if you used Apple's Hide My Email feature.
  • Gameplay data: your tickle count, owned hats, equipped hat, friends list, season pass progress, daily challenge progress.
  • Purchase data: if you buy something via Apple In-App Purchase, Apple tells us which product you bought and whether your subscription is active. We do not see your credit card or Apple ID password — Apple handles that.
  • Device info: crash and performance logs from the Expo / React Native runtime. No advertising identifiers, no location, no contacts.

We do not collect: location, contacts, photos, microphone audio, advertising IDs, or any analytics beyond crash logs.

How we use it

  • Run the game (show your stats, leaderboards, friends).
  • Validate purchases and entitlements (RevenueCat + Apple).
  • Make the app not crash (crash logs).

Who we share it with

We use a few third parties whose privacy policies apply alongside this one:

  • Supabase — database hosting. supabase.com/privacy
  • Apple App Store / TestFlight — distribution + IAP. apple.com/legal/privacy
  • RevenueCat — subscription validation. revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Expo / EAS — app build infrastructure (no user data). expo.dev/privacy

We do not sell your data, ever.

Your rights

  • See your data: ask us, we'll send you a JSON dump of everything.
  • Delete your account: ask us, we delete your row plus related rows (friendships, season progress, owned hats).
  • Cancel a subscription: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Tickle the Pig → Cancel. We don't gate this.

Children

We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The App is rated 4+ for content (cartoon pig) but is not directed at children under 13.

Subscription auto-renewal

If you subscribe to Snout VIP, Apple will auto-renew at the end of each term unless you cancel at least 24 hours before. You can manage or cancel in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Changes to this policy

We'll update this page if anything material changes. The effective date at the top will reflect the latest version.

Contact

Questions: brian@broeking.dev (or whichever address Apple has on file for the developer account)
