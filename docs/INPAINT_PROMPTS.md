the prompt verbatim into the Editor's prompt input, mask the indicated area,
submit, pick the best variant, save as `{id}.png` into
`assets/images/hats/`.

## Workflow tips

- **Same mask, batch the category**: do all 10 hats in one Editor session — same mask region every time, just the prompt changes.
- **Always pick upper-left** unless it's clearly worse than another variant.
- **Re-roll if you get extras** (e.g., MJ adds a flower next to a hat) — easier than masking out artifacts later.
- **Save filenames consistently**: `assets/images/hats/{id}.png` (no underscores in path, just the id).
- **After every 10 items**, append the new ids to `HAT_IMAGES` in `constants/hats.ts:2-8`. Reload to verify they show up in the shop / barn overlay.
