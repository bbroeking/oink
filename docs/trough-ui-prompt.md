# The Trough — UI design prompt (for claude.ai/design)

Prompts for the **Trough** (friend-funded item drive) screens, in Tickle the
Pig's cozy paper-sticker style. Functional wiring is built separately; this is
for the *look*. Tokens: warm cream paper `#fffaf0`, ink-brown linework
`#2a1f15`, pastel accents (rose, sky, sage, sun, lilac), paper-sticker cards
with a 2–3px ink border + a **hard drop shadow** (solid, offset, no blur),
chunky rounded headings (Fredoka/Caprasimo feel), hand-written sub-labels.

Anchor metaphor: a literal cozy **feeding trough** — friends toss snouts in to
help a pal afford a coveted item. Warm, communal, barn-raising — never a
gambling/wager vibe.

---

## 1. Trough card (the core unit — lives in a Shop "Troughs" section)

> A cozy paper-sticker card for a group item-drive. Top: the coveted item
> (e.g. a hat or flag sticker) sitting in/over a little wooden **trough**, with
> the opener's round cartoon pig beside it and their name ("briguy's Trough").
> Middle: a chunky **funding progress bar shaped like a filling trough** —
> snouts piling up — labelled "320 / 500 snouts". A small **countdown** chip
> ("18h left") in a corner. Bottom: a big friendly **"Chip in"** button (sun-
> yellow, ink border, hard shadow). Whimsical, warm, readable. Show three
> states: lots-left, almost-full, and a celebratory **"Funded!"** state with
> confetti where the button becomes a soft "Filled" tag.

## 2. Donate sheet (tap "Chip in")

> A bottom sheet, cozy paper style. Header: "Chip in to briguy's Trough" with
> the item + trough. A **snout amount stepper** (− / number / +) capped at the
> remaining gap, a coin pill showing the cost, and a friendly line: "You'll get
> **N tickles** for helping" (with a tickle-heart icon). Big "Give N snouts"
> confirm button + a quiet cancel. Reassuring, generous tone.

## 3. Open-a-Trough sheet (from an item's preview)

> A bottom sheet to start a drive for the previewed item. Shows the item in a
> trough, its price, and a **"your stake" stepper** (minimum = 10% of price,
> highlighted) with a line: "You put up at least 10%; your Sounder funds the
> rest. When it fills, it's yours." Confirm: "Open the Trough (pay X snouts)".

## 4. Claimable reward (small, celebratory)

> A little sticker chip/toast: "You helped land the Golden Snout — claim **3
> tickles** 🐷" with a tap-to-claim button. Warm, rewarding.

## Guardrails
- Cozy storybook paper-sticker — match the existing game, never a finance/
  betting aesthetic.
- The progress bar should read as a *trough filling with snouts*, not a loan
  bar. Lean into the barn-raising warmth.
- No casino/odds/wager language anywhere — it's giving, not gambling.
