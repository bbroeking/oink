# The Great Hunger — server-wide shared enemy

The founder's seed: *"a shared enemy for everyone to work together to beat — e.g. slower speed until X,Y,Z happen, or stealing snouts."* Five divergent forms were designed and pressure-tested on four axes (collective-punishment / economy / cozy-tone / clash-with-existing-systems); this is the synthesized recommendation.

## Recommendation: build **"The Great Hunger"** — form A (community HP raid) as the *theme*, sitting on the D+E chassis (phase-less siege shell + world_events spine), with the debuff **per-user gated to the flag** so it can never confuse an unflagged pig.

### Why this weave, grounded in code

**The two levers, centered.** The founder named two seed levers; the code says exactly how to use each:
- **The SLOW** is a one-line graft. `regen_secs_for(uid)` (migration `20260679`) is already a chain of multiplicative CASE terms — `warm_tea ×0.5`, `sluggish_snout ×2`, alignment `±10%`, happiness, `war_winner_regen ×0.85`. Appending `* world_boss_blight_mult(uid)` is precedented and server-authoritative. The blight at full HP is **×1.12** and eases *linearly to ×1.00 as the community lands hits* — the community literally lifts the fog off its own pigs. That "the debuff is a receding weather front, not a static penalty" property is what makes it read as adventure, not punishment. It is also *milder than a single `sluggish_snout` curse* (×2), so it is defensibly the gentlest slow in the game.
- **The STEAL** is the trap. `snouts = profiles.counter`; raiding it reads as loss/shame — the exact anti-pattern the brief forbids. **So I do NOT steal from wallets.** I keep the founder's "snout thief" *fantasy* but reframe it as **reclaim, not raid**: the Goblin King is sitting on a mound of *lost/house-funded* snouts (form C's insight), and coaxing it open *pays snouts OUT* to helpers. That converts a scary steal into a gift-unwrapping. But I make it **optional/off-by-default** (see open decisions) because `grant_tickles` — the existing reward chokepoint — writes `user_items.item_count` (tickle-charges), NOT `counter`. The mildest, already-safe boon path grants charges, not spendable snouts. **Primary boon = charges + a regen buff; the snout hoard is a second, flag-gated garnish.**

**Solving the collective-punishment trap (the central mandate).** Both the Goblin-King and Siege verdicts converged on the *same* residual leak, and it's real in this codebase: `regen_secs_for` feeds `home_stats().regen_seconds` (migration `20260643`), which the Barn renders as the live "Next tickle" countdown. If the blight is global but its explanation (the raid card) is `useFeatureFlag("world_boss")`-gated, an unflagged or brand-new player watches their timer silently slide ~60→~67 min **with no on-screen reason** — "weather you can't see isn't cozy, it's a bug report." **The fix is decisive and cheap: gate the blight on the caller's EFFECTIVE flag.** `world_boss_blight_mult(uid)` returns `1.0` unless the *caller's* resolved `world_boss` flag is true. During dark-launch only Brian is flagged, so only Brian sees both the slowdown AND the card — they appear and disappear together, per user. At global launch, the card's flag gate drops and the blight goes truly global with full context on Home. Nobody ever eats an unexplained slowdown. This closes the one seam the "shared weather" story couldn't.

**Four more anti-punishment guarantees, all baked into the spine so no future theme can break them:** (1) magnitude is tiny (+12% max, self-easing); (2) there is **no failure state** — on timeout the King "wanders off full and happy," participants still get a consolation boon, the cozy loop was never touched; (3) reward is a **BOON you WIN** (charges + a `full_belly_regen ×0.90` blessing, structurally a clone of the shipped `war_winner_regen`), framed as sunshine breaking through, never "the penalty stops"; (4) **headcount, not leaderboard** — flat identical reward for anyone who did ≥1 action, no top-damager, no shame.

**Anti-abuse rubric — passes.** Async/local-time (per-UTC-day caps, like the existing dig/blessing caps). Binary floor: ≥1 qualifying action → a `world_boss_hits` row → full reward. Flat capped ceiling: `DAILY_DMG_CAP=10`, so a whale contributes ≤~100 over a 10-day raid and *cannot solo* a boss sized in the tens of thousands, while a one-tickle lurker still scores. Co-presence: the community HP bar from `world_event_state()`. Name-attributed artifact: a season-stamped title ("Hunger-Shooer, Summer '26"), granted through the earned-only titles path (`20260677`). Gift-framing throughout.

**Two verdict-driven trims I'm adopting:** (a) **drop `send_curse` as a contribution verb.** "Curse a friend to hurt the shared boss" is the one discordant sentence in a cozy pig game and invites cursing-as-chore; the other five verbs (tickle, visit, blessing, dig, bury) cover every alignment. This also keeps the boss **alignment-agnostic** — `world_boss_strike` must NOT call `shift_alignment`; the King is everyone's problem, so it never contradicts the Goblins-vs-Angels axis. (b) Keep the blight tuned gentle at **+12%** (the founder's example figure; I considered +8% but +12% ≈ 7 min on an hour is still barely-noticeable and gives the fog visible teeth to lift).

**Why A over the pure-shell/spine forms:** the founder wants a *shared enemy the playerbase beats*. A community **HP bar going down** is the most legible "we are winning together" instrument — you see the King shrink. The Blight (B) is softer but the "we lifted something real" payoff is thinner; the pure Siege phases (D) add a phase state machine that's more surface area than a Brian-first MVP needs. So: **A's HP-raid fantasy and legibility, on E's spine (one state table, one boot-read, one striker, the anti-punishment rules in the plumbing) and D's live-ops lifecycle (`sweep` cron + seasonal cadence).** That's the cheapest thing that satisfies all constraints, reuses the done goblin art, the regen lever, and the `20260692` flag infra verbatim.

## Recommended design (tight spec)

**Name:** The Great Hunger — a server-wide community HP raid (a hungry Goblin King, reusing the done Goblin Horde sprite scaled up).

**Loop:** SPAWN (pg_cron `sweep_world_boss` every 15 min, mirroring `sweep_mud_wars`) inserts one `world_bosses` row when `app_config('world_boss')` is on and none is active → community chips HP via normal cozy actions → HP bar + receding fog rendered on Home → WIN (HP→0) or DEPART (timeout) → boon fan-out → quiet until next season.

**The SLOW (blight):** append ONE term to `regen_secs_for` (carry the full `20260679` body verbatim, drop nothing): `* public.world_boss_blight_mult(caller_uid)`. The helper returns `1 + 0.12 * (hp/hp_max)` for the single active boss **only when the caller's EFFECTIVE `world_boss` flag is true** (per-user gate — reuses the exact `feature_flags()` COALESCE(override, global) logic), else `1.0`. So at full HP a flagged pig is ×1.12 (~+7 min on 3600s), easing to ×1.00 as the community wins; an unflagged/new pig is a literal 1.0 no-op and sees no unexplained slowdown. Milder than one `sluggish_snout` curse (×2). Symmetric, global, self-easing.

**The STEAL (reframed as reclaim, flag-gated garnish):** the King hoards LOST/house-funded snouts (`hoard_total = LEAST(active_players × 12, 60000)`, minted once into the boss row at spawn, NEVER scraped from `profiles.counter`). On WIN, IF the `world_boss_hoard` sub-flag is on, the reclaimed snouts redistribute OUT to participants, flat-capped (~300 each, ceiling ~320). Ships OFF by default — the base event is snout-free.

**Contribution — five verbs (curse dropped):** `tickle_at_barn`(2), `dig_truffle`(2), `bury_truffle`(3), `send_blessing`(3), tickle-your-pig(1). Each gets ONE guarded line at its tail: `BEGIN PERFORM public.world_boss_strike(caller_id, '<verb>'); EXCEPTION WHEN OTHERS THEN NULL; END;` (side-effect can never roll back a core action). `world_boss_strike` is idempotent-per-verb-per-UTC-day, alignment-agnostic (NO `shift_alignment`), and clamps cumulative daily damage to `DAILY_DMG_CAP = 10`.

**Contribution cap + floor:** BINARY FLOOR — ≥1 qualifying action → a `world_boss_hits(boss,user,day)` row → full reward. FLAT CAP — ≤10 dmg/player/UTC-day, ≤~100 over a 10-day raid; `hp_max` sized at `active_players × DAILY_DMG_CAP × raid_days × target_participation` (~100k for 2000 actives) so thousands of chip-ins fell it and no whale solos. Reward is FLAT — identical for a 1-action and a maxed player. No leaderboard.

**Reward:** every participant gets `grant_tickles(uid, 30)` (charges, capped, NOT the counter wallet) + a `full_belly_regen` blessing (×0.90 regen, 48h — clone of `war_winner_regen`) + the season-stamped "Hunger-Shooer" title (earned-only path, `20260677`), delivered via INLINE per-participant `system_announcements` INSERTs (never `send_system_announcement` — admin-gated rollback footgun). Timeout → a smaller consolation boon + "he wandered off full and happy."

**Cadence:** seasonal live-ops, ~10-day window, one boss at a time (partial unique index `WHERE status='active'`). Between raids: no active boss → blight reads 1.0 → core loop fully normal.

## Fit with the existing game

SERVES COOPERATE at global scale — the always-on, everyone-included layer (solo cozy players and non-warring friends) that the consolidated brief flagged as whitespace between Mud Wars seasons.

COEXISTS WITH MUD WARS (separate axis, zero shared state): Mud Wars is crew-VS-crew competitive PvP with its own flag/tables/cron/regen buff and its own snout mint (resolve_war). The Great Hunger is everyone-WITH-everyone PvE co-op: separate `world_bosses`/`world_boss_hits` tables, its own `world_boss` flag, its own `full_belly_regen` blessing kind, its own `sweep_world_boss` cron. They share no tables, no currency mint, no alignment axis. Both regen buffs (war_winner ×0.85, full_belly ×0.90) are multiplicative and compose without conflict. OPEN: whether sweep refuses to spawn during an active Mud War (one-line EXISTS guard) — a +12% fog atop a war is tolerable and symmetric, but pick a side explicitly (I recommend allowing concurrency; audiences differ).

ALIGNMENT (Goblins vs Angels = greedy↔generous): the King is on-lore for the greedy-goblin end AND on-art (the done Goblin Horde set, zero new art), but the mechanic is deliberately alignment-AGNOSTIC — world_boss_strike never calls shift_alignment, and both Goblins and Angels fight the same shared threat. So it never contradicts or gets weaponized by the alignment/curse system. Dropping send_curse as a contribution verb reinforces this (no greedy-side privilege).

ECONOMY (cozy core, earned-not-bought): the base event NEVER touches profiles.counter — grant_tickles writes item_count (charges), the boon is a time-boxed blessing, the title is earned-only. So the base ships trivially economy-safe: one small, capped, seasonal, participation-gated faucet per player, in the same grant-lane as Trough/World-Cup grants — nothing per-action, nothing scaling with effort, no transfer to farm. The optional snout-hoard garnish, if ever enabled, is house-funded (minted once at spawn, capped, Sybil-diluting since more accounts only shrink each share) — a bounded pro-economy pressure valve for lower-wallet players, never a wallet raid.

REUSES (cheap high-impact): the done goblin art (scaled boss pose), the regen lever (one appended term), the 20260692 app_config + feature_flags + per-user-override infra verbatim, the sweep_mud_wars cron pattern, grant_tickles as the reward chokepoint, the war_winner_regen blessing shape, the earned-titles path (20260677), and the inline-announcement + guarded-side-effect precedents.


## Open decisions (with recommendation)

1. **Ship the optional snout-hoard 'reclaim' garnish at all, or launch boon-only (charges + regen buff + title) with the hoard sub-flag permanently off?**  
   → Launch BOON-ONLY. The base is trivially economy-safe (no counter touch) and the founder's 'steal' fantasy is honored by the reclaim FRAMING without any wallet risk. Gate the hoard behind its own `world_boss_hoard` sub-flag and only enable it AFTER a DAU + snout-velocity read confirms the ~300/head seasonal faucet stays bounded against sinks (buy_hat/Trough). Never a raid on personal wallets.

2. **hp_max sizing — what active-player count + participation rate to calibrate against?**  
   → Do NOT hardcode for global. Auto-size in sweep_world_boss: hp_max = clamp(active_players_7d × DAILY_DMG_CAP × raid_days × target_participation, floor, ceil). Dark-launch Brian-first with dev_spawn to feel the pacing, read real DAU, then set the target_participation constant (~0.5) before the global flip. The 'departed' timeout is the safety valve so it always ends cleanly even if mis-sized.

3. **Blight magnitude — +12% (founder's figure) or gentler +8%?**  
   → Keep +12% (≈7 min on an hour). It's still barely-noticeable and milder than one curse (×2), but gives the fog visible teeth to lift as the community wins — the '+8%' version risks the 'we lifted something real' payoff feeling too thin. Revisit only if dark-launch playtest reads it as friction.

4. **Does a raid coexist with an active Mud War, or does sweep refuse to spawn during one?**  
   → ALLOW concurrency. The fog is mild, global, and symmetric, and the audiences differ (solo/cozy vs crew-competitive). A 'no boss during a war' guard is a one-line EXISTS check if a later playtest shows double-slow fatigue — but ship allowing it and pick the side explicitly rather than leaving it open.

5. **Keep send_curse out of the contribution verbs permanently?**  
   → YES, permanently. 'Curse a friend to damage the shared boss' is the one discordant sentence in a cozy pig game and invites cursing-as-chore; the other five verbs cover every alignment. Keeping it out also keeps the boss cleanly alignment-agnostic (no greedy-side privilege).

6. **Blight-vs-explanation gating during dark-launch vs at public launch.**  
   → During dark-launch: gate world_boss_blight_mult on the caller's EFFECTIVE per-user flag, so only Brian sees BOTH the slowdown and the raid card (they appear/disappear together — no unexplained countdown for anyone else). At public launch: keep the per-user gate OR drop the card's flag gate so the blight and its Home-screen campfire explanation go global together. Never let the +12% land on a pig without on-screen context.

7. **Cosmetic keepsake — reuse an existing Goblin-set sprite as the season charm, or author a tiny new item?**  
   → Ship TITLE-ONLY day one (zero art, 'Hunger-Shooer, Season N', season-stamped, earned-only). Reuse a Goblin-set sprite (a truffle-crumb / napkin charm) as the cosmetic later if the season warrants it — keeps the MVP zero-new-art.

## Appendix — the 5 forms explored

### [revise] The Goblin King — a server-wide community HP raid ("The Great Hunger stirs") · punish=low · econ=none · cozy_ok
*The whole barnyard vs one hungry goblin king: every daily chore lands a hit, the fog of his hunger lifts as his HP drops, and everyone who helped once shares the feast.*  
- **Fantasy:** A giant, comedically ravenous Goblin King (reuse the DONE Goblin Horde sprite set, scaled up — the "Great Hunger" who ate all the truffles) has waddled onto the server and is sitting on the community's mud. He is not scary — he is a charming glutton with a napkin tucked in. His hunger casts a light, hopeful "peckish fog" over the land that makes everyone's pigs a touch sleepier (slower tickle regen). This is framed as weather you push back together, not a curse laid on you: "The barnyard is chipping in to shoo the Goblin King home." As the community lands hits, the fog visibly thins and the pigs perk up — you FEEL the collective effort in your own core loop. When he's finally full-and-shooed before the deadline, he leaves behind a feast: everyone who helped once gets a warm boon and a keepsake with the season's name on it. Solo/cozy players who ignore it entirely lose nothing meaningful — the fog is mild and self-easing.
- **Loop:** SPAWN: pg_cron (a new sweep_world_boss(), upserted by cron.schedule like sweep_mud_wars) seasonally inserts one row into a singleton-ish world_bosses table (status='active', hp=hp_max, spawned_at, ends_at = spawned_at + 10 days). Gated: sweep only spawns when app_config.enabled for 'world_boss' is true, so it dark-launches. CONTRIBUTION: existing action RPCs (tickle_at_barn, dig_truffle, bury_truffle, send_blessing, visit_barn/barn taps, send_curse) each get ONE added line — PERFORM public.world_boss_strike(caller_id, <verb>) — inside their already-SECURITY-DEFINER bodies (same seam the truffle announcement INSERT lives in). world_boss_strike is idempotent-per-verb-per-UTC-day and CAPPED: it records the player's participation, decrements the boss's shared hp by a flat per-verb amount, and stops counting once that player hits the daily damage cap. SHARED PROGRESS: hp / hp_max is the community HP bar, read at boot/home via a new world_event_state() RPC (alongside home_stats + feature_flags). The boss's aura is a global regen slow that EASES with hp: regen_secs_for gains one term, mult = 1 + BLIGHT_MAX * (hp / hp_max), so at full HP it's a mild ~+12% slow and at low HP it fades to ~0 — the community literally lifts the fog off its own pigs as it wins. TIMEOUT: if ends_at passes with hp>0, status='departed' — the Goblin King "gets full and wanders off on his own." NO punishment: the fog simply lifts (aura term reads 0 for non-active bosses), participants still get a smaller consolation boon and a "we tried, and he left happy" announcement. The core loop is untouched either way.
- **Slow/steal:** SLOW only — no steal (snouts are never touched, so there is zero collective-punishment-via-wallet risk). Concretely: regen_secs_for(uid) gets ONE new multiplier term appended to the existing chain (carry-latest-def discipline — copy the whole live body from 20260679, add the term, drop nothing). The term: * public.world_boss_blight_mult() where world_boss_blight_mult() returns 1 + 0.12 * (hp::numeric/hp_max) for the single active boss, else 1.0 (STABLE, reads the world_bosses singleton). At full HP that's x1.12 (~+12% regen time — one extra ~7 min on a 3600s base, gentle and shared); as hp falls it eases linearly to x1.00. ANTI-PUNISHMENT: (a) it is MILD and capped at +12% — never the x2 that sluggish_snout imposes; (b) it EASES as the community wins, so it is never a static "you are being penalized" state — it is a visibly-receding weather front; (c) it is global and symmetric (every pig, including the Goblin King's fans), so it reads as environment, not blame; (d) framed as "peckish fog" the barnyard is clearing together, with a boon at the end — you WIN the pigs perking up, you are not merely un-penalized. New/offline players who did nothing wrong feel at most a slightly sleepier pig that gets better on its own.
- **Reward:** COMMUNITY WINS: (1) a temporary server-wide BOON — a "Feast blessing" that gives every participant grant_tickles(uid, 30) over-cap-safe PLUS a 48h warm_tea-style regen buff granted as a blessing row (reuse the existing blessings faucet path, capped, non-minting of snouts) — the pigs are well-fed and perky after the Hunger leaves. No leaderboard-style "top damager gets more" (that would recreate whale dominance and shame). The season number stamps the artifact so it's a dated keepsake ("I was there when we shooed the Great Hunger, Summer '26").
- **⚑ Skeptic:** Strongest objection: the debuff is genuinely defused on 3 of 4 fronts (economy verified none — grant_tickles writes item_count/charges capped at 25/50, NOT the counter wallet, so it's the mildest faucet and there is no steal/transfer to farm; regen_secs_for confirms blight is +12% max vs sluggish_snout's static x2, symmetric, self-easing to exactly 1.0 for any non-active boss; binary-floor/capped-ceiling and alignment-agnostic-no-shift_alignment both hold). ### [assessed-in-synthesis] The Blight — "a cozy fog rolls in; the whole sounder lights lanterns to warm it off" (goblin-lore skin: The Great Hunger's chill, reusing the existing Goblin Horde art). Player-facing label: "A Blight has settled over the valley."
*A gentle fog drifts over every barn; the whole sounder lights lanterns — by doing the small kind things they already do — until golden hour breaks and everyone's pig perks up.*  
- **Fantasy:** It's a shared-weather adventure, not a monster fight. A soft violet fog (The Great Hunger's breath, on-lore with the greedy-goblin end of the alignment axis) drifts over EVERY barn at once. Nobody caused it; nobody is behind. The valley feels a little sleepy — and the whole sounder, together, warms it off by doing the small kind things they already do: tickling their pig, visiting a friend, burying a truffle. Each action lights a lantern on a shared valley-wide horizon. When enough lanterns glow, the fog lifts into a GOLDEN HOUR — warm light, everyone's pig perks up and regenerates faster for a day. The feeling is "we made the sun come out together," never "we were being punished until we performed."
- **Loop:** SPAWN via pg_cron sweep_blight → global mild 1.15x regen fog + community warmth bar → every player's first-of-kind cozy action lights a lantern (capped 8/player) → bar hits 100% → fog lifts into a 24h golden-hour regen BOOST for all who did >=1 action + a Lantern-Bearer title; OR the fog simply drifts off at ends_at with no penalty and no boon if it never fills.
- **Slow/steal:** SLOW ONLY — never steal. Zero snout touch (see economy_safety). Add exactly ONE term to `public.regen_secs_for(uid)`, carried from the latest def (20260679) per carry-latest-def discipline: `* (CASE WHEN EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM public.blight_events WHERE status='active' AND started_at <= now() AND ends_at > now()) THEN 1.15 ELSE 1 END)`. This is a GLOBAL, player-independent slow (same for everyone, so it can't be weaponized against a rival, unlike a `sluggish_snout` curse — it therefore cannot collide with or be exploited by the alignment/curse system). The BOON reuses the identical precedented mechanism in the opposite direction: the `golden_hour` blessing term `* (CASE WHEN EXISTS(... golden_hour ... expires_at>now()) THEN 0.85 ELSE 1 END)`, structurally a clone of the shipped `war_winner_regen` ×0.85 line. Anti-punishment framing baked into the magnitude: 1.15 is intentionally the gentlest slow in the game (a `sluggish_snout` curse is 2×; a Blight is 1.15×), so an offline/new player returning mid-Blight loses almost nothing, and the boon (0.85) MORE than repays it. Net regen across a full Blight-to-golden-hour cycle for anyone who does one kind thing is a WIN, not a wash.
- **Reward:** On CLEAR (bar hits 100%): (1) the fog lifts for everyone instantly; (2) GOLDEN HOUR BOON — a `blessings` row of a new kind `golden_hour` (`expires_at = now() + 24h`) is granted to EVERY qualified participant, read by `regen_secs_for` as ×0.85 (a real regen BOOST, same magnitude/mechanism as the existing `war_winner_regen` buff — you WIN speed, you were never "un-slowed"); (3) a name-attributed ARTIFACT: a defeat-cosmetic title/flair "Lantern-Bearer of the [Blight name]" plus, for the very first Blight cleared each season, a cosmetic `blight_lantern` (a warm held-lantern, on-lore, can be authored later — title ships day one with zero art). Binary-floor rule: `qualified = true` (≥1 action) is the ONLY gate for the boon + title — capped warmth affects the BAR, never your eligibility. Lurkers who did nothing that window don't get the boon, but they were never punished either (fog was mild, and it's gone now). The clear broadcast names the community ("2,140 neighbors lit their lanterns"), not individuals — co-op, not competition.

### [assessed-in-synthesis] The Great Hunger — a hungry goblin hoards the world's lost snouts, and the whole Sounder digs it back out.
*A soft-hearted goblin got greedy and buried a mountain of never-claimed snouts. Everyone who does one cozy thing helps unbury the hoard — and everyone who helped gets a share when it opens.*  
- **Fantasy:** You wake to a note pinned to the barn: "The Great Hunger has been through the valley again." A round, sheepish goblin (existing Goblin Horde art, single boss pose) has scooped up every snout that fell down the back of the couch — coins nobody was holding, lost-and-found treasure — and packed them into an enormous glittering mound he's guarding out past the mud pit. He is not a villain; he is HUNGRY and a little embarrassed. The Sounder doesn't fight him — they coax him. Every cozy thing you already do (tickling, visiting, blessing, digging) is a pat on the head that convinces him to let a little of the hoard go. He is a communal piñata made of feelings, not an HP sponge. When the mound is fully coaxed open, he sighs with relief, the snouts rain down on everyone who lent a hand, and he waddles off full and happy until next season. It reads as a neighborhood barn-raising, not a raid.
- **Loop:** SPAWN: pg_cron `sweep_world_boss` (new, modeled on `sweep_mud_wars`) opens a Hunger event when `app_config.enabled('world_boss')` is true and none is active — inserts a `world_boss_events` row (status 'active', `hoard_total`, `hoard_recovered=0`, `starts_at`, `ends_at = now()+7 days`). CONTRIBUTION: the existing action RPCs (`update_profile_and_item_count`/tickle, `tickle_at_barn`, `send_blessing`, `dig_truffle`, `bury_truffle`) each call a tiny new SECURITY DEFINER helper `world_boss_strike(uid, base_points)` at their tail (best-effort, wrapped in `BEGIN...EXCEPTION WHEN OTHERS THEN NULL` like the mud-wars announcement guard, so it can NEVER roll back a core action). Each strike coaxes `base_points` snouts out of the mound (tickle=1, barn visit=2, blessing/dig/bury=3), UP TO a per-player daily cap. SHARED PROGRESS: a single community bar — `hoard_recovered / hoard_total`, read at boot by `world_event_state()`. Milestones at 25/50/75/100% fire inline `system_announcements` broadcasts ("The mound is half-open! The goblin is giggling now."). RESOLUTION on WIN (recovered ≥ total before ends_at): sweep flips status 'won', runs `resolve_world_boss()` which pays every qualifying contributor a CAPPED equal-ish share via `grant_tickles`, grants the defeat title, and broadcasts. RESOLUTION on TIMEOUT (ends_at reached, not full): status 'coaxed_partial' — the goblin STILL waddles off and STILL pays out everyone who helped, prorated to how open the mound got (min 60% payout floor so it never feels like a loss), grants a softer "Kind Neighbor" title, broadcasts a warm "he took what he could carry and left the rest for you." There is NO punishing failure state — the only difference between win and timeout is the size of the smile.
- **Slow/steal:** Primarily the STEAL lever, reframed as HEIST-RECLAIM, plus a whisper of the SLOW lever as opt-in flavor. STEAL: the hoard is NOT raided from personal wallets. It is HOUSE-FUNDED / unclaimed — `hoard_total` is minted-once into the event row at spawn (server-authoritative, capped: `hoard_total = LEAST(active_player_count * 12, 60000)` snouts, a fixed budget the goblin "found," never scraped from `profiles.counter`). The community "recovers" it by acting; on resolution it REDISTRIBUTES out of the event row to contributors via `grant_tickles`. Net economy effect = a bounded, one-directional transfer from a house pool INTO players, gated by participation — it is a controlled faucet whose size is fixed at spawn and whose distribution is capped per head (see economy_safety). SLOW (optional flavor, dark by default): a single extra term in `regen_secs_for` — `* (CASE WHEN <event active AND hoard_recovered < 25%> THEN 1.10 ELSE 1 END)` — a mild 10% "the valley feels sleepy while the snouts are missing" that AUTO-LIFTS the instant the community crosses 25% recovered (a near-trivial threshold), so it is a shared weather system for the first hour, not a week-long punishment. Anti-punishment: the debuff is 10% (inside the existing ±10% alignment band, so it never exceeds a swing players already tolerate), it clears almost immediately from collective action, and it is behind its OWN sub-flag (`world_boss_blight`) so we can ship the whole event with ZERO debuff if playtests find it reads as punishment. The BOON (a win-only regen BUFF, below) is the headline; the blight is a removable garnish.
- **Reward:** The community wins TWO things. (1) A shared BOON, not the mere end of a debuff: on WIN, every qualifying contributor gets a `full_belly_regen` blessing (72h, ×0.90 regen) minted via the existing blessings table — the SAME shape as `war_winner_regen`, so it composes cleanly and is capped/temporary. (2) The reclaimed snouts: each contributor receives an EQUAL base share plus a small participation-weighted top-up, all capped. Distribution: `per_head = LEAST(floor(hoard_recovered / contributor_count), 300)` snouts equal to everyone (binary floor honored — showing up once gets the full base), then leftover distributed +1 per point up to each player's earned points, so a 20-point player gets at most 20 extra over a 1-point player. Hard ceiling per player ≈ 320 snouts (roughly two truffles' worth — meaningful, not economy-warping). NAME-ATTRIBUTED ARTIFACT: on the FIRST win of a season the top-3 contributors (by points, ties broken by earliest first-strike) are stamped into the broadcast and earn a unique title ("Fed the Great Hunger — <username>"), and EVERY contributor earns the season's cosmetic title ("Hoard-Coaxer" on win / "Kind Neighbor" on partial), granted through the existing earned-titles path (20260677_titles_earned_only). Co-presence is the community HP bar itself; the artifact is a title anyone can wear.

### [revise] The Siege — a re-runnable, phased, community live-ops shell (payloads: The Great Hunger, The Blight, The Goblin King) · punish=low · econ=none · cozy_ok
*A cadenced neighborhood-wide adventure where everyone who shows up once helps rebuild the barn, and every phase we clear turns a debuff into a shared boon.*  
- **Fantasy:** Once a season, a charming goblin menace rolls up on the whole sounder at once — not YOUR barn, OUR valley. A campfire lights on the Home screen; a communal Rampart bar shows the neighborhood pushing back together. It never feels like "you're behind." It feels like the whole town leaning a shoulder against the same door — a barn-raising with a friendly monster on the other side. Because it is a SHELL, the SAME warm ritual returns each season wearing a different coat: The Great Hunger (a hungry goblin eating the valley's snacks), The Blight (a mild fog over the fields), The Goblin King (a boastful little tyrant). Same chassis, new outfit, so it always feels familiar-but-fresh — the RuneScape holiday-event feeling of "oh, it's THAT time again."
- **Loop:** SPAWN (pg_cron, monthly): sweep_the_siege() inserts one row into siege_events (phase=1, rampart=0, status='active', theme='great_hunger', ends_at=now()+7d) only if the world_boss flag's global is on AND no active siege exists. A one-time INLINE system_announcements fan-out ("The Great Hunger has wandered into the valley — lend a hoof when you can") lands in every player's WhileAway feed. CONTRIBUTE: existing action RPCs (tickle via update_profile_and_item_count, dig_truffle, bury_truffle, do_visit, send_blessing, send_curse) each call a tiny helper siege_strike(caller) at their tail. siege_strike records ONE row per (player, UTC-day) in siege_participation and adds a FLAT, CAPPED amount to the day's rampart — your first qualifying action of the day is worth the daily notch (10), further actions that day add nothing to the bar (binary daily floor + flat ceiling). CO-PRESENCE: world_event_state() (boot/home read, same slot as home_stats/feature_flags) returns {theme, phase, rampart_pct, phase_goal, ends_at, my_tier, contributed_today} so Home renders a live Rampart bar + a "you've helped today" checkmark. PHASES: rampart is really a per-phase counter; when it crosses phase_goal[phase], sweep advances phase (1→2→3), resets the sub-bar, and INLINE-broadcasts a milestone ("Phase 2! The valley pushed the goblin back to the creek"). Each phase cleared flips ONE cozy world boon on for everyone (see reward). RESOLUTION — WIN: reaching phase 3's goal before ends_at sets status='won', fires the finale boon + mints the name-attributed artifact for every qualifying participant. TIMEOUT: at ends_at, sweep sets status='resolved' regardless — the goblin "wanders off, full and sleepy," EVERY phase already cleared stays as a permanent-for-the-season boon, everyone who scored once still gets a (smaller) keepsake, and copy is warm ("the valley held; the goblin's off to nap"). There is no failure state and no punishment for not finishing — you keep what you built.
- **Slow/steal:** Uses the SLOW lever only, and only as a MILD, HOPEFUL, phase-DECREASING debuff — never a steal, never a wallet raid. Add ONE term to regen_secs_for(uid): `* public.siege_blight_mult()`. siege_blight_mult() is a STABLE helper returning 1.0 when no siege is active, and otherwise a small multiplier that SHRINKS as the community clears phases: phase 1 = 1.10, phase 2 = 1.05, phase 3 = 1.00 (gone). So the "blight" is at most +10% regen time (≈6 min on a 60-min cycle) and the community VISIBLY dissolves it by playing together — you literally tickle the fog away. Crucially it is capped tiny and it only ever gets BETTER, so it reads as "we're winning" not "we're punished," and it fully clears the moment phase 3 lands (often before the event even ends). The anti-punishment framing: the debuff is a shared weather system, not a scarlet letter — it applies equally to everyone including offline players, so no individual is singled out, and because the cozy core loop (tickle Rosie, visit friends) still works at +10% it never blocks anyone. No snout is ever touched by this design.
- **Reward:** Two layers, both non-power, both gift-framed. (1) COMMUNITY BOONS — each phase cleared flips a cozy, non-economy world boon on for EVERYONE (participant or not): phase 1 = a warm valley ambiance / campfire on Home; phase 2 = a free festive Barn décor swap for the season; phase 3 (finale) = a 48h valley-wide "warm_tea"-style regen BOOST blessing granted to all (server-inserted blessing kind='siege_feast', capped 48h, non-stacking, non-purchasable) — you WIN a boost, you don't merely stop a penalty. (2) PERSONAL COSMETIC LADDER by distinct participation-days (the anti-whale, binary-floor rule): 1 day = the keepsake title/badge (everyone who showed up once); 3 days = a themed cosmetic (e.g. "Hunger-Chaser Cap"); 5 days = the animated variant; 7 days = a "prismatic" tint of the same item. Same item, escalating polish — no exclusive power, only expression, honoring "earned over bought." NAME-ATTRIBUTED ARTIFACT: on WIN, mint a per-season "Valley Ledger" — a titles/hats row (e.g. hat id `siege_great_hunger_s7`) whose description embeds the event name and season, so the Closet permanently reads "You helped push back The Great Hunger, Season 7." On TIMEOUT, a humbler keepsake still drops for every day-1+ participant. Rewards grant via the existing grant-lane pattern (server-authoritative INSERT into user_hats / a titles grant), never a snout mint.
- **⚑ Skeptic:** Strongest objection (verified against code): the blight is invisible to exactly the players most likely to misread it. siege_blight_mult() lands inside regen_secs_for(), which feeds home_stats().regen_seconds, which the Barn renders as the live "Next tickle" countdown (migration 20260643). But the Rampart card that explains the event is gated behind useFeatureFlag("world_boss"). So the debuff is GLOBAL/ungated while its ONLY on-screen context is PER-USER-flag-gated. A player with the world_boss override still off (every new/unflagged account, or any solo cozy player who never opens the card) sees their tickle timer silently slide ~60→~66 min with zero explanation — a "why is my pig slower today?" with no answer. That is the collective-punishment/confusion trap re-entering through the one seam the "shared weather" framing doesn't close: weather you can't see isn't cozy, it's a bug report. CONCRETE FIX: bind the blight to the same gate as its explanation. (a) Gate siege_blight_mult() on the caller's EFFECTIVE world_boss flag so an un-flagged player is a literal 1.0 no-op AND sees no unexplained slowdown (blight + Rampart card appear/disappear together per-user); or (b) if the debuff must be truly global, make the Rampart card global too — drop the flag gate on the card at public launch, keep the flag only for the Brian-only dark-launch window — so no one ever eats +10% without the campfire on Home explaining why. Use (a) during dark-launch, flip to (b) at launch. Everything else is sound: zero snout touch (economy-safe by construction), a real binary-floor/flat-cap (10/day, whale==lurker, ≤70 total/7d, goals scale with playerbase — no solo/whale domination), correct reuse of guarded-inline-announcement + dark-launch precedents, no Mud Wars/alignment clash (separate lever/tables/flag, no war titles, alignment_score untouched). Secondary (lower priority, the design's own open-Q5): finale siege_feast (×0.85) stacks multiplicatively with war_winner_regen (×0.85), warm_tea (×0.5) and happiness (×0.85) in regen_secs_for — cap/clamp siege_feast or make it explicitly non-stacking with war_winner in the migration, else the "boon" briefly out-powers the earned/paid buffs.

### [assessed-in-synthesis] The Long Night — the shared foundation for server-wide events (world_events spine)
*One boring, safe, cozy substrate every world-boss theme plugs into: a state table, a boot-read, a dark-launch flag, one regen term, a capped-per-player striker, and the anti-punishment rules baked into the plumbing.*  
- **Fantasy:** This is the spine, not a theme — but the reference skin it ships with is a gentle, charming goblin: "The Long Night" (a sleepy goblin mischief-maker curled over the whole valley, dimming everyone's snout-lamps). The player-facing story is always the same shape: something soft and shared drifts in, the WHOLE valley notices it at once (co-presence), everyone who shows up even once helps push it back, and when it lifts the valley gets a warm BOON and a keepsake with the community's names on it. It never feels like your fault, never like a punishment, never like a deadline you can fail alone. The threat is weather, not blame. Zero new art — reuses the shipped Goblin Horde set (docs/mudwar-goblins-brief.md, SlopToss.tsx).
- **Loop:** End-to-end, form-agnostic: (1) SPAWN — pg_cron `sweep_world_events()` (or a dev RPC) inserts one row into `public.world_events` with status='active', an `hp`/`hp_max`, `regen_mult` (e.g. 1.15), `starts_at`/`ends_at`, `theme`. Only ONE event is active at a time (partial unique index). (2) CO-PRESENCE — every client boot-reads `world_event_state()` (folded into home_stats + the useFeatureFlags boot) and, if `world_boss` is on for that user, renders a shared community progress bar ("the valley is X% through the Long Night") from `hp/hp_max`. (3) CONTRIBUTION — existing action RPCs (tickle, visit, blessing, dig, bury) call ONE new helper `strike_world_event(caller, weight)` that (a) records the caller in `world_event_participants` if absent (binary floor), (b) decrements `hp` by `weight` ONLY up to that caller's daily CAP, (c) is a no-op if no event / event over / cap reached. (4) MILESTONES — as `hp` crosses 75/50/25/0%, an inline `system_announcements` fan-out broadcasts a warm beat. (5) RESOLUTION — WIN (hp<=0): `resolve_world_event()` flips status='won', grants the community BOON (a `world_boon` blessing) to every binary participant, and stamps the name-attributed artifact. (6) TIMEOUT (ends_at reached, hp>0): status='waned' — the goblin "gets sleepy and drifts off on its own"; participants STILL get a smaller consolation boon + keepsake. There is NO lose state that punishes — worst case the valley "helped it settle" and still gets a warm thing. The regen debuff simply ends either way.
- **Slow/steal:** SLOW only — the spine never touches snouts (that's left to a theme's opt-in, see economy_safety). The global debuff is ONE term added to `regen_secs_for(uid)`, composed exactly like the existing war_winner term: `* (SELECT COALESCE(regen_mult, 1) FROM public.world_events WHERE status='active' AND starts_at <= now() AND ends_at > now() LIMIT 1)`. With `regen_mult` seeded at 1.15 this is a +15% slower refill — MILDER than a single sluggish_snout curse (×2) or being fully Goblic (alignment ×1.10). Anti-punishment framing is baked into the SQL comment and the copy: the multiplier is small, hopeful, shared by everyone equally (so it reads as weather, not a personal penalty), and it LIFTS the instant the event resolves — plus the reward is a regen BUFF (`world_boon`, ×0.85 for 48h, reusing the exact war_winner_regen mechanic), so a participant ends up FASTER than baseline. You win a boost; you don't merely stop being punished. Offline/new players are never charged anything: the debuff is on regen only (a rate they weren't using while away), it's clamped by `GREATEST(60, ...)`, and it costs zero snouts.
- **Reward:** The community wins a BOON + a keepsake. BOON: a `world_boon` row in `public.blessings` (kind='world_boon', ×0.85 regen for 48h) granted to EVERY binary participant on WIN — identical for all, so a whale and a one-tap player get the same warm boost (the cap principle carried into the payout). On TIMEOUT/wane, a smaller `world_boon_settled` (×0.92 for 24h) still goes to all participants — showing up always pays. KEEPSAKE / name-attributed artifact: a cosmetic title unlock (e.g. "Lantern-Bearer of the Long Night", granted via the existing earned-titles path) to participants, and the event's `world_events.roster` (a small jsonb of, say, the first-100 or a random sample of participant usernames) is stamped into the win announcement + a "Valley Chronicle" line so real names are attributed to the defeat. No snout reward at all — status is earned, never minted (SKILL charter). Reward math is flat + binary, exactly per the rubric.
