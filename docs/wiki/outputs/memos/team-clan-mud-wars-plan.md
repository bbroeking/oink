# Team / Clan / Mud Wars — Rollout Plan

> **This is the detailed rollout/gating companion to [[mud-wars-master-plan]].** The master plan owns the vision + the async-telephone design story; this page owns the *sequence* — the blocking-first naming fix, the economy wall, the population flip-trigger, the launch coupling, push-dark, and the MVP phasing. Read the master plan for "what the war is"; read this for "what must land, in what order, before `MUD_FIGHTS_VISIBLE` flips."

> **Founder's one-sentence goal:** ship the *fully optional* async ("telephone-type") clan war where you **invite ≤5 friends into a crew, challenge & accept another crew (consensual, one war at a time + cooldown), cooperate *asynchronously* over ~3 days to out-tickle them, and win their snouts + a regen buff + a pile of war-only cosmetics** — a Clash-of-Clans-style competitive meta-layer that competitive players use to climb/defend the core leaderboard, while collectors and dress-up players ignore it entirely. **The core cozy game must stand alone; the war is never the end-all.**
>
> The base layer is already built and dark. This plan turns the founder's intent — as resolved by the 2026-06 *telephone pivot* — into the rollout: flip it on at the right time, layer the async co-op mechanics on the fair flat sling, wall off the economy, and resolve the "Sounder" word collision first.

TTP's clan-war layer — **Sounder Mud Fights** — is a ≤5-friend crew that fights another crew on a buff-free, reset-to-zero field via a flat **20 mud-slings/day** (`DAILY_ALLOTMENT`), per-capita scored with a quorum-2 floor. The full base stack (server + client) is shipped behind `MUD_FIGHTS_VISIBLE = false` (`constants/featureFlags.ts` line 19) and the migration `supabase/migrations/20260647000000_mud_fights.sql` is **still UNPUSHED** (awaiting an explicit "push it now"). The build is done; the decision is *when and how to turn it on* — and the strategic answer (per [[future-direction-2026-06]]) is **not at public launch; hold it dark until the population can fill two real crews.**

## The telephone pivot — what changed

This plan was first written before the **async "telephone-type" pivot**. The old framing posed the war's cooperation question as an open *Tension #1* — "the founder wants cooperation to raise the crew's score, but the flat per-capita sling deliberately flattens group output; reconcile via a future hand-waved 'cooperation-bonus'." **That tension is now RESOLVED.** The resolved design (founder grill + the two research briefs, [[coop-mechanics-research-2026-06]] and [[coop-telephone-items-research-2026-06]]) answers "what is the cooperation" concretely and built-out: a set of **async telephone mechanics layered as *capped bonuses* on the flat base sling**, never a second scoring track. The flat sling stays the anti-snowball spine; cooperation is the bonus on top — exactly the founder's "advantages are EARNED only via in-war cooperation."

The four async co-op mechanic plans are written and build-ready (each a follow-on migration on the unpushed base):
- [[mudwar-coop-slop-bucket]] — **Pass the Slop Bucket**: a parallel meter the whole crew fills on their own time, a telephone *narration* nudge riding on top; capped flat per-lap bonus.
- [[mudwar-coop-mud-heap]] — **The Mud Heap**: a telephone sculpture each member stacks on the last person's piece; the finished Heap *becomes* a unique combinatorial cosmetic + shareable card (the item-generator + virality variant).
- [[mudwar-coop-truffle-hunt]] — **Crew Truffle Hunt**: a buried war-truffle that needs N *distinct* crewmates to unearth; the distinct-human gate is both the co-op hook and the anti-alt mechanism.
- [[mudwar-coop-mud-fort]] — **Build the Mud Fort**: the crew's slings re-read as a visibly-growing staged structure (a derived progress bar) kept as a war-tagged cosmetic; the minimal-new-surface variant.

And the war-exclusive item economy that the cosmetic rewards run through:
- [[mudwar-war-spoils-items]] — **War Spoils**: the war-only cosmetic vault (animated bog backgrounds, mud-splatter hats, themed sets with completion capstones, recolor floors, the Mud Bucket gacha, named-season scarcity) — many SKUs from a few dozen anchors via the ChatGPT/icon-gen pipeline, plus the *capped, contribution-gated, idempotent* grant RPCs.

The other still-valid tension — **"fight strangers with your friends" vs the friend-on-opponent-crew discovery filter** — is unchanged and carried forward below (it predates the pivot and the pivot doesn't touch it).

### The cooperation answer — RESOLVED (Tension #1 closed)

The old plan flagged a real conflict: the founder wants cooperation to *raise* the crew's score; the build's `resolve_war` scores `SUM(mud) / COUNT(active members)` of a flat-20/day cap, which deliberately neutralizes group size + total cooperation to kill the whale/roster-stuffing snowball. The pivot resolves it **without** reopening that hole and **without** a future hand-wave: the cooperation is a concrete, build-ready set of **async telephone mechanics layered as capped bonuses on the flat sling** — the flat per-capita math in `resolve_war` is *never touched*, so the war outcome is decided exactly as today; the cooperation pays out as a small, flat, capped, off-leaderboard treat + war-exclusive cosmetics.

All four mechanics share the same fairness shape (from the briefs' "low floor, flat ceiling" + "the scoring shape is the whole ballgame" findings, [[coop-mechanics-research-2026-06]] / [[coop-telephone-items-research-2026-06]]):

- **A scoop / a dig / a brick / a piece IS a sling** (or rides alongside it) — clamped by the *same* flat-20 daily allotment, so no one can exceed the flat ceiling; the flat-20 cap *is already* the strongest anti-whale, anti-collusion lever (capping payout starves alt-funneling of payoff).
- **The co-op bonus is flat, small, capped, off the leaderboard, idempotent** — e.g. `SLOP_LAP_BONUS = 5` snouts/lap capped at 50/war (Slop Bucket), `TRUFFLE_BONUS = 15` once/war (Truffle Hunt), `FORT_BONUS`/`HEAP_BONUS ≈ 30` once/won-war (Fort/Heap). `counter`-only where it shouldn't touch rank. No multiplier on slings, no scaling by who did most → no DKP-gap snowball.
- **A binary participation floor** ("logged ≥1 scoop/dig/piece this war") gates the war-exclusive cosmetic — clearable in one tap, kills the pure free-rider without grading effort (Clash of Clans' "contribute ≥1 to claim anything").
- **The distinct-human / parallel-meter structure is the co-op hook AND the anti-abuse mechanism.** A whale slinging 1000× still counts as 1 distinct digger; a lap completes only when *every active member* scooped; the Heap needs multiple hands across days. The scarce resource is *distinct active friends*, which no whale or alt can fake (crews are `are_friends`-gated + cap-5).
- **Async, no homework, graceful degradation.** A missing member contributes 0 and never freezes the crew (parallel meters, not a serial baton — the briefs' "parallelize, never serialize" mandate); the clock resolves the war on schedule. **Everyone scores, win or lose** (the cosmetic + bonus are participation-gated, decoupled from win/loss — defangs sandbagging).

So "maximize the crew's tickles by cooperating" becomes *literally true* — the earned advantage is the capped bonus + the kept cosmetic — while the fair flat floor is untouched. **Which mechanic ships first is the Season-2 content decision (D6 below); the fairness spine is identical across all four, so the choice is about feel + art, not safety.**

**The one deliberate trade (called out so it reads as a choice, not an oversight).** The co-op-mechanics brief's headline recommendation is to make togetherness *strictly better* via a **multiplicative** crew bonus. TTP consciously **declines multiplicativity** — a multiplicative crew score is exactly the whale / big-crew snowball lever the founder grill ruled out. Each mechanic substitutes the brief's *other* load-bearing levers (the binary participation floor, the distinct-human gate, the filling parallel meter, the dual-track win/participation reward) and keeps every bonus **additive + flat-capped**, with the per-capita base as the untouched fairness spine. This matches the master plan's "one deliberate trade" note verbatim — it is a deliberate, founder-aligned rejection of the briefs' top rec, not a gap.

### The still-open tension — "fight strangers with your friends" (discovery filter)

`find_challengeable_crews` only surfaces a target crew if you share a friend edge with one of *its* members (`are_friends(auth.uid(), m.user_id)`). At ~27 players that's a reasonable discovery filter, but it structurally nudges you toward challenging crews containing your friends — the exact "fight your wife" outcome the founder rejected. The challenge is consensual (they must accept), so it's not forced, but the *discovery surface* nudges the wrong way.
- **Resolution:** at the small beta scale, leave the friend-gated discovery (it's the only sane way to find an opponent in a 27-person graph) but keep the **challenge explicitly leader-to-leader and opt-in on both ends** — which the build already does. **Post-launch, when the population grows, relax `find_challengeable_crews` to surface non-friend crews** (open-challenge board) so you can rally your friends *into* a crew and fight *strangers*, which is the founder's true intent. Flag as D7. No code change needed pre-flip.

## The arc / rollout — the experience in phases

The clan layer is deliberately the **competitive counterweight** to the cozy season, sequenced *behind* the launch package, not in front of it.

- **Build-up (pre-flip, now → public launch).** Mud Fights stays invisible. The retention spine ships first (the [[streak-and-garden]] Garden, the daily "done" beat, the founder-skin + leaderboard reset at the App Store cutover). The "Sounder" word collision gets resolved. The base migration is pushed *dark* so the schema is live and battle-tested without any UI exposing it.
- **The moment (a war).** A leader rallies up to 4 friends into a Sounder, posts a `challenge_crew` to a rival crew (or `challenge_house` for the bot on-ramp); the rival leader `accept_challenge`s (consensual — never forced). Both sides sling their flat daily mud across the ~3-day window; **on top of the flat sling, the async co-op layer runs** (passing the slop bucket / stacking the Mud Heap / unearthing the truffle / raising the fort — whichever ships as the Season-2 headline). The `/mud-war` tug-of-war bar moves on every tap; the co-op verb is showing up daily and pulling your crewmate's open loop. No whale lever — a crew's ceiling is `members × 20 × days`, and the co-op bonus is flat-capped on top.
- **The reveal.** `resolve_war` fires lazily on the first read after `ends_at` (no cron — it clones the Trough's `resolve_expired_drives` pattern), pays the winners (own mud 1:1 in snouts + a per-capita share of 50% of the loser's pot, **capped**), grants the `war_winner_regen` blessing and `mud_champion`/`mud_veteran`/`mud_legend` titles, grants the **War Spoils** cosmetics to contributors (idempotent, contribution-gated), and surfaces the outcome through the existing WhileAway / `system_announcements` path plus the on-screen resolved recap (and, for the Heap, a shareable card). **This is the competitive reveal — distinct from the cozy [[seasons-and-judgement-day]] Judgement Day verdict** (see "Two seasons" below). The reveal *is* the reward (the briefs' authorship-and-surprise finding) — even a losing crew sees and can share what they built.
- **What's next / Season 2.** Mud Fights becomes the **Season 2 competitive headline** — flipped only once post-launch DAU can fill 2+ real crews of 5. **Season-2 content = the async co-op mechanic(s) + the War Spoils economy** (not a vague "deepening"): one capped co-op layer shipped first, the war-only cosmetic vault behind it, a war-only token replacing raw-snout payout to close the economy, and the cosmetic-only display tie (your closet loadout + alignment aura in the pit, display-only) so it's *your* pig fighting.

### (B) Wall off the economy — the no-flood isolation
Today `resolve_war` mints raw spendable **snouts** (`counter + tickles_earned`) — a new uncapped faucet against the [[snouts-economy]] no-sink problem (the visit cash-faucet lesson: every payout needs a cap *and* an anti-collusion gate, not just a rate limit). The founder's "rewards paid as snouts to the CORE economy" is honored long-term by redeeming a token *for* core-applicable rewards, plus the **cosmetic vault** as the headline reward, not by minting raw snouts. For Season 2:
- **War cosmetics are the headline reward, not snouts.** [[mudwar-war-spoils-items]] is the war-only cosmetic economy — a large, durable, cosmetic-only vault (animated backgrounds, mud-splatter hats, themed sets, recolor floors, the Mud Bucket gacha, named-season scarcity). Cosmetics are ~80% of revenue in the games that flex hardest, and they *can't* flood the cozy core (they're `cost = 0`, unbuyable + un-Troughable, equip-only). This is where the "many war-exclusive cosmetics" reward lives.
- **NEW war-only token** (`profiles.war_tokens` column, or a `war_token_ledger` table) that `resolve_war` mints *instead of* raw snouts for the core-economy payout, redeemable only for war-exclusive cosmetics + the regen buff. This is the [[future-direction-2026-06]] "closed competitive economy" call — the competitive layer can't flood the cozy core, while the buff still "pays out to the core" exactly as the founder asked.
- **Keep the core snout/tickle payout CAPPED.** The `resolve_war` win payout (`floor(loser_pot × 0.5 / win_active)`) and every co-op bonus (Slop Bucket / Truffle / Fort / Heap) is flat-capped, contribution-gated, idempotent (rides `resolved_at`). The War Spoils grants are flat-per-contributor (no proportional scaling) — the worked alt-farming trace in [[mudwar-war-spoils-items]] shows each cap biting.
- **Gate house-bot wins to cosmetic/title only** — already partly done: beating the Mudlarks grants only a flat `c_house = 25`-snout stipend (`resolve_war`, constant at line 594, applied via `counter = counter + c_house` in the bot-war branch) + the regen buff, with **no** `tickles_earned`, **no** `war_wins`/titles. Tighten further so the bot win grants *no regen buff, no token transfer, no War Spoils cosmetic* — the fixed-pace bot is practice only, never farmable for core value or prestige.
- **Pre-model and cap** the war faucet before it scales. The current real-war faucet per winning member is `own_mud + floor(loser_pot × 0.5 / win_active)`, both terms bounded by the flat-20 cap (a 5-member crew over a 5-day war tops out near `5×20×5 = 500` own-mud + a capped share). **First-pass budget placeholder:** tolerate ≤ ~one crew's-worth of injected snouts per resolved war (~500-1000 snouts) per active war pair per cadence; tune against the live `tickles_earned` distribution after the leaderboard reset (F) sets the baseline. The war-token wall below removes this from the spendable-core faucet entirely.
- **Exit condition:** `resolve_war` mints into a capped, contribution-gated, idempotent payout (token or capped snouts) with the bot win granting cosmetic/title only; the per-war injection ceiling is written down and instrumented against the pre-modeled budget.

### (C) Cosmetic tie to the cozy loop — "it's YOUR pig fighting"

Add **display-only** closet loadout + alignment aura to the war roster payload (extend `war_side` with cosmetic slots; `war_side` is the internal helper `war_state` calls, so the new fields surface through `war_state` for free) and render it in `app/mud-war.tsx`. The flag flips on a **population condition**, not a date. **Committed trigger:** flip `MUD_FIGHTS_VISIBLE` only when post-launch metrics show **≥ 2 distinct non-bot crews, each with ≥ 2 active members (quorum) and a combined ≥ 8 distinct active players across them** — i.e. two real 5-eligible crews can fight without the Mudlarks being the *only* opponent. **Instrument it:** a one-query dashboard read (`count(*) FROM crews WHERE is_bot=false` joined to active-member counts) on the live DB; the founder makes the flip call when it clears for ~3 consecutive days. Until then, `find_challengeable_crews` returns near-empty (it also requires a shared friend edge — the discovery tension) and the bot is the whole game, the wrong first impression for a "clan war."

### (F) Couple to the public-launch package (founder's launch decision)

## Decisions to make

1. **The flip trigger — is the committed (D) metric right?** Recommended: ≥2 real crews, each quorum-capable, ≥8 distinct active players, holding ~3 days — then a founder judgment call. 2. **War-only token, cosmetic vault, or both — and from day one?** Flipping with the current raw-snout payout is faster but opens the faucet; building the war-token + War Spoils cosmetic economy first (items B + G) is the cleaner Season-2 story but adds migrations + a redemption surface. **Recommendation:** the War Spoils cosmetic vault is the headline reward *from the flip* (it can't flood the core), with the core snout payout capped; the war-token replacing raw snouts can follow if the capped snout payout still feels too loose. No rush justifies opening an uncapped raw-snout faucet.
3. **Two seasons, one calendar, or staggered?** The cozy [[seasons-and-judgement-day]] finale (`finalize_season('season_1')` in `20260526000000_finale.sql`, ranks alignment, wipes to 0, the `judgement-day-season-1` cron at `0 12 15 7 *` = noon UTC 2026-07-15, `20260579000000_judgement_day_cron.sql`) is the SOUL season. Mud Fights is the TRIBE season. Do they share a season boundary or run independent cadences? **This couples to the cron decision in [[future-direction-2026-06]] (open decision #1) and to the finale-shape choice between [[judgement-day-plan-a-quiet-reckoning]] / [[judgement-day-plan-b-great-schism]] / [[judgement-day-plan-c-living-almanac]] — both must be made first;** the memo recommends unscheduling the July-15 cron (`SELECT cron.unschedule('judgement-day-season-1')`) and re-anchoring Judgement Day to public-launch week, which would then naturally stagger the SOUL finale (launch) ahead of the TRIBE Season-2 flip.
4. **House-bot at flip, or real-crews-only?** Keep the Mudlarks as a low-population on-ramp (risk: the first impression is "I fought a bot"), or require two real crews and let wars be rare-but-special until the base is big enough? The (D) gate makes this mostly moot — by flip time there are real crews — but the bot stays as the *practice/onboarding* opponent (and grants no war value: no `tickles_earned`/titles/regen/War-Spoils cosmetic).
5. **Crew cap — hold at 5 or raise?** Locked at `CREW_CAP = 5` for the 27-player world; post-launch growth may warrant 10 ("10 is too big right now," per the founder — i.e. revisit, don't raise now). One constant in two places (`constants/mudFights.ts` + the inlined RPC literals) — drift risk flagged for the deferred `mud_fight_const()`.
6. **Which co-op mechanic ships first as the Season-2 headline (the cooperation content)?** All four are build-ready follow-ons with the identical fairness spine. **Recommendation:** ship [[mudwar-coop-mud-fort]] (minimal new surface, no new table) OR [[mudwar-coop-truffle-hunt]] (cleanest anti-alt) as the MVP co-op layer, with [[mudwar-coop-mud-heap]] (the shareable item-generator) as the follow-on that pairs with the virality loop. **Note the MVP pick optimizes for lowest *new surface / risk*, not the master plan's optimizer scores** (Slop 94 / Heap 95 / Truffle 90 / Fort 92 / Spoils 95) — the Fort/Truffle picks score slightly lower on richness but are the cheapest and safest first ship for a solo dev; the higher-scoring Heap is the deliberate *follow-on*, not the MVP. Confirm the 5→3 cadence swap rides in with whichever ships first.
7. **Open-challenge board (the discovery tension):** post-launch, relax `find_challengeable_crews` to surface non-friend crews so players rally friends *into* a crew and fight *strangers* (the founder's true team thesis)? No pre-flip change needed; decide before the population grows past the friend-graph discovery limit. (D7)
8. **Does TRIBE membership become permanent (cross-war identity), or per-war?** The crew persists across wars today (one crew per user), which is the durable-belonging TRIBE read; confirm that's the intended identity shape before marketing it as "your clan." (Founder language — "invite your friends to form a clan" — reads as permanent.)

## Success metrics

- **Flip trigger (pre-flip):** ≥2 non-bot crews, each quorum-capable, ≥8 distinct active players, holding ~3 days (item D).
- **Post-flip health (the "is the war working" reads):** (1) **≥1 real crew-vs-crew war resolved per week** (not just bot wars); (2) **war participation rate** — % of crew members who sling ≥1 day during a war they're in (target the quorum-2 floor being cleared by *real* effort, not alts); (3) **co-op clear-rate** — % of real wars where the crew clears the co-op floor (truffle unearthed / fort built / a full slop lap / a real Heap), the signal the cooperation layer actually fires; (4) **the war stays optional** — core DAU / streak retention does NOT dip for non-participants (the "core stands alone" guardrail; if collectors churn because they feel pressured, the war is over-weighted in the UI).
- **Economy guardrail:** weekly snout (or token) injection from `resolve_war` + the co-op bonuses stays within the pre-modeled budget (item B); War Spoils cosmetic grants are flat-per-contributor (no proportional scaling); **collusion signal** — flag any crew pair that hits the `rematch_cooldown` reason **≥ 3× in a rolling 7 days** (i.e. is trying to re-fight the same partner the instant the 24h window opens), the canonical pattern of two crews farming each other for guaranteed payout.

## Risks / open questions

- **Flipping into a ghost town.** At ~27 beta users the spec's own fallback is a house-bot — building/exposing a clan-war layer for a world too small to fill it is the exact mis-sequence [[future-direction-2026-06]] warns against (the retention layer arriving before the acquisition unlock). *Mitigation: the (D) population gate — do not flip until two real crews exist.*
- **The economy faucet.** Raw-snout payout is an uncapped mint with no recurring sink (the visit cash-faucet lesson). *Mitigation: the War Spoils cosmetic vault is the headline reward (can't flood the core); the war-token wall (item B) replaces raw snouts; the core snout payout + every co-op bonus stays flat-capped, contribution-gated, idempotent; pre-model the weekly injection.*
- **The naming collision is live.** Shipping any new social surface before (A) bakes the three-way "Sounder" contradiction into the UI — a brand bug that reads as a real bug. *Mitigation: (A) is blocking, first.*
- **Two-season collision.** If the cozy Judgement Day (2026-07-15 cron) and a Mud Fights "season 2" relaunch land near each other unsequenced, players get two resets in one window. *Mitigation: make decision #3 / [[future-direction-2026-06]] open-decision #1 (unschedule + re-anchor the cron) before flipping the war.*
- **Carry-latest-def on every Season-2 follow-on (highest build risk).** Each co-op mechanic + War Spoils `CREATE OR REPLACE`s `resolve_war` / `war_state` / `war_side` / `sling_mud` — these MUST carry their **latest `20260647` bodies verbatim** and add only the new grant/read block, or the rebuild silently reverts the bot-farm guard, the savepoint hardening, or the per-capita scoring (the build-93 referral-gate class of bug). Diff each carried body line-for-line against `20260647` before push. *Mitigation: the diff gate in [[mudwar-war-spoils-items]] "Verification".*
- **INLINE announcements only.** Every co-op nudge / reveal / drop uses a direct `INSERT INTO system_announcements`. **Never `send_system_announcement`** (admin-gated → silent rollback for non-admins). All side effects savepoint-guarded so a fault can't roll back the sling/grant.
- **Idempotent payouts.** Every co-op bonus + War Spoils grant rides `resolve_war`'s `resolved_at` guard (or its own `unearthed_at`/`last_paid_lap` stamp) + `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` — a double-resolve / concurrent double-close can never double-pay. Needs the pgTAP race tests each mechanic plan lists.
- **Migration filename ordering.** Every Season-2 follow-on must sort alphabetically after the latest on disk (`20260649000000_onboarding_checklist.sql`) and after the unpushed `20260647`; `20260647` must be applied first (it owns `mud_wars`/`mud_slings`/`resolve_war`). Two files sharing a prefix collide on `schema_migrations.version` (PK).
- **The discovery tension** (carried from pre-pivot): friend-on-opponent discovery nudges toward "fight your friend" — resolved via consensual challenge now + open-challenge board post-launch (D7). No code change pre-flip.
- **One-war-per-crew TOCTOU** (low-probability concurrent-challenge race) and **test gaps** (tie/no-winner, bot payout, RLS, title thresholds untested) remain from the review — fine for a dark schema, worth closing before a public flip (step 3 above).
- **Constant drift** between `constants/mudFights.ts` and the inlined RPC literals (now including the new `SLOP_*`/`TRUFFLE_*`/`FORT_*`/`HEAP_*` co-op constants) — the deferred `mud_fight_const()` (P3) is the real single-source fix.
- **Cosmetic-art pipeline is the long pole.** The War Spoils volume + the animated apex + the per-mechanic art (truffle hat, fort stages, heap palette, slop bog) all depend on the ChatGPT/icon-gen pipeline (`icon-gen` skill, ~3-5 gens/day) + a manual reference-drag step. It's decoupled from the code (mechanics ship with placeholder titles + emoji fallbacks; art lands in later builds), but it's the real time sink, not the backend.
