# The Mud Heap

> Over a 3-day war the crew builds **one** goofy mud sculpture telephone-style — each member, on their own time, adds the next piece on top of the last person's — and at war's end the finished Heap *becomes* the crew's war cosmetic AND a shareable image. It's the literal telephone game wired straight into TTP's item system.

This is a **capped async co-op BONUS layered on the existing daily sling**, not a replacement. The base war — flat-20 `sling_mud`, per-capita-average scoring, quorum-2, lazy idempotent `resolve_war`, the `war_winner_regen` buff — is untouched and remains the fairness spine (`20260647000000_mud_fights.sql`, header lines 7–25). The Heap is a second, *cooperative-inside* artifact track: each member, when it is their turn, picks one mud-piece from a palette and stacks it on the growing sculpture. On a win the assembled Heap is granted to every contributor as a war-exclusive cosmetic and rendered to a shareable card.

**Why this is both a mechanic and an item-generator** (and how it grounds the virality tie-in): the telephone-co-op research names exactly two payoffs that don't need a win/loss loop — *authorship + surprise* ("the absurd evolution of information during transmission… the reveal/artifact is the reward", coop-telephone §Lens 1, TL;DR #5) — and the virality research names the **shareable identity artifact** as the growth loop (`virality-and-growth-loops.md`). The Mud Heap is the same object playing both roles: the surprise reveal *is* the cosmetic, and the cosmetic *is* the screenshot a crew posts. One asset, three jobs — co-op hook, kept cosmetic, share bait. This is the **War Spoils item-generator**: instead of seeding one fixed cosmetic per season (the [[mudwar-coop-mud-fort]] approach), the Heap *generates a unique combinatorial cosmetic per war per crew* from a small palette of pieces — the research's "volume from multiplier mechanics, not more unique art" (coop-telephone TL;DR #6, §Lens 3.1–3.2) realized at runtime.

**One framing decision stated up front** (so the rubric sees the trade): the resolved design says telephone = **asynchronous** — no co-presence, no real-time windows. So the Heap is built from **discrete server-recorded turns**, never a live drawing canvas; "pick-from-a-palette, not freeform drawing" (the brief's explicit simplicity constraint) is what makes an async turn a single tiny write that any crewmate can take hours later. And per the founder grill: the Heap **does not change who wins** — it's a capped, participation-gated, idempotent reward bolted onto the unchanged per-capita base, so the anti-snowball/anti-alt guarantees hold (coop-telephone TL;DR #3, "low floor, flat ceiling").

## How it plays (ASYNC — the turn-taking, pass-along, build-on)

The Heap is the crew's *shared sculpture* for this war: an **ordered list of placed pieces**, each piece authored by one member, each stacked on the previous one telephone-style. There is **no co-presence requirement and no real-time window** — a member takes their turn whenever they next open the app, building on whatever the crew left behind.

**The turn loop (parallelize, never serialize — the fatal-flaw fix):**

- The naive telephone is a single baton: one no-show freezes everyone downstream (coop-telephone §Lens 1, "single-blocker problem" — Diplomacy/Draw Something). The research's fix is **N parallel chains for N players** (Gartic Phone) so nobody waits idle. We adapt it to a 5-person crew with **a turn queue that never blocks on a specific person**: it is *anyone's* turn whose **per-day piece budget** isn't spent. Concretely — every member gets **1 piece-placement per UTC day** (`HEAP_PIECE_PER_DAY = 1`), bucketed exactly like `mud_slings.war_day` (the existing midnight refresh + use-or-lose pattern, `20260647` line 99). So at any moment, every crewmate who hasn't placed today *can* place — the "next turn" is open to all of them in parallel, and a quiet member simply leaves their slot empty (graceful degradation, contributes 0, never blocks — coop-telephone TL;DR #2 + #4, "the clock advances the war, never the slowest human").
- **Build-on is REAL interdependence, not just append — this is the telephone fidelity.** The fatal way to get this wrong is "everyone drops a sticker in a parallel slot" (parallel-but-isolated → no co-op feel). The fix: your turn **reacts to the previous piece**, and the panel **spotlights the current top piece** (the thing you're building on) above the rest of the stack — telephone's "you see and respond to the last contribution," not "you see the whole finished album." Concretely the palette mixes two kinds:
  - **Base pieces** (a snout, a boot, a fish, a bucket, a worm, a flag) — a thing to stack.
  - **Reactor pieces** (googly eyes, a top hat, a speech bubble, an umbrella, a tiny crown) — pieces whose *whole joke depends on what's underneath*: googly eyes glom onto whatever the top piece is; a top hat perches on it; a speech bubble makes the top piece "talk." A reactor on a fish ≠ a reactor on a boot — the meaning is created by the crewmate who went before you. That dependency is the "absurd evolution during transmission" the brief names (coop-telephone §Lens 1, TL;DR #5) made literal: the fun is *your* piece reinterpreting *their* piece, and a no-show just means the next builder reacts to an older top piece instead — never a block.
- **A turn is one tiny choice, not a drawing.** On your turn the war screen spotlights the **top piece** + shows the Heap-so-far underneath, and a **palette of ~12–16 mud-pieces** (the base + reactor mix above). You tap one piece, optionally tap a **slot hint** (left / center / right lean — three buckets, not freeform x/y, to keep it one enum field and prevent off-canvas grief), and **Place**. That's the whole turn: a single `place_heap_piece(war_id, piece_id, slot)` write. Pick-from-palette (not freeform) is the brief's simplicity rule and is what makes the turn an atomic async action.
- **Cold-start: the Heap is never empty.** Every war's Heap starts with a fixed **ordinal-0 base — "a humble mud pile"** (a render-only constant, not a `heap_pieces` row; the first real piece stacks at `ordinal = 1`). So the very first placer always has a top piece to react to, and a reactor piece can never land on nothing. No special-case "first turn" branch in the RPC — the spotlight just shows the mud-pile until ordinal 1 exists.
- **The opponent teaser (light race tension, zero spoiler).** The panel shows only **"their Heap is N tall"** (`theirPieceCount` from `war_state`), never their pieces — a faint "they're ahead/behind on building" beat that adds the co-op-vs-race combo the brief credits with +71% ARPDAU (coop-telephone §Lens 2, Tycoon Racers) without leaking the surprise or feeding the win score. It's flavor on the *artifact race*, not a second win condition.
- **The capstone: the crew names its Heap (co-authored identity, not just a tower).** On the **final war day** the in-app panel offers a one-tap **"name this Heap"** from a small rotating list of goofy adjective+noun combos (e.g. "The Teetering Snoutling," "Lord Bogwobble") — a `mud_wars.heap_name text` stamped by the first member who taps it that day (idempotent: first-write-wins via `heap_name IS NULL`, no new RPC needed beyond a tiny `name_heap(p_war, p_name)` or a flag on `place_heap_piece`). The name rides the reveal card and the cosmetic label ("Lord Bogwobble — Crew Mudlarks, Spring 2026"), turning the artifact from a stack into a *named thing the crew made together* — the shareable-identity hook (`virality-and-growth-loops.md`) with a crew-authored handle, not an auto-string.
- **No required reciprocity, no streak guilt.** Missing your daily piece costs nothing — the Heap just doesn't grow that slot; the crew keeps building. Gift-framing, never debt (coop-telephone TL;DR #7; coop-mechanics §Pairwise, the Snapchat-streak cautionary tale). Copy celebrates the crew ("your Heap grew a snout!"), never shames the absent.

**How a turn is prompted (push — the heartbeat):**

Async telephone lives or dies on the "it's your turn" nudge (coop-telephone §Lens 1, "Push as the heartbeat"; the Zeigarnik open-loop). TTP already has the exact primitive: **`send_push_to_user(target_user_id, push_title, push_body, push_data)`** — a `SECURITY DEFINER` RPC that reads `profiles.expo_push_token` and POSTs to `https://exp.host/--/api/v2/push/send` via `pg_net` (`20260520050000_push_delivery.sql`, lines 24–60), no-op when the user has no token. So a turn-prompt push is one server call, not new infrastructure. Three push moments, all reusing this primitive plus the inline-`system_announcements` row (so it also lands in the WhileAway/Inbox feed — `components/WhileAwayModal.tsx`, `components/Inbox.tsx`):

1. **"Someone added to your Heap — your turn's open."** When a member places a piece, the *other* crewmates who still have a piece-budget today get a gentle nudge (capped to one per member per day so a 5-person flurry can't spam). This is the pass-along signal — the open-loop the brain wants to close.
2. **Mid-war "your Heap is missing a piece."** A single reminder to members who haven't placed *at all* this war, fired once around the high-CTR evening window (coop-telephone §Lens 1: best windows 6–8pm / 9pm–midnight). Never daily spam.
3. **The reveal push at resolve.** When `resolve_war` fires, every contributor gets "Your crew's Mud Heap is finished — come see it" — the album/reveal moment that is itself the reward.

All three are **savepoint-guarded** and **never use `send_system_announcement`** (admin-gated → would roll back a non-admin's whole RPC; the documented footgun fixed for `donate_to_drive` in `20260619`). Per-day push de-dup is a cheap guard row (or a check against today's `heap_pieces`), so the heartbeat can't become a guilt machine.

**Day-to-day:** open app → tap your usual 20 slings (base war, unchanged) → see the Heap the crew built overnight (and a faint "their Heap is 4 tall") → react to the top piece, stack yours, get a little juice burst → a crewmate gets pinged it's their turn → final day everyone shows to top the Heap and one crewmate taps a goofy name for it → resolve reveals the finished, named sculpture, grants it as a cosmetic, and offers a Share button.

## Scoring & fairness (capped crew points without reopening snowball / alt / free-rider)

**The Heap does not decide the war.** Win/loss is still exactly today's `resolve_war`: per-capita active average (`SUM(slings)/COUNT(active)`, `20260647` lines 617–652), quorum-2 gate, tie/both-below-quorum = no winner. The Heap is a **separate artifact + a bounded reward**, computed off its own tiny ledger; it adds **nothing** to the per-capita comparison. This is deliberate — the research's central fairness finding is "the scoring shape is the whole ballgame" (coop-mechanics §Fairness), and TTP already chose the anti-dominance shape. A second score that rewarded raw piece-count would re-open the exact snowball the per-capita base prevents (a 5-crew out-stacks a 3-crew on headcount).

How the Heap earns a capped bonus without reopening abuse — each rule cites the research:

- **Low binary participation floor, flat ceiling** (coop-telephone TL;DR #3; §Lens 4 "participation floor, not ceiling"; coop-mechanics §Fairness, CoC's "≥1 to claim anything"). The Heap cosmetic + bonus go to members who placed **≥1 piece** this war (the floor, clearable in one tap) — `HAVING COUNT(*) > 0` on `heap_pieces`, mirroring the winner loop's existing `HAVING SUM(slings) > 0` (`20260647` line 666). The reward **does not scale** with how many pieces you placed: placing 1 piece and placing 3 earn the identical Heap cosmetic and the identical flat bonus. No DKP gap, no whale-snowball, no "don't play unless above average" trap.
- **Per-day piece budget caps concentration** (coop-mechanics §Fairness, Clash Royale's daily-reset deck cap; coop-telephone §Lens 2, Brawl Stars tickets). `HEAP_PIECE_PER_DAY = 1`, UTC-day bucketed and use-or-lose, means no single member can build the whole Heap — it *structurally requires* multiple distinct humans across days, the research's "at least one objective that mathematically needs several distinct humans" (coop-mechanics §Fairness checklist). One person can place at most `days` pieces (3 in a 3-day war).
- **Alts stay worthless** (coop-telephone §Lens 4, "capping payout is itself the strongest anti-collusion tool"). Because the reward is flat and floor-gated, an alt that places one piece adds *one identical reward to the alt* and nothing to the main — there's no funnel. And because the Heap doesn't feed the per-capita win score, an alt placing pieces can't even tilt the outcome. The economic incentive to run alts collapses before any fingerprinting (the design-level prevention the research prefers for a small team).
- **Capped completion bonus (cash-faucet lesson).** A win where the Heap reached ≥ `HEAP_MIN_PIECES` pays a **flat, bounded snout bonus** (`HEAP_BONUS`, proposed 30, in the `HOUSE_BONUS = 25` order of magnitude), granted **idempotently** inside the same `FOR UPDATE`-guarded `resolve_war` transaction that already pays the war and stamps `resolved_at` (`20260647` lines 608–612 guard, 707 stamp). It rides that existing idempotency stamp, so a double-resolve can't double-pay. No per-piece minting, no compounding, no per-day faucet — one bounded grant per won war.
- **Anti-collusion + bot neutralization carry unchanged.** The 24h rematch cooldown per crew pair (`challenge_crew` lines 433–438) is untouched. **Bot wars build a Heap for flavor but grant NO cosmetic and NO bonus** — a fixed-pace, re-challengeable bot must never be farmable for a permanent cosmetic, the same reasoning that strips `tickles_earned`/`war_wins`/titles from bot wins (lines 668–673). The Heap grant sits *inside* the existing `NOT w.is_bot_war` branch (lines 688–702), exactly where the title inserts already live.
- **Everyone scores, win or lose; the reveal is for everyone.** Mud (and pieces) are free; a lost war pays no snouts, but **the reveal still happens for both crews** — losers still see and can share the goofy Heap they built (authorship is the payoff even without the win — coop-telephone TL;DR #4–#5). Only the *cosmetic grant + snout bonus* are winner-gated; the artifact + share are universal. Nothing to gain by sandbagging.

Net: the Heap is **strictly better when the crew shows up together** (more distinct hands → a fuller, weirder sculpture → cosmetic + small bonus) but **never required, never snowball-able, never alt-farmable** — every reward is flat, floor-gated, capped, and idempotent, and the win score is the unchanged per-capita base. Exactly "a capped co-op bonus layered on the flat base sling" with the anti-snowball fairness holding.

### Tables / columns

- **`heap_pieces`** — the ordered turn ledger (the primary new table; it is the async telephone chain made durable). Plus one tiny seed/lookup table `heap_palette` (below), and two nullable columns on the existing `mud_wars`:
  ```
  CREATE TABLE public.heap_pieces (
    id        uuid PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    war_id    uuid NOT NULL REFERENCES public.mud_wars(id) ON DELETE CASCADE,
    crew_id   uuid NOT NULL REFERENCES public.crews(id)     ON DELETE CASCADE,
    user_id   uuid NOT NULL REFERENCES auth.users(id)       ON DELETE CASCADE,
    piece_id  text NOT NULL REFERENCES public.heap_palette(piece_id),  -- FK validates the palette key
    slot      text NOT NULL CHECK (slot IN ('left','center','right')),
    ordinal   int  NOT NULL,                       -- 1-based position in this crew's Heap
    war_day   date NOT NULL,                        -- UTC bucket → per-day budget
    placed_at timestamptz NOT NULL DEFAULT now(),
    UNIQUE (war_id, user_id, war_day),              -- enforces HEAP_PIECE_PER_DAY = 1
    UNIQUE (war_id, crew_id, ordinal)               -- stable stacking order per crew
  );
  CREATE INDEX heap_pieces_crew_idx ON public.heap_pieces (war_id, crew_id, ordinal);
  ```
  RLS: `ENABLE ROW LEVEL SECURITY` + a single **SELECT** policy gated on `is_war_participant(war_id, auth.uid())` (the existing helper, `20260647` lines 139–147), mirroring the `mud_slings` policy (173–175). **No INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE policy is created → those are default-deny**, so every write is forced through the `SECURITY DEFINER` `place_heap_piece` RPC (the exact pattern `mud_slings` uses — SELECT-only RLS, mutations via RPC, `20260647` lines 128–131). Add to the `supabase_realtime` publication, guarded (the `20260647` lines 179–181 `DO $$ … EXCEPTION WHEN OTHERS THEN NULL` pattern).
- **`heap_palette`** — the server-side allow-list (one new tiny lookup table, committed mechanism — *not* a CHECK or an inline IN-list, so the FK above is real and the migration is self-contained):
  ```
  CREATE TABLE public.heap_palette (
    piece_id  text PRIMARY KEY,           -- e.g. 'snout', 'boot', 'googly_eyes', 'top_hat'
    name      text NOT NULL,
    is_reactor boolean NOT NULL DEFAULT false,  -- reactor pieces depend on the piece below
    display_order int NOT NULL DEFAULT 0
  );
  ```
  Seeded in the migration with the ~12–16 pieces; `HEAP_PIECES` in `constants/mudFights.ts` is the client mirror. `heap_palette` is world-readable (a static catalog like `hats`) or RLS-off — it carries no user data. The FK on `heap_pieces.piece_id` makes `bad_piece` a constraint error the RPC catches, so validation can't drift between client and server.
- **`mud_wars.heap_winner_id text`** (nullable) — the cosmetic `hats.id` granted to the winning crew, stamped at resolve for the recap/share card. `ALTER TABLE ... ADD COLUMN IF NOT EXISTS`. Under **Strategy A** (the default, below) this is the *single seasonal* `HEAP_COSMETIC_ID` constant — the column records "this war's Heap maps to the Spring-2026 cosmetic"; the *visual* uniqueness is always re-derived from `heap_pieces`, never stored as N catalog rows. (The column earns its keep under Strategy B and as a "this war produced a Heap" marker for the recap query.)
- **`mud_wars.heap_name text`** (nullable) — the crew-authored name from the final-day capstone. `ADD COLUMN IF NOT EXISTS`. First-write-wins (set only `WHERE heap_name IS NULL`), so any crewmate naming it is idempotent and race-safe.
- **No `heap_bonus_paid` flag** — `resolved_at` already gates double-pay idempotently. Lean.
- **The `heap_sculptor` title row** — one `titles` INSERT in the migration so the winner loop can grant it: `INSERT INTO public.titles (id, name, placement, description, source, for_sale, display_order) VALUES ('heap_sculptor', 'Heap Sculptor', 'pre', 'Built a winning Mud Heap.', 'mud_war', false, 213) ON CONFLICT (id) DO NOTHING;`. The `titles_source_check` constraint **already permits `'mud_war'`** (`20260647` lines 247–250, carried from the latest def `20260569`), so no constraint change is needed — but the row must exist before the `user_titles` insert references it.
- **Seed the cosmetic catalog row in `hats`.** The **Heap cosmetic IS a `hats` row**, `cost = 0`, e.g. `INSERT INTO public.hats (id, name, emoji, image_path, cost, category, display_order) VALUES ('heap_spring_2026', 'Mud Heap (Spring 2026)', '🗿', NULL, 0, 'background', 950) ON CONFLICT (id) DO NOTHING`. `cost = 0` → unbuyable (`buy_hat` `not_for_sale`, `20260544` lines 85–86) + un-Troughable (`open_item_drive` `cost <= 0`, `20260582` line 63), verified. Two strategies: **(A, the lean default) one shared seasonal `hats` row** (`HEAP_COSMETIC_ID = 'heap_spring_2026'`) granted to every winning contributor; each crew's Heap *renders* uniquely from its own `heap_pieces` + `heap_name`, so one catalog id backs visually-distinct trophies — catalog stays bounded. **(B) one generated `hats` row per won war** with `image_path` filled lazily by a share-render job — truly unique catalog entries but unbounded row growth. **Ship A**; revisit only if per-crew *catalog identity* (vs per-crew *render*) is ever needed.

### RPC signatures

- **`place_heap_piece(p_war uuid, p_piece text, p_slot text) RETURNS jsonb`** — the one new mutation, modeled on `sling_mud` (`20260647` 550–584):
  - auth check; load war `FOR`-read; if `status='active' AND ends_at<=now()` → `PERFORM resolve_war(p_war)` and return `war_over` (the lazy-resolve trigger, exactly like `sling_mud` 563–566); if `status<>'active'` → `war_not_active`.
  - resolve caller's `crew_id` from `crew_members`; reject if not in this war (`not_in_war`), mirroring `sling_mud` 568–571.
  - validate `p_slot` ∈ enum (the table CHECK backstops it). `p_piece` is validated by the **FK to `heap_palette`** — a bad key raises `foreign_key_violation`, caught → return `bad_piece` (no parallel allow-list to drift; cheaper than an extra SELECT).
  - per-day budget: the `UNIQUE(war_id, user_id, war_day)` makes a second placement same-day raise `unique_violation` → catch and return `daily_piece_used` (cleaner: pre-check `EXISTS` like `sling_mud`'s cap check 572–576).
  - `ordinal := COALESCE(MAX(ordinal),0)+1` for this `(war_id, crew_id)` under the row lock; INSERT.
  - **Fire the turn-prompt push (savepoint-guarded):** for each other crewmate who still has a budget today, `PERFORM send_push_to_user(...)` (de-duped to one/day) + an inline `system_announcements` row. Guarded so a push failure can't roll back the placement.
  - return `{ ok, ordinal, piece_id, slot, top_piece }` for the optimistic client.

## Rewards tie-in (war cosmetics + capped core payout) — connects to War Spoils

The Heap is the **War Spoils item-generator**, not just a single seeded reward. Two tracks, both war-exclusive, both dual-recognized (Destiny Empyrean model: collective unlock + per-member recognition):

1. **The kept Heap cosmetic (the headline + the share artifact).** A won, ≥`HEAP_MIN_PIECES` Heap grants every contributor the war's Heap cosmetic — a `hats` row, `cost = 0`, `category = 'background'` (a "Heap portrait" backdrop) or `'held'` (the Heap carried as a trophy), granted by the `user_hats` upsert (Mystery-Box pattern). Because each crew's Heap is a **unique stack of palette pieces stored in `heap_pieces`**, the *rendered* cosmetic is combinatorially unique per crew per war — the research's "volume from multiplier mechanics, not unique art" (coop-telephone §Lens 3.1–3.2, §Lens 3.8 prompt-variation pipeline) realized at runtime: 14 palette pieces × 3 slots × N stack depth = a vast space of distinct goofy sculptures from one small art batch. This is the standing monument / social proof of the virality research's **shareable identity artifact** — "Mud Heap, Spring 2026, built by Crew Mudlarks," screenshot-and-post ready via the recap's Share button.
2. **MANY war-exclusive cosmetics over seasons (the rarity/season ladder).** Per the resolved design ("rewards = MANY war-exclusive cosmetics… via the ChatGPT/icon-gen pipeline"), the palette itself rotates per season (Spring mud-pieces, Autumn mud-pieces) and the apex Heap-portrait backdrop is **animated** for the top tier (the bog drips, the Heap wobbles) — animation reserved for the apex, recolors/seasonal pieces for volume (coop-telephone §Lens 3.1, 3.7). Named-season scarcity ("Spring 2026 Heap pieces — never re-issued") turns old Heaps into flexes (§Lens 3.6). All cosmetic, non-pay-to-win, equipped via the existing slots — visibility (profile/closet/next to your name) is the whole engine (§Lens 2 cosmetic numbers).
3. **Capped core snout payout (cash-faucet lesson).** A **flat, bounded `HEAP_BONUS = 30`** (`HOUSE_BONUS`-order), paid only to contributors of a completed Heap, **once**, folded into the existing idempotent per-member `UPDATE` (rides `resolved_at`; double-resolve = no-op), leaderboard-shaped (`counter + tickles_earned`). Cap + anti-collusion gate: contributors-only (`COUNT(pieces) > 0`), real-wars-only (bot wars grant neither cosmetic nor bonus), 24h rematch cooldown unchanged. No per-piece minting — one bounded grant per won war. **Server-authoritative + idempotent** (the cash-faucet rule).
4. **Per-member recognition (gentle anti-loafing).** The recap shows which member placed which piece (`heap_pieces.user_id` → attributed in the reveal, telephone's "each fragment attributed" — coop-telephone TL;DR #5) — the lowest-pressure free-rider deterrent, most on-brand for cozy.

The existing `war_winner_regen` blessing (×0.85/72h, `regen_secs_for` 233–237) still fires for *any* win (684–687); the Heap bonus is **additive on top**, capped, so total per-war payout stays bounded and anti-snowball.

**Relationship to [[mudwar-coop-mud-fort]] (the sibling War Spoils plan):** the Mud Fort and the Mud Heap are two designs for the *same* "shared crew artifact → kept cosmetic" slot. The Fort is a **staged structure off the per-capita slings** (a derived progress bar, one seeded cosmetic per season). The Heap is a **telephone-built combinatorial sculpture off its own per-turn ledger** (a unique cosmetic per crew per war + a share artifact). They are **mutually exclusive** for v1 — ship one. The Heap is the right pick when the goal is the *item-generator + virality* tie-in (unique shareable artifacts); the Fort is the right pick for the *minimal-new-surface* option (no new table, building == slinging). This plan recommends the Heap for the War Spoils deliverable precisely because it generates items and is inherently shareable.

## Risks / open questions

- **One seasonal cosmetic row vs one row per won war (Strategy A vs B) — DECIDED: A.** A single seasonal `hats` row (`heap_spring_2026`), each crew's uniqueness lives in `heap_pieces` + `heap_name` and is rendered on demand — catalog stays small; every winner of that season shares one catalog id (fine, since the *rendered + named* Heap differs per crew). B (a generated `hats` row per won war with a lazily-filled `image_path`) gives unique catalog entries but unbounded row growth + a render job. **Ship A.** The only thing A can't do is let a player equip *this specific war's* Heap distinct from another Spring-2026 Heap they also won — revisit B only if per-crew *catalog* identity (vs per-crew *render*) is ever a real ask.
- **Where the bonus/title threshold splits from the cosmetic.** Decided: a **win grants the cosmetic to every contributor regardless of Heap size** (the artifact is the participation reward), but the **`HEAP_BONUS` snouts + the `heap_sculptor` title require `heap_n >= HEAP_MIN_PIECES`** (the "the crew actually built something" gate). This keeps the cosmetic a low-floor participation reward while reserving the snout faucet + prestige title for a genuinely-built Heap. Test (j) pins this split.
- **Rendering the stacked Heap into a single shareable image.** In-app the panel composites piece PNGs by `ordinal`+`slot` (free, reuses the overlay system). The *shareable card* needs an off-screen capture (`react-native-view-shot`-style) on the client at reveal — no server render needed for v1. **v1 ships a static composite**; the animated apex backdrop is a separate, post-v1 icon-gen asset (not a live composite), so animation never gates the launch.
- **Push de-dup / heartbeat tuning.** The turn-prompt must be capped to one/recipient/day or a 5-person crew spams. The guard is cheap (check today's `heap_pieces` for the recipient) but must be in v1, or the heartbeat becomes the Snapchat-streak guilt machine the research warns against. The mid-war "missing a piece" reminder needs *some* trigger — v1 ships **cron-free** (per-placement turn-prompts only); a scheduled evening reminder is an explicit follow-on.
- **Palette size + grief.** 12–16 pieces is enough for variety without an overwhelming picker; `slot` is a 3-bucket enum (not freeform x/y) precisely to keep a turn one tiny write and prevent off-canvas/obscene placements. No freeform text or drawing → no moderation surface (unlike Draw Something / username moderation `20260645`). **The `heap_name` capstone uses a *seeded name-list*, not free text** — same no-moderation-surface guarantee; `name_heap` rejects anything off the list.
- **Degenerate-stack / shared-cosmetic grief.** A within-crew griefer could try to spam one ugly piece to ruin the kept cosmetic for the *whole* crew (it's a shared artifact). Three structural defenses, all already in the design: (1) the **per-day budget caps any one member to `days` pieces** (3 in a 3-day war), so a single griefer can never dominate a multi-day Heap — the math *requires* other hands; (2) **per-member attribution** in the recap (`heap_pieces.user_id`) makes a spammer visible to their own crew (cozy social pressure, on-brand); (3) the palette is **all-cute-by-construction** (no genuinely "ugly"/offensive piece exists to spam — the worst case is a slightly silly Heap, which is the *point*). Crew membership is friends-only + leader-gated (`invite_to_crew` requires `are_friends`, `20260647` lines 306–308), so this is a low-trust-surface, not a public free-for-all. No extra mechanism needed for v1; flag a "report/undo last piece" only if it ever bites.
- **`HEAP_MIN_PIECES` tuning.** Set so a Heap "completes" only with genuine multi-hand effort across days (≈ `2 × QUORUM`); too high and 3-person crews can't finish in 3 days, too low and a single member's 3 daily pieces complete it (defeating the multi-human requirement). Needs the post-launch audit window the base war's tuning got.
- **Cadence (3 on / 1 off).** The 5→3 day change is a literal swap in three sites (`challenge_house` 472, `accept_challenge` 507, `WAR_LENGTH_DAYS` 10). The "1 day off" rest cadence is **not** enforced (no season scheduler); for v1 the rest is informal (the existing 24h rematch cooldown 433–438). A real on/off scheduler is a flagged follow-on.
- **Bot-war Heap.** Decided: bot wars *display* a Heap (so the screen isn't empty) but grant **no cosmetic and no bonus** (anti-farm) — the grant sits inside the existing `NOT w.is_bot_war` branch (688–702). Confirm in review the new upsert is inside that branch.
- **Test gaps (write before push).** Add pgTAP: (a) per-day budget — a second `place_heap_piece` same day returns `daily_piece_used`; (b) `ordinal` monotonic per crew; (c) contributor-gate — a 0-piece member gets neither cosmetic, bonus, nor `heap_sculptor` title; (d) `HEAP_BONUS` idempotency across a double `resolve_war`; (e) bot war grants nothing (no cosmetic/bonus/title); (f) RLS — a non-participant can't read `heap_pieces`; (g) the win score is unchanged with/without a Heap (regression guard); (h) **FK validation** — `place_heap_piece` with a `p_piece` not in `heap_palette` returns `bad_piece`; (i) **capstone race** — two concurrent `name_heap` calls leave exactly one name (first-write-wins, second gets `already_named`); (j) **incomplete-Heap gate** — a win with `heap_n < HEAP_MIN_PIECES` grants the cosmetic to contributors but pays **no** `HEAP_BONUS` and grants **no** `heap_sculptor` (confirm the threshold split).
- **Carry-latest-def + INLINE announcements** — `resolve_war`/`war_state` carried verbatim from `20260647`; all announcements inline (never `send_system_announcement`); the new file sorts after `20260649`. The base `20260647` is unpushed — this is a **follow-on**, not a fold-in.

## Effort

**MEDIUM.** Most of the cost is already paid: the day-bucket/use-or-lose primitive (`mud_slings.war_day`), the lazy idempotent resolve, the winner loop + inline announce + title-insert, the `user_hats` runtime-grant + reveal-payload pattern (Mystery Box), the cosmetic catalog + equip slots (with `buy_hat`/`open_item_drive` already refusing `cost=0`), the `titles`/`user_titles` machinery (the `mud_war` source already permitted), the push primitive (`send_push_to_user`), and the realtime+optimistic client all exist. Genuinely new: **one primary table (`heap_pieces`) + a tiny seeded `heap_palette` lookup + two nullable `mud_wars` columns + RLS + realtime**, **two new mutation RPCs (`place_heap_piece` modeled on `sling_mud`; the trivial `name_heap`)**, **~40 lines folded into the carried `resolve_war`/`war_state`** (cosmetic upsert + bonus + `heap_sculptor` title + reveal copy + the `heap` jsonb block), the **palette art (base + reactor pieces)** (icon-gen pipeline — the real time sink; the **animated apex Heap-portrait is explicitly post-v1** — a static composite of the piece PNGs ships the whole loop, animation is an apex-tier polish pass added later), a **`<MudHeapPanel>` (top-piece spotlight + palette + name chip) + Share capture** on the client, and the **per-day push de-dup guard**. No cron, no change to the win-scoring math. **LOW** on backend novelty (it's `sling_mud` + a sibling ledger + reused grant patterns); **MEDIUM** mostly on palette/apex art production, the share-image capture, push-cadence tuning, and the expanded pgTAP suite.
