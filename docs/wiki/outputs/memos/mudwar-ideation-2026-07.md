# Mud Wars — Season 2 Ideation

Divergent ideation across the four founder-chosen lanes (thin-crew, reward+sink, crew-feel, strategy/monetization), each proposal adversarially pressure-tested against the anti-snowball rubric and the shipped code, then synthesized into one coherent shape with an MVP slice. The pressure-test caught several proposals whose own anti-abuse arguments were false against the shipped SQL — those were revised or parked.

## Season 2 — "Songs of the Bog," one coherent shape

The four lanes are not four features; they are four pressures on **three shared surfaces**: `resolve_war` (the reward/faucet loop + the win-agnostic announcement loop), the **cost=0 cosmetic economy** (`grant_war_spoils_on_resolve`, `user_hats`, `WAR_SPOILS_IDS`), and **tone** (cozy-beats-competitive, earned-over-bought, no shame/FOMO). I verified every load-bearing claim against the shipped code; the synthesis below keeps only what survives that check.

### The spine we will not cross (verified in code)
- **Per-area 12-cap is the anti-lone-ace invariant.** `rhythm_area_holds` (20260668:168-190) computes each crew's per-area hold as `SUM(LEAST(mud, cap))` over `mud_front_pushes` rows, and `fold_rhythm_margin` flips the area on `ch_holds`/`df_holds`. **Anything that adds an extra `mud_front_pushes` row on an area stacks in that SUM and defeats the 3-body coordination gate.** This kills Cover-as-drafted.
- **`counter` is the live spendable wallet.** `buy_hat` deducts from `profiles.counter` (20260501210000:77), as does every Trough/bounty/truffle spend. `resolve_war` MINTS into `counter` on both branches (bot win = `+HOUSE_BONUS`, real win = `own+share`, line 638/642). **Bot wars have NO cooldown** — `next_war_at` is stamped `IF NOT w.is_bot_war` (676). So the bot branch is an infinitely-repeatable solo grind; any *spendable* payout added there is a faucet. This is the OPEN economy-wall precondition.
- **`tickles_earned` write is not dead.** An `AFTER UPDATE OF tickles_earned` trigger (`record_leaderboard_passes`, 20260512:104) fires the "you passed [friend]" Connect feed. Zeroing the war's write silently deletes a Connect artifact — **cap it, don't drop it.**
- **`season` lives only on `mud_war_fronts` (DEFAULT 1), never on `mud_wars`.** Every "per-season COUNT, no new column" pity claim is unbuildable; un-seasoned it's a lifetime-monotonic march to a permanent apex floor. **Any pity/dupe floor must be capped below the apex tier.**
- **The winner loop (634-666) and the spoils trigger (20260660:39) both hard-gate `crew_id = winner_crew`.** But the **resolve-time announcement loop (693-703) already iterates BOTH crews' `crew_members` and only INSERTs `system_announcements`** — no counter touch. That loop is the safe, win-agnostic, faucet-free surface every "everyone scores" and "crew-feel" beat should piggyback.

### SHIP — woven into one design

**A. The reward spine: cap the faucet + open the first sink (this is the rollout gate).**
Ship **P-Cap-and-split (revised)** and **P-Wager-Pit** as a matched pair — they are the two halves of B2 and together make the war net-neutral on money supply, which is *the* precondition to widen the flag past Brian.
- Cap-and-split, with the skeptic's fix: `reward := LEAST(own+share, 60)` into `counter` **AND** `SET tickles_earned = tickles_earned + LEAST(own+share, 60)` (keep the pass-feed firing at the honest capped magnitude — do NOT zero it). Add an uncapped `war_xp` write to a new `war_progress(user_id PK, war_xp, war_dupes)` table as pure cosmetic-economy fuel. Bot wins stay flat `+25`, unchanged. This kills whale-carry and alt-farm payout in one lever (Pillar 3 / #3) while a win still pays real money and a visible progression number climbs.
- The Wager Pit is the **only designed true sink** (money transfers, never mints) and the **only mechanic that pulls non-warring friends in** (Connect, whitespace C8). Ship it on **real crew-vs-crew wars only**, `WAGER_CAP` low, boost-not-buy 2× payout cap, below-quorum full refund, combatant-exclusion, composite PK `(war_id, backer_id)` = one bet per human (anti-alt). Frame it as **"back a friend," never "gamble"** — zero house rake, a "Bog Bookie" flavor line, never a cosmetic. The pit's RAKE (or the pure-transfer loss to losers) is the drain that offsets the capped payout.

Together: money injected per resolve drops from unbounded to ≤300/war, and a real sink pulls it back out. That is the honest proof the economy is safe to flip.

**B. The "everyone scores" valve — ONE cosmetic reward loop, not three.**
The three thin-crew reward proposals (Salvage, Mud Bucket, Wallow-gated cosmetic) all want the same thing: *a kept cosmetic for showing up, win or lose, that never mints snouts.* Do not build three grant paths. Build **one win-agnostic cosmetic pass inside `resolve_war`** that iterates BOTH crews' contributors (`SUM(slings)>0`) via the same both-crews loop that already exists at 693-703, granting from the cost=0 `war_exclusive` pool, idempotent `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`. This is the "Mud Bucket / Salvage" idea, corrected:
- **Rarity-weighted draw** (P2's ladder half — the `hats.rarity` CHECK already carries all 5 tiers, so this is a semantic relabel + weighted pick, zero schema) so most drops are Muddy/Caked and Heirlooms stay rare.
- **Soft-pity + dupe-insurance capped BELOW apex.** Floor tops out at Caked (uncommon); **hard-exclude the 5 legendary/epic-apex IDs** (`golden_mire_bg`, `festival_night_bg`, `heirloom_mire_aura`, etc.) from the consolation sub-pool — apex is earned only by winning. Because there's no `season` column, cap the floor at Caked regardless of streak length. Dupes route into set progress via `war_progress.war_dupes`.
- **Anti-alt gate:** grant to a losing/thin crew only when a distinct-human quorum on that crew is met, OR restrict consolation to real (non-bot) wars. For the bot-heavy beta, the honest answer is: a **solo/thin crew that fights the bot gets ONE bucket for showing up** (cosmetic-only, capped 1/war), but the *pity-floor escalation* requires a distinct-human crew so a single-alt lane can never grind rarity.

This single loop makes SKILL's Collect pillar true for the 27-player bot-heavy population — "what you own says how you've played" — without a snout faucet or an alt farm.

**C. The crew-feel artifact — Mud Fort + Heave-banner, both derived, both free.**
- **Mud Fort (SHIP, verified clean):** a staged fort as a pure derived view of `war_side.perCapita` (already server-returned, already daily-capped so no whale out-builds a tier). Its only server change extends the announcement loop (693-703) — INSERT-only, no counter, no faucet. Fix per skeptic: bind the "N of you raised it" pips to the **`own>0` distinct-human set** (not `mineCommitters`, which can include a zero-throw member), and stamp the top-tier line into the resolve announcement so a name-attributed trace survives the war. This is the cheapest "we built this together" (Cooperate + render-co-presence #6).
- **Heave Tier 1 (SHIP, banner only):** `mineCommitters` already computes the hidden 2-body coordination as an alt-proof distinct-human count. Surface it as a **score-neutral "defending the Gate together today" banner** (not "holding the line" — that overclaims liveness). **Drop every "+1 Wallow bonus" claim** — there is no Wallow meter and building one is redundant with the Fort. **Cut Heave Tier 2** (the 90s live window bends the async-cozy spine #1 and needs a `mud_front_pushes` timestamp that doesn't exist).

Fort + Heave-banner together render the coordination the fold already does but the player never sees — the exact gap #6 names — at ~S cost, zero new tables, zero faucet.

**D. The strategy layer — ship the redeploy picker, split the spectator surface.**
- **Redeploy picker (SHIP, verified, near-zero cost):** `redeploy_member` (20260667:502) writes only `front_key`, force-locks, is `crew_members`-gated, never touches `mud_slings` — pure routing, zero snouts, zero ceiling lift. The RPC, wrapper, and hook all exist and are inert; only a FrontBoard sub-sheet is missing. **The one fix is tone, not code:** frame the live push to the moved member as a named rally — *"Jen, your leader is rallying you to the Wallow"* — so a non-consensual yank of their own pick reads as togetherness (#8 gift-not-guilt), not coercion. This activates the entire designed bluff/counter-read axis for a client-only change.
- **Bog Gallery, split (SHIP the morale half only):** read-only spectate + 1/day named **cheer as pure morale** (Connect #6/#7/#8) is clean. **Cut the "cheer mints a run" half** — `grant_war_access` is `REVOKE ... FROM PUBLIC` and hard-gates the recipient to `is_crew_member` of a participating crew (verified via 20260669's own caller comment), so minting a run for an outside friend needs a new un-crew-gated token path = a run-farming alt vector. The spectate view is also net-new fog surface (`war_fronts_state` returns NULL for non-participants), so it's real work, not "mostly exists" — hence PARK to a later phase, not MVP.

### PARK (with the reason)
- **P-Right-Sized-Horde:** its own anti-abuse mitigation is false in the shipped code (no bot cooldown, `counter` is spendable), so lowering the solo marquee threshold turns a rigged loss into a repeatable +25 mint. **The cheaper, cleaner fix for the same lane is B (a cosmetic bucket for showing up) — which makes the bot war *rewarding* without making the mint *easy*.** Revisit horde-scaling ONLY after the Wager-Pit sink lands and only if bot wars get a cooldown; otherwise it reopens the exact faucet we're closing.
- **P-Cover-the-Wallow:** breaks the per-area 12-cap invariant (the cover row stacks in the hold SUM) and its 12h-stale absence predicate is unbuildable (`last_active_date` is a DATE). PARK; the crew-feel need it targets is met by Fort + Heave-banner more cheaply.
- **P-Bog-Coffer:** ship-worthy only as **pure vanity with the payout code removed entirely** (not flag-off) — the +20-to-`counter` payout is a live faucet on the bot grind. It's an S-effort juice layer; hold it as a fast-follow *after* the reward spine, shipped payout-less. Not MVP.
- **Heave Tier 2, Bog Gallery run-cheer, Bog Coffer payout, Right-Sized-Horde:** all park behind the same gate — they either need a faucet decision that hasn't landed or a schema/plumbing cost the MVP doesn't need.

### The Mire Pass (dual-track) — PARK to post-flag, but pre-committed in principle
It's an L-effort seasonal monetization surface. Its dual-track *identical-rewards* rule is genuinely on-charter (premium buys pace + recolor breadth, never power — consistent with the 2026-06-25 "subscriptions are cosmetic/convenience" decision), and it rides `war_progress.war_xp` from the reward spine. But it is **the last thing to build**, after the flag is wide and the economy is proven net-neutral. Confirm B3=permanent-crews before its set-capstone layer. Do NOT let a monetization surface be the thing that widens the flag.

## Decisions forced (with recommendation)

1. **B2 — payout economy-wall: raw-snout faucet vs war-token wallet vs capped-real-snout. This is the flag-widen gate.**  
   → Capped real snouts (LEAST 60) into counter AND a matching capped tickles_earned write, PLUS the Wager Pit as the drain — NOT a second player-facing war-token currency (the pit needs only internal escrow). A capped mint + a real sink is net-neutral and keeps the wallet single/legible; a parallel token wallet is complexity the beta doesn't need. Do NOT zero the tickles_earned write (it fires the Connect pass-feed) — cap it.

2. **B7 — which co-op/crew-feel mechanic (the proposals framed Wallow-meter vs Mud-Fort vs Heave as partly mutually exclusive).**  
   → Ship Mud Fort (derived view) + Heave Tier 1 (banner) — both free, both additive, not exclusive. Do NOT build the standalone Wallow meter: it's redundant with the Fort as the crew-progress artifact, and the cosmetic it gated is better delivered by the unified consolation pass (win-agnostic, both crews). Fold Wallow's one good idea (a kept cosmetic for a crew that showed up) into that consolation loop instead of a new table.

3. **Thin-crew/solo lane — scale the bot war easier (Right-Sized Horde / Cover) vs reward showing up (consolation bucket).**  
   → Reward showing up, do NOT scale the win easier. Both scaling proposals reopen the faucet (verified: bot wars have no cooldown, counter is spendable) or break the 12-cap invariant. The consolation cosmetic pass makes the bot war rewarding without making the mint easy — same lane, no snowball, no alt farm. Park Right-Sized-Horde until bot wars get a cooldown AND the pit sink is proven.

4. **B4 — population flip-trigger: when to widen the flag past Brian.**  
   → Two-gate it. Gate A (build): Wave 1 lands (cap + pit) — economy is bounded and drained. Gate B (metric): widen to a small friend cohort once the consolation loop + Fort make a bot-heavy war rewarding, then flip global when >=2 real crews each field >=2 distinct-human active members (read distinct humans off the mud_slings composite PK, the anti-alt primitive — never crew_members roster size, which is static/absence-blind/alt-inflatable).

5. **B3 — crew identity permanent vs per-war (blocks the Mire Pass set-capstone layer and the pity ladder's cross-war pacing).**  
   → Confirm PERMANENT crews before Wave 3. Permanent crews are what make 'the crew you keep chase a set together' and cross-war pity coherent, and they deepen Connect (a durable friend-crew, not a disposable lobby). The Wave 1-2 MVP works either way, so this decision can wait — but it gates the Mire Pass, so decide it before Wave 3.

6. **Redeploy override consent — is force-locking a teammate's own front pick on-charter?**  
   → Ship it, but only with the named-rally copy fix. A silent non-consensual yank brushes the no-shame/gift-not-guilt line (#8); framing the live push as 'Jen, your leader is rallying you to the Wallow' (leader's name attached) turns the override into togetherness. Copy-only, no code change — non-negotiable before ship.

7. **Bog Coffer payout — ship with the capped house-funded payout, flag-off, or code-removed.**  
   → Ship pure-vanity with the payout code REMOVED entirely, not flag-defaulted-off. The +20-to-counter payout on the infinitely-repeatable bot branch is a live faucet; a flag is a footgun waiting to be flipped. If a coffer payout is ever wanted, gate it on B2 landing anti-faucet AND route it to a real sink (the pit), never to counter.

## Appendix — all 15 proposals + skeptic verdicts

### [revise] Right-Sized Horde — bot hold-pressure scales to your real defender count  (M) · snowball=low · tone=cozy_ok
*Lane:* Fix thin-crew / solo play — make the bot rhythm war winnable-and-rewarding for a 1-3 person crew without reopening the snowball or the faucet.  
*Source:* novel (quantifies + inverts the C3 self-flagged wall; complements A9 Cover)  

The Mudlarks push exactly as hard as your crew can field: a solo faces a horde a solo can hold, a trio faces a trio's horde — the 12-cap stops being an impossible wall and starts being a coordination target.

- **Mechanic:** Today the bot's marquee threshold is fixed at 0.78*P*deploy_press regardless of who's fighting it, while PER_AREA_CAP=12 hard-caps one body at 12 mud/area. A heavy marquee needs 26.4 -> unreachable below 3 bodies, and bot_front_eff auto-holds 2 of 3 areas (30/marquee, 14/cheapest) for a standing -8/day. Fix: in bot wars ONLY, multiply the bot's per-area hold requirement AND bot_front_eff by a crew-size factor botScale = LEAST(1.0, 0.34*N) where N = the challenger crew's roster size clamped 1..3 (so N=1 ->0.34, N=2 ->0.68, N=3+ ->1.0). Concretely: solo marquee threshold 26.4*0.34 = 9.0 (a single perfect defender banking 12 clears it); duo 26.4*0.68 = 18.0 (two bodies at 12 each = 24, clears with headroom); trio faces the full 26.4 (needs all three, the intended coordination). Same factor multiplies bot_front_eff so the bot doesn't auto-hold two areas against a solo (solo faces bot marquee 30*0.34 = 10.2, contestable). Net: every crew size has a genuine, winnable-with-skill bot war; the 12-cap still forces a trio to actually co-defend at full scale, so the anti-lone-ace lesson is preserved at the tier where you HAVE the bodies.
- **Graft:** Pure bot-branch math change, no schema, no new RPC. In 20260673_bot_scale.sql CREATE OR REPLACE the three functions that already special-case is_bot_war: (1) add `bot_scale(p_war)` = LEAST(1.0, 0.34*LEAST(3, (SELECT count(*) FROM crew_members WHERE crew_id = challenger_crew))); (2) in rhythm_area_holds, wrap the `IF w.is_bot_war` df_deploy/df_mud branches so `df_mud := round(bot_front_eff(rank) * bot_scale)` and the challenger's hold check uses `hold_coef * P * deploy_press(df_deploy) * bot_scale`; (3) mirror the same scale in score_mud_war_days' `df_pc := c_bot_pace` branch -> `df_pc := c_bot_pace * bot_scale` (base_notch). Client: surface botScale as a one-line hint in FrontBoard.tsx ('The Mudlarks match your crew of N') off war_state.mine.members.length — war_state already returns mine.members. Constants: add BOT_SCALE_PER_HEAD=0.34, BOT_SCALE_CAP_HEADS=3 to constants/mudFights.ts. No touch to real-crew (non-bot) fold — the sim-validated path is byte-identical.
- **Rubric fit:** Satisfies #1 (async, missing member -> smaller N -> gentler bot, graceful never griefable), #3 (the FLAT/CAPPED ceiling is retained — 12-cap still binds at N=3; scaling the FLOOR down doesn't reopen a whale lever because bot wars grant only the flat HOUSE_BONUS), #4 (a solo can now actually win-or-lose a real contest instead of a rigged loss). RISK on #3: could a crew shrink to N=1 right before challenging to farm the easy bot? Mitigated three ways — the bot win already grants nothing spendable (counter+=25 only, no leaderboard/title/spoils per resolve_war 655-707), N is read at each daily fold not gamed once, and 24h WAR_COOLDOWN_HOURS caps re-challenge rate. RISK on #5 (togetherness strictly-better): scaling down for solos slightly flattens the 'bring friends' gradient in BOT wars — mitigated because real crew-vs-crew wars (the prestige path) are untouched and still reward breadth via the loser-pot per-capita split.
- **Forces:** B4 population flip-trigger metric — makes head-count a first-class fold input, so the flip metric ('>=2 crews each >=2 active') can lean on the same crew-size read; also informs B6 cadence (a solo's winnable bot war changes how long a bot war should run).
- **⚑ Skeptic:** Its own anti-abuse mitigation is factually false, and that reopens a (small) snout faucet exactly where it makes the win easy. The proposal claims "24h WAR_COOLDOWN_HOURS caps re-challenge rate" and "the bot win grants nothing spendable." Both are wrong in the shipped code: resolve_war stamps next_war_at only `IF NOT w.is_bot_war` (20260668 line 676), and challenge_house gates solely on `already_in_war` + the crew's own (never-set) cooldown — so a solo can re-challenge the house the instant a war resolves, back-to-back, forever. And the bot win increments `profiles.counter` (line 638), which IS the live spendable snout balance (the consolidated brief C1/B2 flags this faucet as "REAL and live today," no sink). So lowering the solo marquee threshold to a single perfect defender (26.4->9.0) turns a rigged loss into a repeatable +25-counter mint with NO real rate limit. FIX: gate botScale<1.0 behind an actual limit — either stamp a bot-war-scoped cooldown the trigger also checks, or make the sub-scale (solo/duo) bot win grant a buff/cosmetic-consolation only (zero counter) so the faucet is genuinely closed at the tier where the win got easy. Secondary fix: bot_scale must read distinct ACTIVE humans (mud_slings composite-PK COUNT, the brief's own anti-alt primitive), not crew_members roster size — roster size is static, absence-blind, and alt-inflatable, which contradicts principle #3. The core idea is sound (async/graceful #1, 12-cap retained at N=3 so anti-lone-ace holds, pure bot-branch math, PvP path untouched, no whale/big-crew snowball reopened, cozy framing) — hence revise not cut.

### [revise] Salvage the Mire — a legible thin-crew loss still banks a cosmetic + soft-pity floor  (M) · snowball=medium · tone=cozy_ok
*Lane:* Fix thin-crew / solo play — make the bot rhythm war winnable-and-rewarding for a 1-3 person crew without reopening the snowball or the faucet.  
*Source:* A13 (catch-up/stall-valve: earn-on-a-loss + soft-pity rarity floor)  

Even a war you lose to the bot pays a Muddy-tier war cosmetic to anyone who threw >=1 mud, and every 2nd loss/draw bumps your next drop's rarity floor — so a stuck solo player accretes the collection instead of nothing.
