# Streak as a second regen multiplier

Adopting a streak mechanic (consecutive-engagement state per player) that multiplies tickle regen rate on an independent axis from happiness, in the 0.75×–1.00× range, raised by tickling within a rolling 36-hour window of the last streak credit, hard-resetting on a 36h gap. Caps at day 30 = 0.75× (25% faster). Surfaced as a Garden — a 5-stage growing visual object in the Barn's ambient layer — with no exposed number.

The motivation is what happiness *cannot* deliver for solo players: meaningful regen reward independent of friends. A solo player at happiness floor sits at 1.13× regen permanently. A solo player at floor + day 30 streak sits at `1.13 × 0.75 = 0.85×` — back below baseline. Streak is the loyalty axis; happiness is the social axis; together they let lonely-but-committed players catch up without forcing friend-acquisition. Companion to ADR-0001.

## Considered options

Each fork below was an explicit alternative we rejected.

**Time-scope — session vs daily vs streak vs drop-entirely.** Picked **streak**. Session-scope rewards the player who's already engaged (rich-get-richer at the worst possible scope). Daily-scope barely improves on happiness's daily login bump. Drop-entirely was on the table; the deciding factor was that happiness's solo-floor permanently penalizes the lonely-but-loyal player, and streak is the cleanest counterweight on a separate axis.

**Day boundary — UTC midnight vs player local vs rolling 36h.** Picked **rolling 36h**. UTC midnight is consistent with the rest of the app but creates timezone-edge unfairness (west coast players cross the boundary mid-evening, every streak-skip looks worse for them). Player-local requires trusting client tz or storing it on the profile, and adds parameters to every streak-relevant call. Rolling 36h sidesteps timezones entirely at the cost of inconsistency with the rest of the app's day mechanics; the 12-hour buffer between 24h and 36h is a built-in grace mechanic.

**Break behavior — hard reset + warn vs streak-freezes vs soft decay vs hard reset silent.** Picked **hard reset with a push-notification warning at hour 24**. Streak freezes (Duolingo-style) add another piece of state and a UI surface explaining "you have N freezes left" — too much weight for a cozy game. Soft decay undermines the "I worked for this" semantics of a streak number. Hard reset without warning is the maximally engagement-pumping variant but a poor fit for TTP's vibe. The 24h push warning compensates for the no-number display (player has no other way to know they're in danger).

**Magnitude — linear-day-30 vs tiered vs logarithmic vs subtle.** Picked **linear cap at day 30, max 0.75×**. Tiered creates cliffs and adds knobs to tune. Logarithmic flatlines too fast — kills the long-term retention purpose of streaks. Subtle (~0.90× max) repeats the happiness-ADR lesson: invisible mechanics are unteachable. Day 30 is the natural month-milestone players already think about; 25% faster is "felt" without compounding dangerously with warm_tea + max happiness (all-buffs ceiling = 0.26× = ~16 min per tickle, still well above the 60s floor and earned by genuine engagement).

**Display — visible number vs growing object vs qualitative tier vs hidden.** Picked **growing visual object** with no number. The happiness ADR locked in "TTP doesn't expose raw numbers" as a design principle, and streak is the one mechanic where convention would say a number is mandatory (Duolingo). Honoring the principle costs precision (player can't see "day 23 of 30") but the push warning compensates for the danger-window question. Qualitative tier was rejected because happiness already uses tier labels (Thriving/Happy/Content/Lonely); a second tier system competes for the same brain-space. Hidden defeats the engagement purpose of picking streak-scope in the first place.

**Placement — Barn ambient vs Account vs modal vs on-Rosie.** Picked **Barn ambient layer** near Rosie. Account is too rarely visited to surface the growing visual reliably. A modal hides the growth by default. On-Rosie (pose, clothing) conflicts directly with happiness, which already owns Rosie's mood overlay. Barn ambient is the layer that's already visible every session without claiming surfaces other mechanics own.

**Streak/happiness composition — same axis vs independent multiplicative.** Picked **independent multiplicative**. Folding into one axis means choosing whose floor wins (the lonely-loyal player either sits at floor or above it, never both simultaneously). Separate axes preserve both signals: a lonely-loyal player at day 30 = `1.13 × 0.75 = 0.85×`, neither at the floor nor above the social ceiling — exactly the "earned-back-to-neutral" state we want.

## Consequences

- `regen_secs_for(uid)` now composes **four independent multipliers** (blessing × curse × happiness × streak). The function body grows, and every future regen multiplier compounds with all three. We need to be careful about the all-buffs ceiling: the current cap of 0.26× lands at ~16 min per tickle, which is well above the 60s floor, but a 5th multiplier could clamp.
- **The push notification at hour 24 is load-bearing.** A growing-visual display gives the player no precise readout of when their streak breaks. Without the push, players will lose streaks they didn't realize were at risk → rage-quit risk. The push infrastructure already exists (`send_push_to_user` per referrals migration), so this is integration work, not new infra.
- **Five new sprite stages required for the Garden.** Buildable via the ChatGPT cosmetic pipeline already in use, but real art work. The metaphor (bonsai? flower bed? hay pile? sunflower?) is deferred to art-time; the spec defines stage count + thresholds, not the visual concept.
- **Garden competes for Barn real estate with future habitat-building (item #6).** If habitat ships later, the Garden has to either coexist with player-placed decoration or migrate to a different anchor on the Barn. Worth picking a Garden position that leaves a habitat-canvas free.
- **Reversal cost is meaningful.** Once shipped, removing streak slows down all players who'd earned the multiplier (same public-facing nerf concern as happiness). Audit the magnitude after 2–4 weeks of public-build data; a linear function is one-change retunable.
- **No retroactive grant.** Players existing pre-launch start at streak=0 on ship day, same as new players. The data exists (`distinct_active_days`) to grant retroactive streaks, but it counts lifetime active days, not consecutive — semantically wrong. A 200-day "skipped every other day" player should not start at streak=200.
