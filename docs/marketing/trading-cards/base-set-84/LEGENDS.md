# The six Legends

## Legend card rule

A **Legend** is the player's top-level pig identity.

- Choose exactly one Legend before building the deck.
- Keep it face up in the Legend zone outside the deck.
- It begins with 12 Cheer and at level 0.
- It defines the deck's two favored styles.
- It has one always-available Legend Ability.
- It gains levels and exposed actions when cards are used for Training.
- It is never drawn, discarded, shuffled, or obtained through a random
  in-game item effect.
- The six base-set Legends are guaranteed play pieces. Premium alternate art
  may be collectible, but may not change the rules.

`Legend` is a card class, not merely a rarity word. Other cards should say
“your Legend” when they refer to the player's outside-the-deck pig.

## Breed inspiration

The designs are **breed-inspired visual characters**, not pedigree claims
about the fictional pigs. The mapping uses visible coat patterns and broad,
positive breed traits:

- Large White pigs are described as inquisitive, friendly, quick to learn, and
  recognizable by white coats and pricked ears.
  ([British Pig Association](https://www.britishpigs.org.uk/breed-information/large-white))
- Large Black pigs are described as extremely docile, hardy outdoor pigs and
  gentle giants.
  ([British Pig Association](https://www.britishpigs.org.uk/breed-information/large-black))
- Hampshire standards use a predominantly black coat with a white saddle or
  belt crossing the front legs.
  ([British Pig Association standard](https://www.britishpigs.org.uk/documents/bpa-standards-of-excellence-hampshire.pdf))
- Gloucestershire Old Spots are white with decisive black spots and are also
  known as orchard pigs.
  ([GOSPBC breed standard](https://www.gospbc.co.uk/the-breed-introduction/breed-standard/),
  [breed history](https://www.gospbc.co.uk/breed-histroy/))
- Oxford Sandy and Black markings are sandy with larger random black blotches,
  a pattern historically nicknamed “plum pudding.”
  ([Oxford Sandy and Black breed standard](https://oxfordsandypigs.co.uk/breed-standards/))

## 001 — Rosie, Heart of the Herd

**Pattern inspiration:** Large White  
**Favored styles:** Brave + Spark  
**Deck identity:** flexible Gear and hand shaping  
**Legend Ability — Loadout Instinct:** Once each turn after you equip Gear,
draw a card, then discard a card.

Rosie is the welcoming all-rounder and the easiest first Legend. Her ability
turns every equipment decision into a small hand-quality choice without
creating raw card advantage.

**Identity accent:** poppy red under the shared coral Legend frame; curious
pointed-ear rhythm; one flower crossing the internal art boundary.

**Illustration beat:** Rosie trots through the cottage garden while three
possible pieces of Gear glimmer along branching paths.

## 002 — Copper, Trailblazer

**Pattern inspiration:** red Duroc and Tamworth families  
**Favored styles:** Brave + Steady  
**Deck identity:** Critter-led pressure  
**Legend Ability — Trail Charge:** The first Critter you attack with each
Battle gets +1 Bash.

Copper wants one decisive Critter attack each Battle rather than an
indiscriminate bonus to the whole board. The ability rewards sequencing and
leaves ready Critters available to protect.

**Identity accent:** trail orange under the shared coral Legend frame; one
forward trail arrow; windblown leaves that never cross the cut line.

**Illustration beat:** Copper charges down a shortcut while fireflies and a
small frog scramble into formation behind.

## 003 — Pepper, Night Watch

**Pattern inspiration:** Large Black  
**Favored styles:** Steady + Spark  
**Deck identity:** Critter protection and damage prevention  
**Legend Ability — Gentle Giant:** The first time a ready Critter protects you
each turn, prevent 1 additional damage.

Pepper turns the decision to keep a Critter ready into the strongest defensive
moment of the turn. The once-per-turn limit prevents wide boards from becoming
an impenetrable wall.

**Identity accent:** moon blue under the shared coral Legend frame; a broad
shadow shield behind the Cheer anchor; sparse firefly points.

**Illustration beat:** Pepper stands quietly on a moonlit boardwalk while small
Critters shelter beneath the arc of a large leaf.

## 004 — Bandit, Quick Hoof

**Pattern inspiration:** Hampshire and British Saddleback belt markings  
**Favored styles:** Brave + Spark  
**Deck identity:** Stunt-to-Gear combos  
**Legend Ability — Beltline Feint:** Once each turn after you play a Stunt,
your Held Gear gets +1 Bash for its next attack.

Bandit converts timing tricks into one temporary equipment payoff. The bonus
expires after the next attack, keeping the Stunt turn explosive without
permanently scaling the weapon.

**Identity accent:** saddle brown under the shared coral Legend frame; one
cream belt crossing the art seam and one sharp Spark notch in the ability rail.

**Illustration beat:** Bandit cuts across the mud derby course, throwing a
ticket ribbon behind while winding up a toy sword.

## 005 — Pickles, Orchard Finder

**Pattern inspiration:** Gloucestershire Old Spots  
**Favored styles:** Steady + Spark  
**Deck identity:** draw planning and deck scouting  
**Legend Ability — Orchard Nose:** At the start of your turn, look at the top
card of your deck; you may put it on the bottom.

Pickles improves the next draw without adding a card. It works every turn,
supports planned Training decisions, and makes an uncertain deck feel
deliberate.

**Identity accent:** orchard green under the shared coral Legend frame; a few
decisive black spots and an apple-leaf curve at the illustration seam.

**Illustration beat:** Pickles noses apart leaves to reveal a hidden patch,
with the next card represented as a glimmer beneath the foliage.

## 006 — Biscuit, Stouthearted

**Pattern inspiration:** Oxford Sandy and Black  
**Favored styles:** Brave + Steady  
**Deck identity:** Head Gear defense and Cheer recovery  
**Legend Ability — Stouthearted:** The first time your Head prevents damage
each turn, restore 1 Cheer.

Biscuit makes defensive equipment proactive. Because recovery triggers only
once each turn and requires prevention, the Legend cannot heal without
participating in combat.

**Identity accent:** oat brown under the shared coral Legend frame; a low,
weighty shield curve and sparse crumb-like speckles confined to the art.

**Illustration beat:** Biscuit braces beneath a barn beam as falling hay and a
Head Gear shield divert danger from the smaller Critters behind.

## Balance matrix

| Legend | Primary engine | Resource affected | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rosie | Equip Gear | Hand quality | Once each turn |
| Copper | Attack with a Critter | Bash | First Critter each Battle |
| Pepper | Protect with a Critter | Prevention | First protection each turn |
| Bandit | Play a Stunt | Held Gear Bash | Once each turn; next attack |
| Pickles | Begin turn | Next draw quality | One top-card decision |
| Biscuit | Prevent with Head Gear | Cheer | First prevention each turn |

Every Legend asks for a different deck-building emphasis, touches a different
resource, and has an explicit frequency limit. None produces unconditional
card advantage or permanent stat growth.
