# Special Character full-art prompt set

Mode: built-in image generation  
Use case: `illustration-story`  
Output: 1024×1536 PNG  
Identity inputs: each pig's production `happy_1.png` sprite

## Shared art stem

Create a premium vertical narrative trading-card painting with the energetic
visual language of a classic 2000s Korean side-scrolling fantasy MMORPG:
saturated storybook color, large readable chibi subject, crisp expressive ink
contours, softly painted layered environment, foreground overlap, diagonal
movement, luminous particles, and a clear action beat.

The result must remain wholly original. Do not copy an existing franchise
character, logo, card frame, or environment. Preserve the referenced pig's exact
coat, markings, eyes, snout, hooves, proportions, and tail. Use one pig only.
No clothing, weapons, extra limbs, text, logos, frame, or watermark.

Composition is portrait 2:3. Keep the pig fully visible in the central upper
portion and leave a calmer lower quarter for the printed rules overlay.

## Character scenes

### Rosie

Rosie bounds toward the viewer down a sunlit cottage-garden path. Oversized
flowers, clover, fireflies, half-buried golden truffles, a cozy village, and
bright morning rim light create a joyful first-adventure scene.

### Copper

Copper leaps across mossy stepping stones above a sparkling forest stream.
Enormous red mushrooms, fern fronds, a tiny trail sign, amber motes, and a
distant tree village frame his shortcut.

### Pepper

Pepper keeps watch on an old wooden bridge in a moonlit reed marsh. Fireflies,
mist, enormous cattails, lily pads, distant lanterns, and a crescent reflection
make the scene vigilant but cozy.

### Bandit

Bandit skids around a festive mud-derby turn. A playful mud fan, pennants, hay
bales, glowing motes, and a distant scoreboard create a mischievous action arc.

### Pickles

Pickles noses aside giant clover leaves and discovers a glowing truffle patch.
Acorns, curled ferns, dew, a tiny frog, stepping logs, and a distant marsh
lantern make the discovery feel hidden and rewarding.

### Biscuit

Biscuit braces happily at a hilltop barn entrance as autumn leaves and hay
ribbons sweep past. A shield-shaped barn sign, warm window, supply sacks, and
rolling fields establish a stouthearted protector at golden hour.
