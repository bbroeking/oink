# Rosie's Loadout

A tiny two-player equipment battler for release tables and quick friendly
matches. Each player needs the same nine-card armory. One nine-up print sheet
makes one complete armory; print it twice for a full game.

## The pitch

Build Rosie a three-piece loadout, reveal it, and see whose gear wins the bout.
Every item has three stats:

- **Bash** — your attack.
- **Guard** — what the other pig must beat to land a hit.
- **Flair** — breaks a bout where both pigs hit or neither pig hits.

There are three equipment slots: **Head**, **Held**, and **Aura**. Every card has
exactly six stat points, so no item is simply stronger than another.

## How to play

1. Each player lays out their nine cards and keeps them hidden from the other
   player.
2. Secretly choose one Head, one Held, and one Aura. Put all three face-down.
3. Reveal both loadouts together and add each loadout's Bash, Guard, and Flair.
4. Your pig lands a hit when your Bash is higher than the other pig's Guard.
5. If exactly one pig lands a hit, that pig wins the bout.
6. If both pigs hit or neither pig hits, the higher Flair wins. Exact Flair is
   a drawn bout.
7. Set the six used cards aside and build again from the remaining equipment.
   Play three bouts. The pig with more bout wins takes the match.

If the match is still tied, play **Sudden Squeal**: each player secretly chooses
one previously used card. Compare Flair, then Bash, then Guard. An exact tie is
a shared win—or an excellent reason for a rematch.

## The nine-card armory

| Slot | Item | Bash | Guard | Flair |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Head | Ticket Taker's Cap | 1 | 3 | 2 |
| Head | Bog Helmet | 1 | 4 | 1 |
| Head | Release Party Crown | 2 | 1 | 3 |
| Held | Toy Sword | 4 | 1 | 1 |
| Held | Mud Shovel | 3 | 2 | 1 |
| Held | Golden Truffle | 1 | 1 | 4 |
| Aura | Shadow Aura | 3 | 1 | 2 |
| Aura | Firefly Aura | 1 | 2 | 3 |
| Aura | Golden Bog Aura | 2 | 2 | 2 |

Release Party Crown, Golden Truffle, and Golden Bog Aura use a special
edge-to-edge **Special Illustration** treatment—one showcase in each equipment
slot. Showcase is purely visual: the cards keep the same six-point budget and
rules as every other playable equipment card.

Each card also carries a small in-world provenance stamp—`MUD CREW`,
`RELEASE NIGHT`, `PATCH FIND`, and so on—plus a handwritten Rosie observation.
These are collectible flavor only and never change the item's stats.

## QR and prize structure

Every physical card uses the same back, carrying the functional Release Party
Crown reward QR labeled `SCAN IN SHOP TO CLAIM`. Scan it with the Shop scanner
to claim the Crown. The redemption landing page provides an App Store path when
the game is not installed. Identical backs keep face-down loadout choices
secret.

- The Crown has a lifetime ten-owner cap shared by the entire card run.
- The first ten eligible accounts to redeem become its owners; printing or
  scanning additional copies cannot increase supply.
- The Crown claim window ends September 1, 2026.
- Do not reuse the QR for another item or place the Crown in random hats, store
  inventory, or any other grant path.
- Mark the ten intended giveaway cards by hand (`1/10` through `10/10`) if the
  release needs deterministic physical scarcity rather than a first-come race.

## Prototype print run

1. Print two identical nine-card armories.
2. Play 20 matches, alternating who explains the rules.
3. Record match time, arithmetic mistakes, drawn bouts, and whether players
   change their second loadout based on what the opponent already spent.

Success target: a first match under eight minutes, no questions after the first
bout, and players deliberately saving equipment for a later combination.

Do not add rarity, booster packs, or card-specific exceptions until the
three-slot loadout decision is fun on its own.

The cards summarize this as `BASH > GUARD = HIT`; if both pigs hit or neither
does, higher Flair wins. A compact color-coded diagram keeps that reminder
visible without turning every equipment card into a repeated rule card.
