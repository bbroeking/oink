## Primary listing (English — United States)

**App name (30 characters max)**  
Tickle the Pig

**Short description (80 characters max)**  
Raise Rosie, tickle with friends, and dig for treasure in a cozy pig world.

**Full description**

Meet Rosie, a cheerful little pig who loves tickles, treasure, and company.

Tickle the Pig is a cozy social game built around small daily rituals. Care for
Rosie, grow your collection, visit friends, and join a Sounder to take part in
shared seasonal adventures.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Tickle Rosie and watch her mood change over time  
• Visit friends and tickle their pigs  
• Dress Rosie in hats, glasses, masks, auras, and backgrounds  
• Dig for Golden Truffles during the Great Hunger  
• Join a Sounder and contribute to shared goals  
• Send blessings, trade tickles, earn achievements, and fill your Field Guide  
• Return for evolving seasons, stories, rewards, and community events

There are no ads and we do not sell personal data. An account and internet
connection are required to play.

Tickle the Pig is not directed to children under 13.
