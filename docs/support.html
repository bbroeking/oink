Common issues

I can't sign in

Sign in uses Apple Sign-In. If the prompt doesn't appear, check:

  • Settings → Apple ID → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple — make sure Tickle the Pig is listed
  • Force-quit the app (swipe up + away) and reopen
  • If you previously used "Hide My Email", that relay email is what we have on file
My tickle count looks wrong

Your tickle bank regenerates one tickle every few minutes (faster with a Warm Tea blessing). If the number seems stuck:

  • Pull-to-refresh on the Barn (drag down from the top)
  • The cap is 25 tickles — once you hit the cap, regen pauses
I can't send a friend tickles

A few reasons this can happen:

  • They're not a friend yet — accept their request first (Friends → Inbox)
  • You already have a pending trade with them — finish or cancel that one first
  • 24-hour cooldown — after a trade is fulfilled between two players, you wait 24 hours before another. Cancelled or declined trades don't count toward the cooldown.
Push notifications aren't arriving
  • Settings → Notifications → Tickle the Pig — make sure "Allow Notifications" is on
  • Inside the app: open Friends tab → grant the permission if prompted
  • Sign-in matters: a brand-new account hasn't registered a push token yet. Sign in first, then notifications start arriving on the next trade/blessing/curse
A friend sent me something but I don't see it

Pull-to-refresh the Friends → Inbox tab. If it still doesn't show after a minute, screenshot the time + sender's name and email us.

I bought something but it's not in my closet

Rare. Email us your username + a screenshot of the purchase confirmation. We'll grant the item manually within 24 hours.

I want to delete my account

Email us from the address on file — we delete your row plus all related rows (friendships, season progress, owned hats) within 30 days. The deletion is permanent.
