# The Great Hunger opening script

Pensive narration for the Season 2 intro. The voice should feel like a warm storybook narrator describing the evil turn of the season: calm, low, sad, and a little ominous. No victory in the intro.

## Runtime Target

About 30 seconds total. Generate video as seven short clips, then stitch them.

| Shot | Duration | Title | Narration | Motion Direction |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | 4s | The Tickle Bloom | Once, the valley glowed with tickles. Little sparks of joy, rising from every puddle, petal, and porch light. | Slow push through golden motes. Warm, drifting, alive. |
| 2 | 4s | Rosie's Dream | Rosie dreamed beneath their glow, safe in the hush before morning. | Gentle push toward sleeping Rosie. Motes orbit softly. |
| 3 | 4s | The Crown Blocks The Moon | But joy has a scent. And far beyond the reeds, something hungry found it. | Low-angle reveal. Crown crosses the moon. |
| 4 | 6s | The Great Slurp | The Great Hunger opened his mouth, and the whole valley began to dim. | Wide spiral of golden motes pulled from barns and flowers into the Hunger. |
| 5 | 4s | The Last Tickle | By dawn, only one small spark remained. Rosie reached for it, but even that light slipped away. | Rosie reaches. The last mote flickers from gold to grey. |
| 6 | 4s | The Quiet Valley | Then she saw the truth. It was not only her barn. Every home had gone quiet. | Slow pan across opened barns and sad pigs. |
| 7 | 4s | The Hoard On The Hill | High on the hill, the Hunger kept what he had stolen. And the valley waited for someone brave enough to win it back. | Rosie from behind. Distant hoard glows. End unresolved. |

## One-Piece Narration

```text
Once, the valley glowed with tickles.
Little sparks of joy, rising from every puddle, petal, and porch light.

Rosie dreamed beneath their glow, safe in the hush before morning.

But joy has a scent.
And far beyond the reeds, something hungry found it.

The Great Hunger opened his mouth,
and the whole valley began to dim.

By dawn, only one small spark remained.
Rosie reached for it,
but even that light slipped away.

Then she saw the truth.
It was not only her barn.
Every home had gone quiet.

High on the hill, the Hunger kept what he had stolen.
And the valley waited for someone brave enough to win it back.
```

## Audio Direction

- Narrator: pensive, warm, low, storybook, not trailer-boomy.
- Music: soft lullaby at first; subtle low strings or muted piano after Shot 3.
- SFX: shimmer motes, soft sleeping barn air, distant crown glint, huge comic slurp, final low golden hum.
- Final beat: hold the unresolved silence for half a second before CTA/title.
