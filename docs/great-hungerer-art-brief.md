# The Great Hungerer — season boss + minion art brief

Season 2 antagonist. A giant gluttonous **Hog** — a dark mirror of Rosie who forgot
how to feel joy and now hoards it. Cozy, charming, ultimately-not-bad. All art must
sit in the same picture book as Rosie (kawaii-sticker aesthetic).


## Batch 1 of 3 — The Great Hungerer (hero boss)

```
Draw THE GREAT HUNGERER in the exact kawaii-sticker style of the attached pig (Rosie).

He is an ENORMOUS, round, over-stuffed HOG — the same species as Rosie but giant and
gluttonous. Details:
- Pale-pink hide like Rosie, but SPLATTERED with brown mud smudges.
- Cheeks bulging, packed full of truffles (a few crumbs on his chin).
- One front trotter clutching a big clump of chocolate-brown truffles to his chest.
- A little white cloth NAPKIN tucked under his chin like a bib.
- A TOO-SMALL golden crown tilted on top of his head (comically undersized).
- Expression: sheepish, greedy, and a little SAD — you can tell he is lonely. NOT angry,
  NOT menacing, NOT a scary monster. A charming glutton with soft, pleading eyes.

Same bold clean outline, white sticker halo, glossy highlighted eyes, blush cheeks, and
soft cel shading as Rosie. Full body, centered, transparent background. He should look
huggable — like the audience will forgive him by the end.
```

## Batch 2 of 3 — Hungerlings (hog variant)

```
Draw THREE "HUNGERLINGS" — the Great Hungerer's little minions — as a horizontal row of
three small HOGS, in the exact kawaii-sticker style of the attached pig (Rosie).

They are tiny round mud-splattered piglets, hungry and mischievous but CUTE. Each clutches
a single truffle. Three distinct poses left to right:
1. Scurrying (running with a truffle held out front).
2. Stuffing its cheeks (both trotters shoving a truffle into a bulging mouth).
3. Peeking (crouched, wide glossy eyes peering up, hiding a truffle behind its back).

Same bold clean outline, white sticker halo, glossy highlighted eyes, blush cheeks, soft
cel shading as Rosie. Each on a transparent background, evenly spaced in one row.
```

## Batch 3 of 3 — Hungerlings (goblin variant)

```
Draw THREE "HUNGERLINGS" as a horizontal row of three little GOBLINS, but RE-DRAWN in the
soft kawaii-sticker style of the attached pig (Rosie) — NOT the gritty style of the attached

They are hungry and mischievous but CUTE, each clutching a single truffle. Three distinct
poses left to right:
1. Scurrying with a truffle held out front.
2. Stuffing its cheeks with a truffle.
3. Peeking up with wide glossy eyes, hiding a truffle behind its back.

Each on a transparent background, evenly spaced in one row. They must look like they belong
in the same picture book as Rosie — adorable green imps, not scary monsters.
```
