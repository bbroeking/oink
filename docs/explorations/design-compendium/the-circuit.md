# The Circuit
> Walk your pig down the county-fair midway, win ribbons in every contest, and climb the pens from scrawny Piglet to Grand Champion — at your own pace, with a soft place to land.

**Tier:** Long-term · **Effort:** L (MVP small) · **Mode:** Async, server-settled, no live realtime — instant Ribbon Point accrual at the contest-result chokepoint + weekly `pg_cron` pen settle + seasonal promotion folded into `finalize_season()` · **Depends on:** at least one Ribbon-emitting contest must ship first (the Snout Oracle is the doc's recommended first source; the Schism Front MVP and `finalize_season()` season-key plumbing should exist). Standalone-capable on the existing alignment loop, but it RANKS modes rather than giving them collective meaning, so it must land *after* the Front, never before it.

## The fantasy
You are showing your pig on the county-fair circuit — not fighting a war, just trying to win ribbons and move up to a nicer pen with better company. Every contest you enter (no matter which one) earns Ribbon Points that quietly build toward the next tier, and at season's end you find out whether your pig got promoted to the Heifer Hall or the Grand Champion's Barn. It is earned mastery without cruelty: you never get knocked back to dirt, the worst that happens is you stay where you are, and the very best pigs get a permanent portrait in the Hall of Champions that no reset can ever erase.

## Player loop
- **Daily / per-contest:** You do what you already do — cast in the Oracle, enter the Pageant, throw down in the Mud-Off. Each contest result you earn already produces a placement; The Circuit's only addition is that the same result emits **Ribbon Points (RP)** into a season-scoped per-player tally. You see a small "+RP" confetti and a pen progress chip ("you're 6th of 28 in the Clover Pen") — the daily act feels responsive, like the Front's instant Tide bump.
- **Weekly (the rhythm):** Every Sunday a `pg_cron` tick settles your **pen** — a fixed cohort of ~30 players in the same tier. It writes the week's standings snapshot and INLINEs a personal "your pen this week" dispatch ("you held 4th — two ribbons from the promotion line"). This is the appointment, mirroring the Schism Tally; it is the same nested-clock discipline (instant → weekly → seasonal) the Front uses so nothing new becomes daily-mandatory.
- **Seasonal (the climax):** At Judgement Day, `finalize_season()` calls `settle_circuit_season()`. The top slice of each pen is **promoted** one tier; everyone else **stays** (MVP = promotion-only, no relegation at all). Promotions grant a dated **rosette cosmetic** that recolors per tier and year-stamps a title. Grand Champions are written into the permanent **Hall of Champions** ledger that never resets. The RP score resets for next season; the record never does.

## Mechanics
- **Ribbon Points (RP):** the single cross-mode currency of the ladder. Every contest result maps its placement to RP through one award helper, `award_ribbon_points(uid, source, placement, field_size)`, so all modes share one scoring frame. Suggested base table (tunable): **win = 30 RP, 2nd–3rd = 15, top-quartile = 8, participation = 3.** RP is reputation, not currency — it is never minted, traded, or purchasable.
- **Pens:** a season is partitioned into **pens of ~30** (`CIRCUIT_PEN_SIZE = 30`, Duolingo-league shape). On a player's first RP of a season they are seeded into the lowest open pen at their current tier via `circuit_join()` (clone-of `choose_allegiance()` lock-once: one pen per player per season, idempotent). Pens fill lowest-first so newcomers cluster.
- **Tiers (6, county-fair framed):** `piglet → shoat → blue_ribbon → heifer_hall → champion → grand_champion`. New players start at `piglet`. A pen only ever contains one tier.
- **Promotion (the ONLY movement at MVP):** at season settle, the **top 7 of each ~30-player pen** (≈ top quartile, `CIRCUIT_PROMOTE_N = 7`) advance one tier. `grand_champion` is terminal — its top finishers re-earn the renewing rosette rather than promoting.
- **Relegation (DE-FANGED, off at MVP; opt-in only if ever added):** the research names competitive relegation as the single most dangerous structure for a cozy game. Guardrails baked in so it can never bite: (1) **promotion-only at MVP** — nobody ever drops; (2) if relegation is later enabled it is **soft-landing** — only the *bottom 3 of 30* who scored **0 RP all season** drift down one tier, and never below `shoat` (a permanent floor above the bottom); (3) it is **opt-in** via a profile flag, default off; (4) it is **never global** — only your ~30-pen, never a server-wide rank.
- **Cooldowns / caps:** none of its own — The Circuit imposes zero new daily windows. It inherits each source contest's existing cooldown (Oracle cast cadence, Pageant daily entry, etc.), so it cannot become a chore-stack. A per-source **daily RP cap** (e.g. 60 RP/source/day) blocks grind-farming a single mode.
- **Edge cases:** mid-season joiners seed into a fresh low pen (no back-fill into settled standings); a pen that never fills past ~12 players merges with the next-lowest open pen at the weekly tick; ties at the promotion line break by **earliest-RP-earned timestamp** (rewards the pig who showed up, not the late surger); a player who earns 0 RP all season simply stays put (MVP) — never punished, mirroring the Front's "missing days never punishes."

## Schema sketch
Migration prefix **≥ 20260624000000** (must sort after `20260623000000`).

- `circuit_pens(id bigserial PK, season_key text, tier text CHECK IN (...6 tiers), pen_index int, created_at timestamptz)` — the ~30-player cohorts.
- `circuit_members(season_key text, user_id uuid REFERENCES auth.users, pen_id bigint REFERENCES circuit_pens, tier text, ribbon_points int NOT NULL DEFAULT 0, first_rp_at timestamptz, PK(season_key, user_id))` — lock-once membership + the running RP tally. *Clones `choose_allegiance()`'s one-row-per-player-per-key lock.*
- `circuit_weeks(season_key text, week int, pen_id bigint, standings jsonb, PK(season_key, week, pen_id))` — weekly snapshot. *Clones `schism_weeks` from the Front.*
- `hall_of_champions(season_key text, user_id uuid, tier text, final_rp int, resolved_at timestamptz, PK(season_key, user_id))` — the **permanent** ledger, never reset. *Clones `season_finales` + the Front's `schism_seasons`.*
- **RPCs (all the real `rpc<T>()` shape):**
  - `circuit_join() -> jsonb` — lock-once seed into lowest open pen. *Clones `choose_allegiance()`.*
  - `award_ribbon_points(p_source text, p_placement int, p_field int) -> void` — SECURITY DEFINER, **called from inside each contest's settle RPC** (the chokepoint, exactly like `shift_alignment` is `PERFORM`ed inside `send_blessing`/`send_curse`). Enforces the daily per-source cap; `UPDATE circuit_members SET ribbon_points = ribbon_points + delta`.
  - `circuit_status() -> jsonb` — STABLE, SECURITY DEFINER, GRANT authenticated. Returns caller's tier, pen standings (rank, RP, distance-to-promotion-line), pen size. *Clones the `alignment_leaderboard(per_side)` table shape.*
  - `settle_circuit_week() -> jsonb` — SECURITY DEFINER, **not** granted to authenticated (cron only); writes `circuit_weeks`, INLINEs a per-member `system_announcements` row. *Clones `settle_schism_week()`.*
  - `settle_circuit_season() -> jsonb` — SECURITY DEFINER, **CALLED from inside `finalize_season()`**, wrapped in its own `BEGIN/EXCEPTION` so a ladder bug can never roll back the per-player verdict + alignment reset (the doc's over-coupling guardrail). Promotes top-7-per-pen, grants rosette via `user_hats ... ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` + title via `user_titles ... ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`, writes `hall_of_champions`, resets `circuit_members.ribbon_points` for the new season.
- **Cron:** `cron.schedule('circuit-week','0 13 * * 0', $$SELECT public.settle_circuit_week()$$)` — Sunday, riding the exact `cron.schedule` idiom from `20260579000000_judgement_day_cron.sql`. No new seasonal cron — it chains off the existing Judgement Day cron through `finalize_season()`.

## Economy
- **Tickle faucets:** none new beyond a tiny banded promotion consolation, paid only through `grant_tickles()` (the only over-cap-safe faucet) — e.g. **5/15/30** tickles for promote/hold/Grand-Champion, a rounding error vs the home loop, exactly mirroring the Front's banded schism consolation. The first such over-cap grant must ship the `GREATEST(...)` display-debt fix in `home_stats` + `admin_tickle_overview`.
- **Snout flow:** RP is **not** snouts and never converts to them. The promotion snout reward reuses `finalize_season`'s existing pattern (`UPDATE profiles SET counter = counter + snouts`) at modest values (e.g. Grand Champion 250, promote 100), a counter→counter-style payout, not a mint — and capped by the small fixed promote count per pen.
- **Cosmetic rewards:** the dated **rosette** ships once per season per tier via `user_hats ... ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` (cost 0, non-purchasable, recolors per tier + year-stamped title), identical to the Front's "ship-once dated cosmetic" pattern.
- **Why it can't inflate:** RP is a reputation tally, never a currency, never minted into the closed economy. The only economy touchpoints (small snout payouts, banded tickle consolations) are bounded by a fixed promote-count per fixed-size pen, so the total payout scales linearly with player count and cannot runaway. No tradable market exists — sidesteps the Neopets inflation trap by construction, same as the Front.

## Anti-abuse / cheat model
- **Server-authoritative RP only.** RP is never client-supplied; it is `PERFORM`ed inside each contest's already-validated settle RPC via `award_ribbon_points`, so a player cannot mint ribbons without actually placing in a server-validated contest. (Cheat tier: client-state forgery — fully closed.)
- **Per-source daily RP cap** (≈60/source/day) defeats the farm-one-mode grind (cheat tier: repetition/grind abuse).
- **Collusion / vote-gaming** is inherited from each source contest's own anti-abuse (e.g. the Pageant's vote validation, the Oracle's pari-mutuel sink); The Circuit adds no new social-voting surface of its own, so it imports no new collusion vector. Because pens are **only ~30 and never global**, a colluding ring can at most game its own small pen's promotion — and promotion-only means the players they out-grind are not harmed, only un-promoted.
- **Smurf / alt seeding:** `circuit_join()` is lock-once per `(season_key, user_id)` (clone of `choose_allegiance()`), and pen seeding is server-side lowest-first, so an alt cannot pick a soft pen.
- **Tie-break by earliest `first_rp_at`** removes the incentive to sandbag-then-surge at the deadline.

## Feel
- **Earned mastery** — the named tier you climb is a legible, durable proof of skill across every mode; the pen progress chip makes each contest visibly move the long needle (the `evoke-online-game-feel` "earned mastery" lever).
- **Belonging** — your ~30-pen is a small, knowable cohort (Duolingo-league shape) you recognize week to week, the "hangout / small persistent crowd" lever, far warmer than a global ladder.
- **Slow time + persistent-world FOMO** — the weekly settle and seasonal promotion are clocks you cannot rush; the Hall of Champions is permanent history.
- **Cozy guardrail (load-bearing):** promotion-only, soft-landing-if-ever, opt-in, never-global, never-punishing. Loss is "you stayed in a pen of friends," never "you got demoted in front of the server." The county-fair framing keeps the voice playful (ribbons, rosettes, pens), not the vicious Elo-ladder tone the research warns is the single most dangerous structure for a cozy game.

## How it composes
- **Framed by the Schism Front (the meta-frame):** The Circuit is explicitly the *optional later side-rail* — it gives the modes a personal-mastery axis, while the Front gives them collective meaning. The two are orthogonal: a Pageant win pushes the **Tide** (collective war) *and* earns **RP** (personal ladder). The Front answers "which side is the world on"; The Circuit answers "how good is my pig." It must ship after the Front so it ranks within a world that already has shared stakes, not instead of them.
- **Absorbs every contest as an RP source** — Oracle, Pageant, Mud-Off, mini-games each gain one `PERFORM award_ribbon_points(...)` line at their settle chokepoint, the same minimal-edit pattern the Front uses for `shift_alignment`. New modes opt in for free.
- **Feeds the permanent halls** — the Hall of Champions sits beside the Hall of Schisms as readable, dated world-history; its rosettes can pay into the complementary Snout Almanac / Hog Line trophy room.

## MVP
Promotion-only, **single source, single pen tier** — proves "any contest result climbs a durable named ladder" with no map, no relegation, no multi-tier settle:
1. **One migration** (`20260624000000_circuit_mvp.sql`): `circuit_pens` + `circuit_members` + `hall_of_champions`; `award_ribbon_points()`; `circuit_join()`; `circuit_status()`.
2. **One chokepoint edit:** a single `PERFORM public.award_ribbon_points(...)` line inside the first shipped contest's settle RPC (the Oracle's resolve).
3. **One component:** a Barn-Exterior **pen-standings strip** — "Clover Pen · you're 6th of 28 · 2 ribbons from promotion" (clone `AlignmentBadge` / the Front tug-of-war strip styling).
That alone delivers earned mastery + belonging. **Increment 1:** the Sunday `settle_circuit_week()` cron + "your pen this week" dispatch. **Increment 2:** the 6-tier ladder + promotion folded into `finalize_season()` via `settle_circuit_season()` (rosette + Hall of Champions). **Increment 3 (optional, gated):** opt-in soft-landing relegation, only if the promotion-only ladder proves it needs downward pressure.

## Risks & open questions
- **Weakest meta-frame of the long-term concepts.** It ranks the modes rather than giving them collective meaning — confirm it ships only as a side-rail *after* the Front, never as the spine.
- **Relegation is a live hazard even de-fanged.** Confirm MVP stays strictly promotion-only and that any future relegation is opt-in + soft-landing + floored + pen-local. One mis-tuned demotion notice could read as punishing in a cozy game.
- **Pen-fill at low population.** With a small player base, ~30-pens may never fill; confirm the merge-small-pens rule and whether `CIRCUIT_PEN_SIZE` should start smaller (e.g. 15) and grow.
- **Solo-dev content cadence.** The renewing rosette is one recolor + year-stamped title per season (cheap, like the Front's dated cosmetic) — but the 6 tier names, the RP→placement table, and the dispatch templates are authored-once content. Confirm the templated weekly dispatch (no hand-written paragraph required) is acceptable so it adds zero recurring labor.
- **Source dependency.** The Circuit is inert until at least one RP-emitting contest ships. Confirm the Oracle (or whichever contest lands first) is the seed source and that its settle RPC is the chokepoint.
- **Tie-break fairness.** Confirm earliest-`first_rp_at` is the right tie-break (rewards showing up early) versus most-recent activity.
