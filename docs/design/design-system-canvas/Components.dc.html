★ 10
Glyph · Icon · PigStage · HangingSignsTabBar
shipped
Glyph (54 hand-drawn PNGs) is pictographic: feelings, objects, rewards. Icon (SVG, 2.4 stroke) is functional: check, x, plus, arrows, search, lock, share, tab signs. The dingbat ruling: ★ ✦ · are typography; ✓ ✕ ♥ are Icon/Glyph. No emoji, ever — 3 files still carry one.
Icon drift to close: Icon.tsx imports MaterialCommunityIcons and Feather beside its own paths, and holds 36 raw hex values. Rule: one stroke style, colors from UI_COLORS, vector-icon families retired.
PigStage is the one pig renderer (sprite/Rive/raster behind one contract): living surfaces sync anchors to the frame; product shots freeze at rest. HangingSignsTabBar is the navigation signature — sway spring, five signs, never a flat system bar.
