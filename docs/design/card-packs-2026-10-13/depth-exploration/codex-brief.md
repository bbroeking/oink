# Card depth concept brief

The user wants Codex CLI followed by Midjourney visual examples, drawing inspiration from impressive digital card games. Explore characters and real 3D models projecting out of a card with playful spring motion.

Produce a concise concept direction and exactly three Midjourney-ready prompts. Do not change application code, run tools, install software, or claim to generate images. Output Markdown only. This is art ideation guided by the already established private Claude Design canvas, not a replacement interface implementation.

Established design: Tickle the Pig / Rosie's Loadout, three cards per pack, six Pig Legends, standard framed cards with full-art later. Warm storybook bog, cream #fbeee2, parchment #fffaf0, cocoa #2a1f15, coral #F17868, sage #c9dec1, lilac #d6c8f0, butter yellow #ffd87a. Fredoka Bold names, Nunito rules, Caprasimo stat numerals. Keep title, rank/Cheer, illustration, stat sockets, Play, Training/Legend ability, and collector line in fixed readable zones. Art may breach its inner frame and selected outer edges but never cover the name or rules. Use the attached screenshot for the existing layout, not as the quality ceiling.

Three treatments:
1. Layered storybook: a dimensional cut-paper Rosie and foreground leaves extending above the art rim; obvious near/mid/far depth, tactile warm shadows.
2. Living Legend: a rounded sculpted pink pig actually emerging from the illustration window, front trotters resting on the lower art rail, ears and snout crossing the rim, matte hand-painted shading, body in a charming upward bounce. Strong silhouette and depth, a premium collectible game object, no aggressive realism.
3. Bog diorama: card as a shallow miniature terrarium with meadow grass, clover, tiny glowing fireflies, Rosie leaning out into screen space, layers and soft contact shadows, strongly legible printed card hardware.

For each, specify concept composition, motion translation (anticipation, spring, settle, tilt), and one prompt showing a polished single framed card at a slight three-quarter angle on a warm uncluttered background. Avoid copying exact commercial card frames/logos. No hands, devices, casino styling, neon, glossy plastic, or dense illegible text. Only ask generator for short labels; typesetting is a later deterministic overlay. Include aspect ratio 3:4 and stylize 150, omit model-version flag so the currently available Midjourney default can be used. Do not claim generated raster images are meshes or working animation.

Project already has static Rosie GLBs and one custom-rig walk GLB, but no native GL renderer. This concept pass must not imply native integration is done. Suggested eventual first proof: one Rosie Legend in a separate browser prototype with real mesh, transparent 3D viewport, native/DOM text overlay and spring bounce.
