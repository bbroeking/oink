# Barn Furnishing Expansion: 100 Designs

> Status: **implemented, pending release.** All 100 designs have generated transparent artwork, runtime assets, and thumbnails. The catalog and deterministic collection milestones are implemented; player rollout remains gated.

This expansion adds ten cohesive furnishing collections without adding room themes, placement categories, random drops, Motes, wagering, or real-money acquisition. Each collection has eight always-available Snout purchases plus two deterministic collection awards. Prices use the proposed defaults: common 50, uncommon 100, rare 175 Snouts.

## Art and rollout recommendation

Produce one collection at a time in two batches: first its eight paid pieces, then its ceiling and rug milestone rewards. Review all ten silhouettes together on the normalized Barn camera before export, especially the four floor pieces that must read clearly in both left and right floor positions. Ship behind catalog activation data only after every asset, thumbnail, accessible name, and placement check is complete. Keep milestones disabled until server-side idempotent grant receipts and explicit collection ownership checks exist.
