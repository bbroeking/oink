# Round 2: Bullion's Market — sets, straight-up swaps, and outlets

Context: cozy iOS pig game. 12 "finds" (8 common, 3 uncommon, 1 rare tin whistle) dropped by digging (~0.9 per dig, 1-3 digs/day, rarity 70/25/5), kept in an unbounded bag. Bullion's Market is a pig-to-pig board (no NPC market maker: an earlier critique showed moving NPC prices mint currency). Money = tickles; every tickle a buyer pays goes to a seller. Existing demand ("outlets"): the Ghost Sheep NPC visits each pig once a day for 6 h and buys finds at 3/8/20 by rarity, x2 for the one find he fancies that day (different per pig), a pair in one visit pays half again, three alike trade up one rarity; a friend's pig "wishes" for one find and handing it over pays both pigs 3.

The founder's new direction:
1. "The optimization should be to sell high to others. We need to think about other outlets." (more demand, so buying from other pigs at a good price makes sense)
2. "We need to trade in sets of 3 or 4 of the item." (lots, not singles)
3. "We can also trade straight up." (find-for-find barter, no tickles)

My current proposal:
- A set is a fixed lot per find: commons trade in 4s, uncommons in 3s, the tin whistle singly. An offer is one set at one price. (Alternative: the poster picks 3 or 4.)
- Straight up: an offer can ask for finds instead of tickles: "4 pinecones for 1 old key" (give one set, ask for 1 to 4 of one other find). Anyone holding what's asked can take it.
- New outlet, Bullion's Orders: each day three "orders from town" want a set ("the miller wants 4 red berries for a pie: 18 tickles"), the same for every pig, each pig can fill one order a day. The same order for everyone pushes that find's price up for the day, so holders can sell high to pigs racing to fill it.
- Other outlets to surface on each find's page: the Sheep (fancy, pairs, trade-ups), friends' wishes, maybe recipes (a set + a set makes a cosmetic) and the herd's seasonal Larder taking sets.

Give me, in under 600 words, bullets:
1. What breaks or confuses in sets-per-rarity and in straight-up swaps (thin markets with only ~1-2 finds/day per pig? whales? griefing? alt farming?). Fixed lot vs "3 or 4": which and why.
2. Bullion's Orders: is it a sound outlet? How to bound it (it mints tickles), what numbers, and how to avoid a stampede that only the fastest pig wins.
3. Two or three other outlets you'd add that create demand for SETS specifically, ranked by how much buying-from-other-pigs they'd cause per unit of build effort. Be concrete.
4. Anything that makes "sell high to others" feel good for the seller without feeling like a rip-off to the buyer (cozy game: losing must still feel warm).
