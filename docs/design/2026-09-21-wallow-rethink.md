# The Wallow, rethought from the start — 2026-09-21

Companion to the canvas `https://claude.ai/artifact/2CWhqfXMHDjkDBtgUnUz55` (page 1: the rank sheet;
page 2: prestige gear). This document walks three premises for what a Wallow *is* to the player and
resolves the tree under each: what a rank grants, where prestige lives, what the sheet shows, what
happens past W6, what it costs, which pillar it serves.

## Where we stand

- **Spec (July 2026):** a Wallow is momentum, not a payout — one full pass of XP becomes permanently
  faster tickles and a public W-rank; a gold aura on portraits; a sparse Prestige Path per lap.
- **Layered on since:** six grant-only wearables (W1–W6, three of them hats), six Barn decor gifts
  (also sold for 50–175 snouts), inscribed keepsakes at W1 / W3 / W6 / W10, five aura rings (W6 reuses W5).
- **Production, 2026-09-21:** 90 pigs. W0: 77 · W1: 2 · W2: 2 · W3: 3 · W5: 1 · W7: 2 · W9: 2 · W11: 1.
  Five pigs are past the last ring, wearable, gift and clock step. They kept wallowing for the number.

## Premise A — the clock

**A Wallow buys a permanently faster tickle clock. The rank is the receipt.**

- **A rank grants:** the clock step only (refill 1h → 45m → 30m → 27m → 24m → 21m → 18m; visits 8h → 3h).
  Past the cap, nothing. The ladder honestly ends at W6; the number keeps counting.
- **Gear:** no longer a rank grant. The six wearables go to the Prestige Path's Mystery Hat Box pool;
  the Barn decor gifts stay shop items; keepsakes stay as the Barn's own milestones.
- **Ring:** one style, a W-numeral badge on portraits. No stages.
- **Lives:** the Season tab (the Wallow card already states before/after). The Me row is one number.
- **The sheet:** a clock card — `1 tickle / 45m · visits every 7h`, the next step, the XP bar. Nothing else.
- **Past W6:** "your clock is at full blaze"; the number climbs.
- **Cost:** deletes content; cheapest of the three.
- **Pillar:** none strongly. It is a private power curve. Friends never see it.
- **Risk:** the W9 and W11 pigs have nothing to show, and nothing to show *to anyone*.

## Premise B — the public rank

**The W-rank is the pig's lifetime standing, worn where other pigs look. The clock is the private perk.**

- **A rank grants:** a mark others can read. Three marks already exist and only one scales:
  - the **ring** on portraits (stages 1–5, then repeats) — needs a rule past 5;
  - the **inscribed keepsake** in the Barn — already prints the *current* rank, including above ten:
    the one object in the system built for an unbounded ladder;
  - the **wearable** — one signature per rank cannot go on forever; under B the wearable is a single
    evolving piece (the crown that grows, canvas direction E) or nothing.
  The mark must also reach the **living pig** on Home, Barn and visits, where friends actually meet
  her. Today the ring is portraits-only.
- **Gear:** the six wearables either become the crown's six stages (re-art) or go to the Mystery Hat Box
  pool. Barn decor gifts leave the ladder. Keepsakes stay, one inscribed frame from W1 that always shows
  the current rank, milestone frames at 3 / 6 / 10 as now.
- **Lives:** on the pig, on portraits, in the Barn — all visitor-facing. The Me row is the number.
- **The sheet:** the rank's public face. "How you look at W3" / "how you'll look at W4": portrait with
  ring, the pig with her mark, the keepsake inscription; then the clock perks; then how to wallow.
- **Past W6:** keeps working by rule — ring badge counts, aura stage advances on a cadence
  (every rank to 6, then every 3), the inscription updates. No cliff.
- **Cost:** the living-pig aura (a `cosmeticFx` recipe; the animated-cosmetic pipeline exists), a
  stage rule past 5, crown-stage art if the crown is chosen.
- **Pillar:** Connect and Collect. It is what the players who wallow are already doing.
- **Risk:** the living-pig mark has to be tasteful in motion; rank inflation is visible on friends'
  faces, which is the point.

## Premise C — the set

**Six laps complete a Wallow regalia. Finite by design; W6 is "done".**

- **A rank grants:** one regalia piece (hat · glasses · neck · bow · held · aura; W3 re-slotted to a
  neck wreath, W6's crown trades the rookie cap), its ring, and an inscribed keepsake (fill W2 / W4 / W5).
  The clock caps at W6 too, so the whole system finishes together.
- **Gear:** the six wearables *are* the ladder. Barn decor gifts leave it.
- **Lives:** the rank sheet's ladder, one home; earned rungs say *wearing / wear it*; the Closet's
  prestige filter and the Me "Prestige gear" row are cut.
- **The sheet:** canvas direction D. The set, top to bottom, with the pig at W6 wearing all of it.
- **Past W6:** "Sovereign" — the set is complete; further laps count as laps beside the title
  (`Sovereign · 5 laps on`), not as new ranks with nothing behind them. The Prestige Path keeps paying.
- **Cost:** re-slot two catalog rows, three keepsake frames. Cheap.
- **Pillar:** Collect. A set that completes on the pig.
- **Risk:** tells the three pigs past W6 that the game ended at six. The leaderboard's W11 becomes a
  title plus a small number.

## Side by side

| | A · clock | B · public rank | C · set |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| What a rank is | a receipt | standing | a piece |
| Who sees it | you | everyone | you, and visitors to your closet |
| Ends? | at W6, honestly | never | at W6, by design |
| Fits W7 · W9 · W11 today | nothing to show | yes | retitled |
| New art | none | living-pig mark, stage rule | 3 keepsake frames |
| Pillar | — | Connect · Collect | Collect |

**A** is the cheapest and the emptiest. **C** is the most finished object and the only one with an end.
**B** is what the players who wallow are already doing, and it is the only premise the keepsake
inscription was built for.

## Recommendation: B, with C as its first chapter

The rank is lifetime standing (B). For the first six laps the standing *shows* as the regalia (C): one
piece per rank, worn, the set complete at W6 where the clock also caps. Past six the mark keeps growing
by rule — ring badge, aura cadence, the inscription — so W11 is a real place and not a footnote. A's one
honest line survives: the sheet says plainly when the clock has capped.

What this cuts: the Closet prestige filter, the Me "Prestige gear" row, Barn decor gifts as rank grants.
What it adds: the mark on the living pig, a stage rule past 5, three keepsake frames.

## Open forks (each changes the build)

1. Must the mark reach the living pig (Home, Barn, visits), or are portraits plus the Barn keepsake enough?
2. Past W6, what grows: ring badge only, aura stage on a cadence, the crown, or all three?
3. Do the six wearables stay the first-chapter set, or move to the Mystery Hat Box pool?
4. Do Barn decor gifts stay on the ladder at all?
5. Does the clock cap stay at W6 / 70 %?
