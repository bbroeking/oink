★ the notch reward
Reach your quarter, draw one Barn design a week
Ratified 2026-09-16, superseding the pot rule drawn earlier that day. The quarter rule: a giver earns from a Trough only once their contributions to it reach 25% of the price — the most anyone may give. One random reward per week, no stacking: the first quarter you reach in a week draws one reward — a furnishing from the current featured collection at 60 / 28 / 12, or a tickle purse when you own the collection — and nothing more that week, however many Troughs or snouts. Never quoted before it lands. The 1-per-100 credit retires.
Separate from the Barn Draw. Every Monday each crew (up to 8, roster only, never the friends list) draws one for-sale furnishing among its diggers, equal odds, seed committed before the week ends. Giving to a Trough never enters or weights it — Troughs reach friends and the draw is crew-only. Collection gifts, keepsakes, pass rewards and bunting are never drawn.
