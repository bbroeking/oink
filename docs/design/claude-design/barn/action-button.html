One button, bottom-right, above the tab bar. A 72pt sun sticker-circle with a small "+" on its shoulder. Its face is the default action: the shovel whenever the patch is open (sage fill, a slow dashed ring so "you can dig" reads without a number), the barn door otherwise. A hand-written tag under it names the default. The two margin pills from the previous mock are gone — Rosie's stage is clear from edge to edge.
Tap → fan. The face flips to ×, a light scrim dims the yard, and the options fan upward with the settle spring, each a paper circle with a Fredoka label to its left: Dig (only while open), Go in, Bury a truffle. (A ready bounty is the Season tab's to announce, not this button's.) The default sits nearest the thumb, drawn bigger with a sun label; its sub-line carries the live detail ("the patch is open · 2h 10m"). Tap the scrim or × to close.
Open question — what a single tap does. As drawn, a tap always fans (safe, one gesture). The alternative is tap = the default action, long-press or the "+" = fan — one tap to dig when digging is the point, at the cost of a hidden gesture. See question below.
