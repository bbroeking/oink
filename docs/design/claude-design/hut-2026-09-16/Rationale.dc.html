★ the trading hut · flow notes
The hut is scenery; the sheep is a state.
A crooked hut stands up the field on every background from the first launch. Nothing announces it. When a dig lands your third of a find, the lantern lights and the hooded bust looks out of the hatch — that is the whole notification. Tap it and the yard dims into the hut. Three of one find for tickles, three sets a day, a printed slip as the receipt. The shade is tone, never terms: the exact tickles are on the chip before you confirm.
unmet · dark
Shutters closed, no light. Tap: the shutters rattle, a tag — Shut. Someone’s in there. No section yet.
lit
Any stack of three, and sets left today. Lantern on, bust in the hatch. Tap: door-swing into /hut. First time: the onboarding line + the exact deal.
known · dark
Met, but no sets or none left. Dark hut, section still opens: shutters down, Nothing for me today? / That’s my lot till midnight., stacks with “one more”.
The deal
Exactly three of one find, oldest three leave the bag. Common 5 · uncommon 12 · rare 30 applied tickles (score, never the bank). Today’s fancy on the slate pays ×2. Three sets a day; the shutters close on the third. Never singles, never mixed, never a find in someone’s ask.
Placement
One overlay hut at the right edge, up the field (behind Rosie’s ground line, clear of the Barn button and the coin), on all 47 backgrounds — Rosie and the mound already sit on the painterly scenes as cartoon overlays. No hut on the Interior or on a friend’s visit. A bespoke variant only for a scene the founder rejects on the sim.
What the section is not
No swaps, asks, friends or shop. One deal, one rule line. Reached only through the hut — never a tab, never the Barn fan.
