the swap on a visit · flow notes
One tap more than a delivery, and the find goes both ways
The visit keeps its shape — plaque · tallies · toggle · scene · strip, and Head home only once the pigs are tickled out. A swap changes two things: the bubble gains a second line (will swap / gift only), and tapping the lifted find opens the offer tray instead of firing the server. Everything else the delivery already did — the ceremony, the receipt, the nap card — keeps its place.
The tray
Rises from the strip in the visit's dialog style. Up to three finds the host's bag can spare, chosen by the server from the host's biggest stacks (frozen per wish, so the tray is the same on every refresh and a bag is never on show). A count under an option means the host has several. …or just give it is the gift. Tap not drag — the stage is a box-none layer.
Gift only
Options empty — the host's bag is empty or holds only the wished find. The tray says so once and offers the one button. The bubble already warned (gift only), so nobody arrives expecting a swap.
The receipt
The host's gain is the title; both finds on the card; the tickles second; the giver never "earns". A gift adds and a generous tick. Past the day's paid cap (ten swaps across all your friends) the line reads (no tickles — you've swapped plenty today) and the swap still happens; a pair can only swap once a day anyway.
After, and the next day
The strip goes quiet and the taken find sits in it with a small mark (from a friend). The bubble shows the pig's next wish under next time. Come back the same day and the bubble says you two swapped today, nothing lifts, the strip says come back tomorrow — the server's gate, drawn honestly.
The nap card
Leave a find becomes Swap. Only offered when the wish is open, the pair hasn't swapped today, and the bag holds a match — otherwise it would be a second Head home. The card folds, the host stays asleep, the tray works as on any visit.
What the server answers
wish_changed and option_gone redraw the bubble and tray in place with a one-line toast (Maple's pig changed its mind) — nothing moves. host_bag_full keeps the tray and points at the swap. already_today quiets the strip. A retry reuses the same nonce, so a double tap can never swap twice.
