★ prestige direction study

the Wallow · five ways to wear the mud

At the top of the season pass — tier 30 — a pig may Wallow: reset the climb back to tier 1 and run the whole season again, keeping every hat, title, friend, and tickle already earned. Each Wallow leaves a permanent, visible mark of standing — the only open question is where that mark hangs.

ink paper cream (you-row) sun rose sage sky accent the mud ramp   ×1→×5 fonts here approximate the game's real set — Caprasimo (whimsy titles / numbers), Patrick Hand (kickers), Fredoka + Nunito (display / body). Pig faces are CSS/SVG shapes. No emoji anywhere — marks are drawn or ★/●/✦.
