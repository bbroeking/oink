A cozy social game for iPhone

What is Tickle the Pig?

Tickle the Pig is a cozy multiplayer game for small groups of friends. You raise a pig named Rosie, collect playful cosmetics, form a crew called a Sounder, and return for short shared moments instead of an endless daily grind.

Raise and customize Rosie

Tickle Rosie to earn snouts, then build a look from hats, halos, capes, auras, and odd little treasures. Her mood responds to how consistently you care for her.

Play with people you know

Visit a friend's pig, trade tickles, and send a daily blessing or curse. The social loop is built around a few real friends, not a feed full of strangers.

Join a Sounder

Your Sounder is your crew. Seasonal cooperative adventures give everyone a reason to return, contribute, and see what the group accomplished together.

Earn status through play

Tickle the Pig is not pay-to-win. Accomplishments and status are earned in the game; optional purchases are for personal expression, never competitive advantage.

Questions, answered

Can I play by myself?

Yes. You can raise and customize Rosie on your own. Adding friends unlocks visits, trades, blessings, and cooperative Sounder play.

Is it a virtual pet game?

Partly—but the point is the friendship around the pet. Rosie's care, your collection, and each season give your group small reasons to think about one another.

Does it have ads?

No. Tickle the Pig has no advertising. It is designed for warm, intentional check-ins rather than an attention-maximizing feed.

Where can I play?

Tickle the Pig is free to download for iPhone from the Apple App Store. It requires iOS 15.1 or later and uses Sign in with Apple.
